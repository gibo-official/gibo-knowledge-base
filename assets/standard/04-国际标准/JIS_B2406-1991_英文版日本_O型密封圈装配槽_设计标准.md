## 1. Scope

This Japanese Industrial Standard specifies the shapes and dimensions of groove parts to be installed on those of 25.0 MPa (255 kgf/cm.²) or under in working pressure, among the O-rings specified in JIS B 2401. This Standard doesnot apply to the groove parts for vacuum flanges and low friction uses.

Remarks 1. The cited standards to this Standard are as given in the following:

JIS B 0401 System of Limits and Fits

JIS B 2401 O-rings

JIS K 6301 Physical Testing Methods for Vulcanized Rubber

2. In this standard, units and numerical values given in { }·are in accordance with the traditional units, and are appended for informative reference.

3. Informative Reference 1 (Matters to be considered concerning installation of O-rings) and Informative Reference 2 (O-ring oil and air pressure - design criteria for O-rings housings - basic calculations) are appended to the text of this Standard.
