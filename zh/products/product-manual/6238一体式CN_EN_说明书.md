## 安装

1 安装感应面板组件1)松开蝶形螺母。

2)把感应面板安装到陶瓷窗口，调正面框位置后，装上固定板拧紧蝶型螺母和锁紧螺栓。

![一体式安装](../../../assets/images/ins/6238%E4%B8%80%E4%BD%93%E5%BC%8F%E4%B8%AD%E8%8B%B1%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E%E4%B9%A620190117-ins-05.jpg)

## 2 安装冲水阀

1)先将水量调节阀拧紧至电磁阀进水端。

2）然后将7字弯软管7字弯头端拧紧至面板进水口，另一端拧紧至电磁阀出水端。

3）将双头软管一端拧紧于墙出水口，另一端拧紧至水量调节阀外芽进水端。

4）最后将电磁阀与电源防水插接头分别按颜色对插至面板上的防水插接头上即可。

![一体式2 安装冲水阀](../../../assets/images/ins/6238%E4%B8%80%E4%BD%93%E5%BC%8F%E4%B8%AD%E8%8B%B1%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E%E4%B9%A620190117-ins-06.jpg)

## 3 DC Battery Case/AC Electric Source Installation

\* DC Battery Case: Please connect sensor wire female connector (red color) with DC battery case male connector and connect sensor wire male connector with solenoid valve wire female connector (black color).

\* AC electric source: Please connect sensor wire female connector (red color) with AC electric source male connector and connect sensor wire male connector with solenoid valve wire female connector (black color) respectively. Then connect the AC electric source with 220V power wire.

NOTE: There must be no gap after wires connection to make sure it 's waterproof.

Please mind the positive charge("+") and negative charge("-").

Please make sure to cut off power source and have earth connection when you install AC electric source.

![一体式 3 DC Battery Case/AC Electric Source Installation](../../../assets/images/ins/6238%E4%B8%80%E4%BD%93%E5%BC%8F%E4%B8%AD%E8%8B%B1%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E%E4%B9%A620190117-ins-07.jpg)
