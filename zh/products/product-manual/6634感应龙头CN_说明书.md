## USE EXPOSITION

The soap dispenser let out soap when the hand reaches sensing area. 当手伸到感应范围时, 龙头自动出皂液.

The soap dispenser stops the soap flow when the hand leaves the sensing area. 当手离开感应范围时, 龙头自动停止出皂液.
