## 喷香机安装
本产品建议安装的高度: 离地面约2.1米- 2.7米高.

1, 将喷香机放置安装的墙面上, 通过安装孔画个标记

2, 在标记处钻孔, 并锁上膨胀螺丝, 请确保螺丝高出墙面4mm左右, 方便拆卸喷香机, 更换清香剂.

3, 装上清香剂后将喷香机挂在螺丝上, 确保喷香机有牢固的挂在墙上.

## Mounting the Dispenser

For bestoperation , mount the unit 7-9 feet above the floor .

1. place the dispenser in the location to be mounted , makeamark on the wall surface through the 1 mounting hole .

2. drill the hole at the mark and insert the screw . please stay 4 mm distance to the wall . because you should take down the dispenser when you replace the battery or the air freshener .

3. ensure the dispenser is mounted securely on the wall .
## 喷香机使用
1, 将4节5号碱性电池装入电池夹内.

2, 调节喷香时间至合适的档位, 分别有5, 15, 25分钟喷一次3个档位.

3, 调节模式至合适的工作模式, 分别有全天, 白天和夜晚共3种工作模式.

全天模式: 根据时间档位全天不间断自动喷香.

白天模式: 只在白天时间根据时间档位自动喷香

夜晚模式: 只在暗光时间根据时间档位自动喷香

4, 在放入喷香机之前, 将卡扣按钮拨至最上方位.

5, 将装有喷香剂的喷头朝前放置在喷香机内.

6, 将开关按钮拨至打开( NO)状态, LED指示灯长亮2秒.

7, 将喷香机喷口对着空旷处, 按下测试按钮进行喷香测试

8, 产品正常工作时, LED指示灯每隔2秒会亮1秒钟.

9, 当电池电量低时, LED指示灯将不会亮; 请及时更换4节新的5号碱性电池.

10, 本产品出厂设置为全天模式, 每隔25分钟喷一次.

## Operating the Dispenser

1. insert " aa " batteries in the battery compartment .

2. select the spray time switch to the prefer rect setting :5,15 or 25 minutes .

3. select the operation mode selector to preferred setting :24 hours operations , night , day mode

When 24 is selected - units operates at selected spray inter va or 24 hours .

When day is selected - unit operates at selected spray interval in lighted conditions only .

When night is selected - unit operates at selected spray inter va when ray lower and change to the dark conditions only .

4. before inserting the can , push the hammer to the " up " position .

5. insert aerosol refill can in between the metal towards the frontof the dispenser .

6. using the on / off switch , turn the uniton , the green LED will light up for 2 seconds .

7. test spray by pressing test switch . always ensure that the dispenser is turned away from openness when testing the spray .8. when dispenser at working , green LED blinking one time interval of two seconds .

9. when battery is low , green LED will does not blinking .

Factory setting is 25 minutes pray time with 24 hours operations .
## 注意事项
1, 本喷香机无防水功能, 请将产品安装在干燥的墙上, 远离潮湿的环境.

2, 请将本产品放置在远离食品的位置.

3, 避免将喷香剂喷在塑料, 清漆或者油漆表面.

4, 请及时更换电量低的电池, 勿将废旧电池留在产品内.

5, 如长时间不使用本产品, 请取出电池和喷香剂的罐子.

## Precautions

1. this dispenser not has waterproof function , please place dispenser away from dampness .

2. place dispenser away from food preparation areas .

3. avoid spraying onto plastic , varnished or painted surfaces .

4. always replace with new alkaline batteries and never leave dead batteries in the unit .

5. if you not use dispenser long time , please take out batteries and refill can .
