## 安装注意事项 Installation Notes

1. 请遵守当地所有给排水及建筑规范.

2. 安装时请关闭供水.

3. 确保感应器安装尺寸, 挂式座便器进水口与排污口安装尺寸的正确, 相关尺寸请参阅图1.

4. 安装陶瓷产品, 因其易损和崩裂, 故不可将螺栓或螺母上得过紧, 且须小心轻放.

1. observe all local plumbing and building codes .

2. shut off the water supply .

3. ensure that the induction flushing valve installation size , hanging type toilet water inlet and outlet installation size is correct .

4. installing ceramic products , because of their vulnerability and cracked , so you can ' t boltor nuts crew too tightly , and must be handled with care .
