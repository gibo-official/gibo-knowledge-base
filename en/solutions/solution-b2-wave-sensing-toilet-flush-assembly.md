## 1. Scenario Requirements and Pain Point Analysis

### 1.1 Flush Management Challenges in Public Restrooms

Public restroom flushing has long faced the following management difficulties:

- **Manual Contact Transmission Risk**: Manual flush buttons are among the surfaces with the highest bacterial density in public restrooms. Studies show colony counts on flush buttons can be over 10× that of toilet seats
- **Missed Flushes and Odors**: Users forgetting to flush leads to odor accumulation, severely impacting restroom user experience and management difficulty
- **Water Waste**: Traditional timed flush valves cannot precisely control water usage, with single flushes reaching 6–12L
- **Aging-friendly Needs**: Elderly users find it inconvenient to bend down and press flush buttons; traditional mechanical buttons require significant force and are difficult to operate

### 1.2 The Value of Wave Sensing Flushing

GIBO wave sensing flush assemblies address the above pain points with the following core values:

- **Zero-contact Flushing**: Hand proximity to the sensing zone triggers flushing, no contact with device surfaces required
- **Intelligent Delay Control**: Automatic flushing upon departure, eliminating missed-flush issues
- **Precise Water Volume Control**: Solenoid valve precisely controls flush volume, saving 30%–50% water versus traditional products
- **Human-centric Interaction**: No bending, no force required — sensing activated by hand proximity, easy for elderly and children alike

## 2. Core Technology

### 2.1 Liteon Dual-Mode Sensing Technology

GIBO's proprietary Liteon dual-mode sensing technology intelligently integrates two sensing modes within a single sensing window:

| Sensing Mode | Working Principle | Applicable Scenario |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **Upper Sensing (Proximity)** | Detects hand approaching from above | Wave-triggered flushing, primary mode |
| **Lower Sensing (Ambient Compensation)** | Detects ambient background compensation, filters interference | Prevents false triggering, enhances stability |

Key advantages of Liteon dual-mode technology:
- **0.7s Rapid Response**: Flush triggered instantly when hand approaches the sensing zone
- **Anti-environmental Interference**: Intelligent background compensation prevents false triggering from moisture and temperature changes
- **Low-power Scanning**: Intermittent sensing scan, standby power ≤0.2mW
- **Single Window Integration**: Patented technology, dual functions integrated into a single sensing window

### 2.2 Three Structural Compatibility Designs

The flush assembly is compatible with three mainstream installation structures to meet different application requirements:

| Structure Type | Applicable Scenario | Characteristics |
|----------|---------|------|
| **Top-press** | Traditional toilets/squat pans | Direct replacement of original mechanical button, lowest upgrade cost |
| **Remote Wired** | Concealed cisterns/wall-hung toilets | Separate sensor panel and valve body installation, adapts to complex structures |
| **Pneumatic** | Scenarios without water pressure constraints | Pure mechanical pneumatic triggering, no power required, suitable for unpowered scenarios |

### 2.3 Ultra-low-power Standby

Using GIBO's proprietary ultra-low-power control module, standby power consumption is reduced to the extreme:

- **Static Power ≤90μA**: Deeply optimized for battery-powered scenarios
- **Long Battery Life**: DC 6V battery-powered for 12–24 months (depending on usage frequency)
- **Intelligent Wake-up**: Intermittent sensing scan with adjustable sensitivity, 20ms-level rapid wake-up

## 5. Application Scenarios

### 5.1 Commercial Restrooms (High-frequency Scenarios)

Applicable venues: Shopping malls, office buildings, airports, railway stations, hospitals, etc.

- **High-frequency Reliability**: Solenoid valve tested to 500,000 cycles, suitable for high-frequency environments exceeding 100 uses per day
- **IPX7 Waterproof Rating**: Adaptable to high-humidity environments
- **Anti-false-trigger Algorithm**: Dynamic threshold adjustment prevents false flushing from cleaning staff mopping and other actions

### 5.2 Aging-friendly Retrofitting

Applicable venues: Nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, residential aging-friendly bathrooms

- **Wave Sensing**: Elderly users do not need to bend to press buttons — a light hand wave triggers flushing
- **Large Sensing Zone**: Wide sensing angle, low movement precision required
- **Optional Pneumatic Mode**: Pure mechanical triggering, no battery replacement needed, zero learning curve

### 5.3 Home Bathroom Upgrades

Applicable venues: Second-hand home renovation, smart home upgrades

- **No-disassembly Installation**: Replace the original mechanical button to upgrade to sensor flushing
- **Battery Powered**: No wiring required, zero renovation cost
- **Quiet Design**: Solenoid valve actuation noise ≤40dB
