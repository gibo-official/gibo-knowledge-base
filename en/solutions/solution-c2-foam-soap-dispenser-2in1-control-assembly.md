## 1. Solution Overview

In public washbasin and household bathroom scenarios, the combined use of hand soap and clean water is an essential need. The traditional approach requires installing a separate soap dispenser alongside the faucet, which not only occupies counter space but also presents practical issues such as mismatched aesthetic appearance, conflicting mounting hole positions, and chaotic consumable management. GIBO's C2 Sensor Foam Soap Dispenser 2-in-1 Control Assembly Solution integrates the **sensor control board + foam generation pump + solenoid valve actuation assembly + integrated liquid bottle structure** into a single control system, fusing clean water and foam dispensing into one system. Users simply extend their hand to trigger clean water rinsing or foam hand soap — a completely touch-free operation.

The core highlight of the C2 Solution lies in its compact system architecture. The control board employs GIBO's proprietary Liteon Agile Sensing Technology (Core Technology #7), differentiating between "short reach" and "long reach" operation intents through the same sensing window — a short reach (≤2 seconds) triggers clean water dispensing, while a long reach (>2 seconds) switches to foam dispensing. This interaction logic is simple and intuitive, allowing users to naturally master it without any learning. The foam generation module utilizes a micro diaphragm pump paired with an air-liquid mixing chamber, blending air and hand soap online at a 1:10 ratio at the instant of dispensing to produce dense foam. The dispensing volume is adjustable from 0.5–2.5ml per trigger, supplying on demand to eliminate hand soap waste.

The C2 Solution supports two structural forms: **Integrated** (sensing module, control board, foam pump, and liquid bottle all integrated within the faucet body, suitable for new washbasin installations) and **Split-type** (sensor faucet + concealed liquid bottle box, suitable for retrofit projects). The liquid bottle capacity is 500ml (integrated) or 1500ml (split-type large capacity). In commercial scenarios, the 1500ml large-capacity liquid bottle can meet the demands of 500 uses per day, extending the refill cycle to 7–15 days, significantly reducing the maintenance frequency for hotel and shopping mall property management. The overall solution standby power is ≤0.2mW, and under AC/DC dual power supply mode, battery life can exceed 12 months.

## 3. Functional Features

### 3.1 Single Window Dual-mode Intelligent Recognition

The C2 Solution adopts GIBO's proprietary Single Window Dual-mode Gesture Intelligent Recognition Technology (Core Technology #8), differentiating two operation intents through a single sensing window. When a hand approaching is detected with a dwell time ≤2 seconds, the system judges it as "clean water needed" and immediately opens the clean water solenoid valve; when the hand dwell time exceeds 2 seconds, the system judges it as "foam needed" and automatically switches to foam dispensing mode. This interaction logic fully considers the user's natural usage habits — the instinctive action sequence of first rinsing with water to wet hands, then taking hand soap — and the C2 Solution aligns precisely with this operational flow. The sensing window incorporates dual-mode Strong Light Immunity and Anti-interference Algorithm (Core Technology #11), enabling stable gesture recognition even under complex lighting conditions such as direct strong light or mirror front lighting.

### 3.2 High-precision Foam Air-Liquid Mixing System

A built-in micro diaphragm pump paired with a patented air-liquid mixing chamber (Utility Model Patent ZL201922156545.2) blends air and hand soap online at a precise ratio at the instant of dispensing. The air-liquid mixing ratio can be adjusted via control board parameters (range 1:8–1:12) to accommodate hand soap products of different viscosities. Foam bubble diameter is controlled within the 0.5–1.5mm range, offering a fine texture, strong cleaning power, and easy rinsability. Compared to traditional manual-press soap dispensers, the C2 Solution's on-demand foam supply mode can save 40%–60% of hand soap usage. In concrete terms — in a commercial scenario with 300 daily uses, one C2 Solution unit can save approximately 15–20 liters of hand soap annually compared to a traditional manual-press dispenser.

### 3.3 Anti-drip & Pipeline Self-cleaning

After foam dispensing stops, the system automatically executes a 0.5-second reverse suction action, drawing residual hand soap in the pipeline back into the liquid bottle, thoroughly eliminating the hygiene issue of soap dripping onto the counter surface. This feature is particularly important for high-end hotels and shopping malls — soap dripping onto the counter or basin edge not only affects aesthetics but also breeds bacteria and increases janitorial workload. Simultaneously, the clean water outlet channel performs a 0.3-second micro-flush after each water shut-off, rinsing residual soap foam from the spout surface and maintaining a clean faucet appearance.

### 3.4 Multi-power Supply Compatibility

The C2 Solution control board supports automatic recognition and switching between three power supply modes:
- **AC 110-240V Mains Direct Supply**: Suitable for new construction projects; one-time wiring for permanent use
- **DC 6V Battery**: 4×AA batteries; approximately 12 months of household battery life
- **Lithium Battery Rechargeable**: 3.7V/1200mA lithium battery, USB Type-C charging; 1.5 hours of charging provides approximately 6–9 months of use

The control board features a built-in AC/DC intelligent switching circuit that automatically disconnects the battery circuit when connected to mains to extend battery life; during a mains power outage, it seamlessly switches to battery power to ensure uninterrupted device operation.

### 3.5 Low-liquid Alert & Refill Reminder

Liquid level detection electrodes are installed in the liquid bottle. When the remaining hand soap is below 50ml, the control board drives the LED indicator on the faucet panel to flash at 1Hz frequency, reminding the user to replenish hand soap promptly. For commercial locations, this feature avoids the problem of empty soap bottles affecting the customer experience. The liquid bottle uses transparent PC material, allowing direct visual level inspection from the outside. The bottle cap features a leak-proof seal design that prevents leakage during inverted installation, and liquid bottle replacement is simple and quick.

### 3.6 Antibacterial Materials & Hygiene Standards

All components in contact with hand soap in the C2 Solution — including the liquid bottle, foam pipeline, and seals — use food-grade PP and silicone materials with added silver-ion antibacterial masterbatch, achieving an antibacterial rate ≥99% (tested per GB/T 31402-2015). The sensing window material undergoes anti-fingerprint coating treatment to reduce stain adhesion. The overall unit design complies with food contact material safety standards, ensuring that hand soap quality is not compromised by secondary contamination.

### 3.7 Fully Automatic Standby Sleep & Wake-up

When the sensing window detects no human signal for more than 60 seconds, the control board automatically enters deep sleep mode, reducing standby power to the minimum. When a hand approaches again, the system wakes up and enters working mode within 0.3 seconds. This mechanism can significantly extend battery life during low-traffic commercial periods (e.g., nighttime), while also slowing the aging rate of electronic components.

The 10–15cm sensing distance design accommodates users of different heights without the need to bend down or lean in.

**Recommended Models**: GBL-6178 (Triple Sensor Water-Soap Integrated) / GBL-6636AD (Full Copper Wall-mounted Compact)

### 4.3 Household Turnkey Bathrooms

Household scenarios place greater emphasis on the balance between aesthetics and practicality. The C2 Solution's compact integrated version (faucet body + built-in liquid bottle) can be installed on a single-hole countertop without additional drilling, making it especially suitable for small bathrooms with limited counter space. The LED temperature digital display function allows family members to intuitively see the current water temperature, with an automatic hot water warning indicator appearing for temperatures ≥45℃ to protect children's safety. The ABS chrome-plated version offers high cost-effectiveness, while the full copper version provides superior texture — users can freely choose based on budget.

**Recommended Models**: GBL-6178 (Water-Soap Integrated with Digital Display) / G33608 (Sensor Foam Soap Dispenser Matching)

### 4.4 Hospitals & Medical Care Facilities

Hospitals impose stringent requirements on disinfection and hygiene standards. The C2 Solution's touch-free operation characteristics completely eliminate the possibility of pathogen transmission through faucet handles or soap dispenser buttons during hand washing, meeting hospital infection control standards. The ≤0.3-second sensor response time ensures that medical staff do not need to wait when performing rapid hand disinfection. Application of antibacterial materials and the anti-drip design further safeguard washbasin surface cleanliness. An optional IoT module (Core Technology #18) can be configured to interface with hospital intelligent hand hygiene monitoring systems, recording the frequency and duration of medical staff hand washing.

### 4.5 Age-friendly Renovation & Accessible Bathrooms

For the elderly or individuals with limited hand function, the C2 Solution's fully touch-free operation eliminates difficulties in rotating or pressing faucet handles. The foam dispensing function automatically lathers hand soap without requiring both hands to rub up a lather, simplifying the hand-washing process. LED light indicators assist elderly users with poor hearing to confirm device and foam status visually. Both sensing distance and foam dispensing volume are adjustable via control board parameters to accommodate different usage habits.
