# 2022 Dual-mode Smart Control Faucet Key Technology R&D and Industrialization Demonstration

> Project undertaking unit: **Fujian GIBO Kitchen & Bath Technology Co., Ltd.**; Project leader: Zheng Shaobo; Implementation period: 2022-03-01 to 2024-06-30; Implementation management agency: Fuzhou Municipal Science & Technology Bureau.

## I. R&D Content and Main Innovation Points

### 1.1 Project R&D Content

#### (1) Dual-mode Smart Control System Design

1. **Voice-interactive control system design**: Develop a voice-control module built into the faucet with voice-interactive control; through wake-up commands it recognizes preset voice phrases (e.g., "turn on water", "turn off water", "increase water temperature", "decrease water temperature", "increase flow", "reduce flow"), outputs via serial/IO and plays through a speaker, realizing human–machine dialogue command control of water on/off, water temperature, and flow adjustment.
2. **Gesture-recognition control system design**: Based on the infrared reflection principle, develop a gesture-recognition control module that recognizes multi-directional spatial movement such as forward, backward, left, right, up, down, and hover; adopt an IR emission array unit and a photosensitive receiving unit to capture the action signal of a hand sweeping through the detection area; after recognition processing, the MCU microprocessor controls the execution unit, realizing gesture-command control of water on/off, water temperature, and flow adjustment.

#### (2) Self-adaptive Electromagnetic Water-valve System Design

Develop a new-type solenoid valve assembly that achieves self-adaptive pressure reduction when switching the solenoid valve, lowering the water-hammer effect; adopt an LCD display to show switch status, water temperature, and flow values via serial interface.

#### (3) New-product Development of Dual-mode Smart Faucet

The product includes solenoid valve, sensing device, control device, and display; the sensing device connects to at least two sensors, receiving signals from the gesture-recognition and voice-interaction systems respectively; adopts MCU control technology, with simple and reliable circuit system, allowing sensing sensitivity, flow time, and other parameters to be modified by programming at any time.

### 1.2 Key Technologies

1. **Voice-recognition processing technology**: For the specific scenario of faucet control with small-vocabulary needs, a dedicated-chip voice-recognition system must be trained and built; the hardware environment is constrained (small size, high reliability, low power consumption, low cost), unable to process natural-language speech (e.g., "give me hot water"). A voice-recognition processing technology satisfying this product's application must be designed—accurate and conforming to scenario characteristics.
2. **Gesture-recognition technology**: Break through the single instant-on-on-sensing mode, develop gesture control with multiple outlet modes, solve sensing recognition of different positions and directions of gestures, and realize multi-mode faucet control according to different directional entry positions.
3. **Self-adaptive Pressure-reducing Water-hammer-proof Disc-shaped Diaphragm Structure Design Technology**: Addressing problems of household solenoid valves such as low operating pressure, poor stability, and excessive outlet impact damaging pipelines, design a self-adaptive pressure-reducing water-hammer-proof disc-shaped diaphragm structure to improve solenoid-valve performance and faucet service life.

### 1.3 Features and Innovations of the Project

**Features**: Develop a dual-mode intelligent control faucet product with voice-recognition and gesture-recognition control, equipped with a self-adaptive pressure-reducing water-hammer-proof solenoid valve, offering advanced intelligent control and higher performance.

**Innovation points**:
1. Adopt a water-saving control system with linked gesture-recognition and voice-interaction systems, realizing voice- and gesture-recognition controlled quantitative water outlet, water-saving mode, on/off water, and humanized application experience, with industry-leading advancement for sanitary-ware product control methods.
2. Adopt a self-adaptive pressure-reducing water-hammer-proof disc-shaped diaphragm structure: design both the disc-shaped diaphragm valve-closing end-face structure and the outlet pipeline into a funnel-trapezoid shape; when closing the water outlet, the water flow gradually overlaps and closes during the diaphragm's descent, and the outlet gap becomes smaller and smaller; at the instant of closing, because the outlet flow is very small, the pressure decreases accordingly, the water-hammer effect is reduced, and a low water-hammer-pressure function is achieved.
