## 1. Product Background and Market Value

### 1.1 The Enormous Market for Existing Bathrooms

China has a vast number of existing public restrooms. Traditional sensor faucet installation requires countertop removal and plumbing modification — complex and costly. Industry estimates suggest approximately 90% of existing restrooms nationwide still use traditional mechanical faucets, with an upgrade market potential exceeding one hundred billion yuan.

The GIBO quick-install Mini sensor faucet is a revolutionary product born for this market — no countertop removal, no plumbing modification, no professional plumber needed. Users can complete installation themselves in just 3 seconds.

### 1.2 World-first Core Innovation

The GIBO G61901 MINI sensor faucet was granted a national invention patent and PCT International Patent in 2015, hailed by the industry as "the pioneer of the sensor faucet category":

- **First Quick-install Structure**: Broke through the installation bottleneck of traditional sensor faucets requiring countertop disassembly
- **First Miniature Sensor System**: Miniaturized the infrared sensor module for integration into the faucet body itself
- **First Ultra-compact Solenoid Valve**: Solenoid valve volume is only 1/3 of conventional products, embedded inside the faucet body
- **Global Cumulative Sales Exceeding 500,000 Units**: Market-validated mature product

## 2. Core Technology

### 2.1 3-Second Quick-Install Structure (PCT International Patent)

The core of the quick-install structure is a precision-engineered snap-fit mechanical mechanism:

| Installation Step | Action | Time Required |
|----------|------|:--------:|
| 1. Unscrew original faucet aerator | Manual rotation | ≤2 seconds |
| 2. Attach quick-install sensor faucet | Align snap, push upward | ≤1 second |
| 3. Done | — | **Total ≤3 seconds** |

**Structural Features**:

- **Standard Interface**: Compatible with Φ35mm standard faucet interface, covering over 95% of domestic basins
- **Tool-free Installation**: Fully manual operation, no wrenches/screwdrivers or other tools required
- **Anti-theft Design**: Requires special tool for removal after installation, preventing theft in public venues
- **Anti-corrosion and Anti-rust**: Structural body treated for corrosion resistance, suitable for high-humidity bathroom environments

### 2.2 Miniature Low-power Sensor System

Integrating a complete sensor control system within the limited space of the faucet body is the concentrated embodiment of GIBO's miniaturization technology:

- **Miniature Infrared Sensor Module**: Sensor module volume is just the size of a fingernail, embedded in the faucet front end
- **Ultra-low-power MCU**: Standby power ≤90μA, DC 6V battery-powered for 12–18 months
- **Adaptive Sensing Distance**: 3–8cm auto-matching, adaptable to different basin depths
- **Intelligent Anti-false-trigger**: Dynamic threshold algorithm eliminates interference from water flow and cleaning actions

### 2.3 Miniature Solenoid Valve Technology

### 2.4 Adaptive Water Pressure Technology

**GIBO Solution**: 3-second installation of one quick-install sensor faucet per guest bathroom, no countertop removal needed, no water shutoff required, no professional construction. Two people can complete retrofitting of 50 rooms in one day.

**Customer Value**:
