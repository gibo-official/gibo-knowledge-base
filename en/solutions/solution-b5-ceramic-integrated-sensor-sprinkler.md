## 1. Core Technical Advantages

### 1.1 Ceramic One-piece Forming Process

GIBO ceramic integrated sprinklers are manufactured from high-purity aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃) ceramic material via isostatic pressing and high-temperature sintering. Compared to traditional plastic sprinklers, they offer significant advantages:

| Comparison Dimension | Ceramic Integrated | Traditional Plastic Sprinklers |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Material Strength | High-density Al₂O₃, Mohs hardness 9 | ABS/PP engineering plastics |
| Weather Resistance | Zero UV degradation, 10+ year outdoor lifespan | 3–5 years, aging and embrittlement |
| Corrosion Resistance | Acid/alkali tolerant, suitable for fertilizer/saline water | Long-term fertilizer contact prone to corrosion |
| Aesthetic Quality | High-gloss glaze, stays like new over time | Fades and yellows |
| Surface Self-cleaning | Hydrophobic glaze self-cleaning, rain washes clean | Prone to dirt adhesion |

### 1.2 Three-level Environmental Adaptive Control

GIBO ceramic sprinklers support three levels of intelligent control modes for "on-demand" water supply:

| Control Level | Sensor | Control Logic | Response Speed |
|----------|--------|---------|---------|
| **Primary: Moisture Trigger** | Soil moisture sensor | Auto on when moisture below threshold | Real-time |
| **Secondary: Light Compensation** | Photoelectric sensor | Increase watering frequency on hot sunny days | Hourly adjustment |
| **Advanced: Multi-factor Linkage** | Moisture + Light + Rainfall | Comprehensive environmental factor intelligent decision-making | Dynamic optimization |

### 1.3 Self-cleaning Anti-clogging Water Outlet Design

- **Ring Outlet Structure**: 360° evenly distributed outlet holes, uniform water pressure
- **Micro-hole Self-cleaning**: Water pressure automatically scours micro-hole surfaces during each watering cycle, preventing mineral deposits
- **Removable for Cleaning**: Water outlet end cap quickly removable for cleaning, simple maintenance
- **Insect-proof Design**: Internal spring automatically closes outlet holes when not watering, preventing insect entry

## 3. Solar Self-powering Solution

GIBO ceramic sprinklers can be optionally equipped with a solar self-powering module for zero-cabling outdoor deployment:

- **Solar Panel**: High-efficiency monocrystalline silicon (conversion rate ≥22%), embedded in sprinkler top
- **Storage Battery**: Lithium battery, capacity 2000mAh, autonomy ≥7 days (overcast/rainy days)
- **MPPT Charge Management**: Maximum power point tracking, effective charging even in low-light conditions
- **Low-power Optimization**: Standby power ≤0.2mW, daytime charging / nighttime standby balance

## 4. Application Scenarios

### 4.1 High-end Courtyard Landscapes

**Configuration Plan**:

- 1 ceramic sprinkler per 20–30㎡
- Differentiated irrigation parameters set according to courtyard zones (lawn area, flower bed area, tree pool area)
- Solar self-powered version suitable for areas far from buildings, no wiring required

**User Value**: Ceramic texture highly matches high-end courtyard design language; 10-year replacement-free product lifespan significantly reduces long-term maintenance costs.

### 4.2 Commercial Plazas / Five-star Hotel Exteriors

**Configuration Plan**:

- Placed at 3–5m intervals along landscape flower beds / green belts
- Dual-mode operation: centralized control + local sensing
- Nighttime switching to "landscape mode" with lighting for waterscape effect

**User Value**: Commercial venues emphasize visual quality and brand image; the premium feel and crystal glaze of ceramic sprinklers elevate the overall venue class, with customizable logos integrated into the design.

### 4.3 Municipal Green Belts

**Configuration Plan**:

- Placed at 5–8m intervals along road green belts
- Paired with centralized controller for zoned management
- Dual assurance: soil moisture sensing + timed control

**User Value**: Strong weather resistance, adaptable to complex environments with road dust and vehicle exhaust; self-cleaning function reduces maintenance frequency, lightening the workload of municipal maintenance personnel.
