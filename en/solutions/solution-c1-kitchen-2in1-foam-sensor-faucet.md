## 1. Solution Overview

The kitchen is the most water-intensive and functionally complex space in the home. Traditional kitchen faucets only provide a single water flow function. Dishwashing requires additional detergent bottles or standalone soap dispensers, which are cumbersome to operate and consume counter space. The GIBO C1 Kitchen 2-in-1 Water & Foam Sensor Faucet Solution, built on years of expertise in sensor control and foam generation technology, highly integrates sensor-activated water dispensing and foam dispensing within a single faucet body. Users simply extend their hand to the sensing zone for clean water, or switch sensing zones / trigger again for fine foam — completely eliminating the pain points of cluttered countertops and complicated operations.

The C1 Solution consists of three core subsystems: **High-sensitivity Dual Sensor Module** (recognizing hand approach and switching gestures), **Foam Generation & Delivery Assembly** (built-in air-liquid mixing pump and foam channel), and **Intelligent Control Board** (coordinating water/foam logic and safety protection). The overall solution supports three dispensing modes — direct water flow, aerated soft water, and foam — with natural switching through sensing logic, requiring no physical buttons or knobs. Sensor response time is ≤0.5 seconds, and foam dispensing starts and stops instantly, supplying on demand to eliminate waste. The built-in foam liquid bottle has a 500ml capacity, supporting 2–3 months of continuous use for an average household without maintenance. The entire unit features IP65 waterproof protection, is suitable for the high-humidity and high-temperature kitchen environment, has a design service life of 500,000 cycles, and meets long-term usage requirements in both household and commercial scenarios.

The solution also integrates GIBO's proprietary **Ultra-low-power Multi-stable Agile Sensing Technology** (Core Technology #6), paired with an AC/DC dual power supply module, ensuring a battery life exceeding 12 months in battery-powered mode to reduce the maintenance burden of frequent battery replacement.

## 3. Functional Features

### 3.1 Dual Sensing Zone Intelligent Recognition

The faucet body is equipped with two independent sensing windows — upper and lower groups: the lower sensing zone (10±2cm) corresponds to clean water dispensing, and the side or upper sensing zone (15±2cm) corresponds to foam dispensing. Users naturally switch functions by extending their hand to different sensing zones, with zero learning curve. When a hand is detected remaining for over 3 minutes, the system automatically enters standby protection mode to prevent false triggers and water waste.

### 3.2 Three Dispensing Modes with One-touch Switching Logic

The C1 Solution supports three dispensing modes: direct water flow (powerful rinsing), aerated soft water (gentle washing), and foam (deep cleaning). Users can switch between clean water and foam during use via a "long reach" gesture, or control the dispensing state through sensing zone dwell time. Direct flow is suitable for rinsing large pots and removing mud from vegetable/fruit surfaces, aerated soft water suits daily hand washing and dish pre-rinsing, and foam mode handles deep grease removal. Together, the three modes cover all kitchen cleaning scenarios.

### 3.3 Built-in Foam Generation System

GIBO's proprietary micro air-liquid mixing pump blends air and detergent online at a 1:8–1:12 ratio, producing fine, dense foam. Foam density is controllable, ensuring effective cleaning while preventing waste from overly thin foam. The foam channel features anti-clogging design with a built-in stainless steel filter that intercepts particles ≥0.5mm, preventing sediment or crystallization in the detergent from clogging the pipeline. The system is compatible with most commercially available dishwashing liquids and hand soaps; users do not need proprietary consumables.

### 3.4 Ultra-low-power Multi-stable Control Platform

Leveraging GIBO's core Ultra-low-power Multi-stable Agile Sensing Technology (Core Technology #6), the control board's standby power consumption is ≤0.2mW. In pure battery mode, 4 AA alkaline batteries support approximately 12 months of normal household use. The AC/DC switching version automatically switches to AC power when connected to mains electricity while float-charging the backup battery, ensuring normal operation for 3–5 days during a power outage. The application of Dual-chip Interchangeable Platform (Core Technology #10) ensures that if the main control chip fails, it can be replaced by a compatible chip, significantly reducing after-sales maintenance difficulty.

### 3.5 Anti-overflow Power-off Safety Protection

Equipped with Intelligent Anti-overflow Power-off Safety Protection Technology (Core Technology #13), the control board monitors water flow and foam pipeline status in real time. When abnormal water output is detected (continuous dispensing exceeding the preset threshold) or foam liquid pipeline leakage is detected, the system automatically shuts off the solenoid valve and cuts power, preventing the severe consequence of kitchen flooding. Protection response time is ≤1 second, and water shut-off success rate is ≥99.5%.

### 3.6 Pull-out Spout (Optional)

A customizable pull-out spout design is available, with the spray head connected to the faucet body via a flexible hose that extends up to 60cm, facilitating cleaning of sink corners and large cookware. The pull-out spout supports both direct flow and aerated soft water modes, suitable for double-bowl or large single-bowl kitchen sink scenarios. The core structure is protected by national utility model patents (ZL201820847903.7, ZL201920200663.6), featuring a dual return mechanism of spring auto-retract and magnetic latching.

### 3.7 Infant-grade Material Safety Standards

All components in contact with water use food-grade materials: the stainless steel version uses SUS304 stainless steel (tested per GB/T 3280), the zinc alloy version is SGS lead-free certified, and seals use food-grade silicone rubber. The foam liquid channel uses PP food-grade material to ensure detergent is not contaminated by plastic leachables. The entire unit passes national standard testing per GB 18145-2014 and GB 25501-2010.

### 3.8 160° Wide-angle Swivel Spout

The faucet body supports 160° wide-angle rotation, with adjustable dispensing angle, covering both left and right bowls of a double-bowl sink. The swivel structure uses precision stainless steel bearings with a rotation life exceeding 100,000 cycles without loosening, and undergoes a 200-hour salt spray test before shipment to ensure long-term rust-free operation.

## 4. Applicable Scenarios

### 4.1 High-end Residential Turnkey Kitchens

The C1 Solution is an ideal kitchen fitting for turnkey high-end residences. The built-in foam system eliminates the need for countertop soap dispenser bottles, maintaining a clean and tidy kitchen counter surface. SUS304 stainless steel or full copper chrome-plated materials complement modern minimalist, luxury, industrial, and other kitchen design styles. The dual-sensor design allows homemakers/cooks to clean without touching any buttons — greasy hands only need to reach out to access clean water or foam, delivering a user experience significantly superior to traditional faucet-plus-standalone-dispenser combinations.

**Recommended Models**: GBL-91605 (dTOF Laser Sensing), GBL-6178 (Water-Soap Integrated Triple Sensor)

### 4.2 Commercial Kitchens & Central Canteens

Commercial kitchens demand high efficiency. The C1 Solution's fast sensor response (≤0.5 seconds) and three dispensing modes significantly boost washing efficiency. Direct flow mode handles high-pressure rinsing of stubborn stains, foam mode supports batch tableware pre-wash, and aerated soft water mode covers final rinsing. The 500ml large-capacity foam liquid bottle reduces refill frequency. IP65 protection and 500,000-cycle design life ensure stable operation under commercial high-intensity use. The AC/DC version can be directly connected to a 220V kitchen power supply, completely eliminating battery replacement concerns.

**Recommended Model**: GBL-6174 (Full Copper Chrome-plated) AC/DC version

### 4.3 Age-friendly / Accessible Kitchens

For the elderly and those with limited mobility, the C1 Solution's fully touch-free operation eliminates the need to twist or press faucet handles — all water operations can be completed simply by reaching out. The foam function allows elderly users with reduced hand strength to easily complete dishwashing. The sensing zone sensitivity is adjustable to accommodate different usage habits, and an external foot switch can be added as an auxiliary trigger method to further lower the operational barrier.

**Recommended Model**: GBL-6175 (ABS Lightweight Edition)

### 4.4 High-end Hotel Apartment Fittings

The C1 Solution's configured foam system is particularly suitable for open-plan kitchens in serviced apartments and high-end serviced residences. The integrated design seamlessly merges the soap dispenser function into the faucet. The 500,000-cycle service life matches the high-frequency usage scenario of hotels, and maintenance is simple. Faucet color, surface finish, and logo silk-screening can be customized to match hotel brand interior design styles. The AC/DC version can interface with hotel intelligent control systems to enable remote water usage monitoring and energy consumption statistics.

### 4.5 Chain Restaurant Back-of-house Standardization

Chain restaurant brands have strict standardization requirements for back-of-house equipment. All functional parameters of the C1 Solution can be preset via the control board, ensuring that sensing distance, foam dispensing volume, dispensing modes, and other parameters are completely consistent across all branches. The foam air-liquid ratio is adjustable, facilitating matching with different brands of specialized detergents. The IP65 protection rating and self-cleaning anti-clogging design significantly reduce back-of-house equipment maintenance frequency, lowering chain operation maintenance costs.
