## 1. Technical Principles and Core Advantages

### 1.1 Bistable Pulse Drive Principle

The pulse solenoid valve employs a bistable design that consumes power only during state switching, with zero power consumption when maintaining its state. This characteristic makes it an ideal actuation component for battery-powered sensor products:

- **Pulse Drive**: The solenoid valve opens or closes the valve core via forward/reverse pulse current with a pulse width of only 20–50ms
- **Self-holding Design**: Once the valve core is in position, it is held by permanent magnetic force with no continuous power required
- **Low-power Performance**: Average standby power consumption approaches zero, enabling 12–24 months of battery life when combined with an ultra-low-power control module
- **Rapid Response**: Switching response time ≤20ms, ensuring zero-delay sensor activation

### 1.2 Low Water Hammer Structural Design

Water hammer is a common technical challenge for solenoid valve products. GIBO significantly reduces water hammer impact through structural innovation:

- **Soft-close Buffer Structure**: A buffer chamber at the valve core closing end slows the closing speed to reduce pressure impact
- **Flow Path Optimization**: Internal flow passages feature converging/diverging designs to minimize pressure waves from sudden water flow changes
- **Damping Materials**: Key sealing components use specialty elastomer materials to absorb vibration energy at the moment of closure
- **Test Verification**: Validated through 100,000 consecutive switching cycles, peak water hammer pressure ≤1.2× nominal water pressure

### 1.3 Self-cleaning Anti-clogging Technology

Public restrooms feature complex water quality conditions where particulates can easily cause solenoid valve clogging or seal failure. GIBO pulse solenoid valves incorporate multiple self-cleaning protections:

| Anti-clogging Technology | Implementation | Effect |
|----------|---------|------|
| Inlet Filter | Stainless steel precision filter, filters ≥0.5mm particles | Blocks large particulate impurities |
| Self-flushing Flow Path | High-speed water flow scours the valve core surface during switching | Prevents impurity sedimentation |
| Large Bore Design | Bore diameter ≥4mm, superior anti-clogging performance | Reduces impurity jamming probability |
| Balance Hole Structure | Micron-scale balance holes in the valve core automatically expel fine particles | Extends maintenance intervals |

### 4.1 Full Inspection Process

Every batch of solenoid valve assemblies undergoes 100% full inspection before shipment, including:

- **Air Tightness Test**: 0.8MPa air pressure, held ≥30s with no leakage

- **Pressure Resistance Test**: 1.2× nominal water pressure, sustained ≥60s
