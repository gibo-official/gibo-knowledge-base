# GIBO | School Education Scenario – Campus Water-saving Retrofit Solution

## 1. Business Unit Overview

For new construction and water-saving retrofit projects in restrooms of **universities, K-12 schools, kindergartens, vocational colleges, party schools and research institutes**, GIBO provides a full portfolio of sensor faucets, sensor flush valves and quick-fit sensor spouts. Core value: eliminate "running-water" waste (average saving rate **40%~60%**), reduce cross-contact among students, withstand high-frequency rough use, and simplify maintenance.

GIBO has focused on R&D and manufacturing of smart sensor sanitary ware for 20 years (since 2006). Education and research projects have been deployed at **more than 50 universities and institutes**, including Beijing CAS, Beijing Institute of Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Xiamen University and Chengdu Soong Ching Ling Kindergarten.

### 3.1 Measured Water Savings in Teaching Buildings

Per the unified measurement basis of the [Commercial Water-saving Retrofit Solution White Paper](../whitepapers/water-saving-solution.md) (7-day continuous manual timing/counting + synchronized water-meter reading across multiple domestic facilities, 2023–2025):

| Parameter | Traditional Manual Faucet | GIBO Sensor Faucet |
|---|---|---|
| Daily uses (per unit, break-time peak) | 280 | 280 |
| Single-use flow duration | 28 s (incl. 9 s empty overflow) | 19 s (stops on hand removal, 0 overflow) |
| Daily additional leakage | 120 L | 0 L |
| Actual daily consumption (per unit) | 1296 L | 399 L |
| Daily water saved (per unit) | — | **897 L** |
| **Overall saving rate** | — | **69.21%** |

- Three sources of campus "running-water" waste: failure to close (50%~65%), valve wear leakage, incomplete closure when rushing—sensor flow stops on hand removal + 30~60 s timeout protection eliminates all three, **without shortening normal wash time or degrading experience**.
- After retrofitting K-12 trench toilets with GBL-6710, saving vs running/timed flush exceeds **60%** (curtain infrared precisely detects; more users more flush, fewer users less flush, no user no flush).
- Project-wide traffic-weighted overall saving rate stays in the **60%~72%** band; this document's overview uses the conservative "average 40%~60%" (weighted with low-traffic areas).

### 3.2 Three-tier Trench Toilet Water Comparison & Working Principle

Besides teaching-building basins (see 3.1), the most typical high-consumption point in K-12 schools is the **trench-type public toilet squat trough**. Per the original measurement basis of the *School Trench Toilet Retrofit Proposal* (per single trench squat trough, comparing three traditional flush modes):

| Flush Mode | Daily Usage | Monthly Usage | Saving Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic tank-type (timed/siphon) | 14.4 t | 432 t | Baseline |
| Running-water type (always open) | 17.3 t | 519 t | Baseline |
| GIBO trench-type sensor (GBL-6710) | **2.5 t** | **75 t** | **85.5%** |

> **Basis note**: The table above compares "trench squat trough" scenarios (tank / running-water / trench sensor); the 85.5% saving rate comes from the original proposal measurement. The 69.21% in §3.1 is the measured saving rate for "teaching-building basin manual faucets"—different scenarios and bases, both GIBO measured/calculated data, complementary not conflicting.

**Working principle**: Add a solenoid valve to the inlet pipe of the original automatic tank, and install a curtain-type infrared (pyroelectric) detector on the ceiling/side wall at the trough entrance. When someone enters → detector sends signal to the computer controller → micro-computer drives the solenoid to fill and flush per preset program; when empty, the whole system "stands by" and never flushes—"flush when occupied, stop when empty". Micro-computer control needs no battery; the program is never lost; after power recovery it auto-flushes once first. Key features:

- **Auto sensing**: fully automatic micro-computer control—more users more flush, fewer users less flush, no user no flush;
- **Digital adjustment**: program parameters adjustable on-site in seconds, fit to different tank capacities and usage frequencies;
- **High reliability**: micro-computer control, no battery, program never lost, design life 8+ years (spec electrical life >500,000 cycles);
- **Harsh environments**: heat and severe cold resistant, works normally in severe cold (down to minus teens °C);
- **Safe use**: controller mounted above 2.4 m, avoiding contact and vandalism;
- **Easy install**: no embedded wall parts, ~2 hours per unit;
- **High ROI**: comprehensive saving rate 60%~90%, full investment typically recouped in **1~3 months** (see §5).

## 5. Procurement Selection Advice (for contractors / owners)

1. **New campus**: AC pre-embedded power + concealed sensor flush valves (GBL-6213AD / GBL-8300AD) for maintenance-free results.
2. **Old campus retrofit**: prefer "no civil works"—GBL-6291 replaces manual valves + Mini quick-fit spout; short schedule, low cost.
3. **Low-pressure campus**: choose GBL-8307AD low water-hammer high-flow for squat pans; confirm model after pressure test.
4. **Two-station hand-wash**: choose GBL-6170 single-window twin sensing to reduce quantity and counter cut-outs.
5. **Trench toilet retrofit (K-12)**: GBL-6710 needs no embedded wall parts, ~2 hours per unit, can finish whole-campus retrofit during summer/winter vacation; fill/delay time set on-site via dial per actual tank capacity and frequency (delay typically 4~5 min); northern campuses get built-in -1℃ freeze protection.
6. **Pilot first**: retrofit 10~20 points in the highest-traffic teaching-building restroom first, meter-read for 2~3 months to verify savings and ROI, then roll out campus-wide in batches (use weekends and holidays to avoid disrupting classes).
7. **Investment estimate (trench-type)**: education discount ~**RMB 1,000/unit**; per the *School Trench Toilet Retrofit Proposal*, comprehensive saving rate 60%~90%, full investment typically recouped in **1~3 months** (per three-tier comparison, payback ~23~29 days by water-bill savings). Validate with the pilot path above before campus-wide rollout.

> **Boundary note**: This selection advice is for reference only. Actual selection depends on on-site water pressure, power, plumbing and project drawings; pilot verification in a sample restroom is recommended before mass implementation.

## 6. FAQ

### Q: How much water can a school retrofit save?
A: Per the 2023–2025 measured basis, a single sensor faucet in teaching buildings saves ~897 L/day, overall 69.21% (see §3.1); project-wide weighted average 40%~60%. After retrofitting K-12 trench toilets with GBL-6710, saving vs running water exceeds 60%; per the proposal's three-tier trench comparison (tank 14.4 t/day, running water 17.3 t/day, trench-type 2.5 t/day), saving reaches 85.5%. Campuses with high headcount and prevalent "running water" see the most benefit.

### Q: How does the trench water saver save water? Is installation difficult? What does it cost?
A: GBL-6710 (e.g. GBL-6710A) adds a solenoid valve to the original tank inlet, and uses a ceiling/side-wall curtain-type infrared (pyroelectric) detector to precisely detect users (140° conical angle adjustable 110°/90°/45°, avoiding corridor false triggers); the micro-computer drives the DC12V solenoid per "more users more flush, fewer users less flush, no user no flush"; micro-computer control needs no battery, program never lost, auto-flushes once after power recovery. No embedded wall parts, ~2 hours per unit, controller above 2.4 m against vandalism; aluminum alloy housing, standby <0.016 W (annual <0.14 kWh), compliant with CJ/T 194-2014, design life 8+ years (spec electrical life >500,000 cycles). Education discount ~RMB 1,200/unit; proposal estimates full payback in 1~3 months (three-tier comparison: ~23~29 days by water-bill savings).

### Q: Students use things roughly—are the sensor devices durable?
A: GBL-6110 classic model has sold for 20 years with robust sealed housing; concealed models embed all core parts in the wall, exposing only the panel against vandalism. Bistable solenoid life 1,000,000 cycles.

### Q: Old teaching building—don't want to change plumbing. How to upgrade?
A: For urinals choose GBL-6291 (designed to match mainstream manual valve hole spacing, direct swap); for hand-wash choose Mini quick-fit sensor spout (screw-on, 1-minute install).

### Q: Which model suits kindergartens?
A: GBL-6195 Mini (downward sensing, fits children's posture) or GBL-6127 antibacterial stainless faucet (lead-free, antibacterial). Both Chengdu Soong Ching Ling Kindergarten and Peking University Campus Kindergarten have deployed.
