### Deep Dive 1: GIBO Sensor Sanitary Ware Commissioned on the Liaoning Aircraft Carrier

A national flagship vessel — three reasons for its trust:

1. **Military-grade anti-interference & fail-safe design** — Passed stringent electromagnetic compatibility testing with anti-interference performance up to **military grade 4A**; both electronics and complete units passed vibration-resistance tests, adapting to the vessel's complex electromagnetic environment as well as tilting and rolling, ensuring zero-failure operation on ocean voyages.
2. **Salt/alkali corrosion-resistant design & extreme-environment adaptability** — Crystal-diamond chrome plating process with a thickened **11 μm coating**; the sensor module passed the rigorous **dual-85 test** and operates from -10°C to 85°C, resisting high salt spray and humidity; the complete sensor urinal passed a **24-hour acidic salt spray test** and remains bright as new after years of shipboard service.
3. **500,000+ cycle life testing & performance beyond national standards** — Core solenoid valves and circuit control modules are inspected and manufactured to military standards, with a design service life of over **1,000,000 cycles** and 10+ years of use under demanding conditions; a **100% equipment-serviceability rate** has been maintained throughout commissioning, safeguarding smooth vessel operation.

### Deep Dive 2: Serving Foxconn's 8 Core Campuses Nationwide

GIBO has cultivated deep expertise in sensor sanitary ware for industrial parks, forming a long-term strategic partnership with Foxconn as the core supplier of bathroom equipment for its campuses. Three reasons the world's OEM giant chose GIBO:

1. **Ultra-large-scale deployment · 20,000+ sets** — Supplied over **20,000 sets** of squat-toilet sensor flushers, sensor faucets, and other products to Foxconn's 8 core campuses nationwide (Shenzhen, Taiyuan, Zhengzhou, Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan, Langfang, etc.), covering production, living, and office areas — a leading single-project benchmark for sensor sanitary ware in Chinese industrial parks.
2. **1 million tons of water saved annually · significant cost reduction** — Smart water-saving control technology precisely manages flow duration and volume, achieving an average water-saving rate of **40%–60%**; Foxconn's campuses nationwide save over **1 million tons of water annually** (equivalent to a small reservoir), helping reduce overall campus operating costs by **more than 40%**.
3. **12 years of stable operation · failure rate below 0.5%** — Continuous close cooperation since first supply in **2013**; core solenoid valves passed **500,000-cycle life testing**, complete units are rated **IPX6 or above** for water resistance, and the failure rate stays below **0.5%** in high-frequency scenarios exceeding 200 uses per day.
