## 1. Product Introduction

The **TYREK Electrolytic Sterilization Module D05-01** is a palm-sized electrolyzed-water generation module connected to the tap-water line. Once powered, it electrolyzes ordinary tap water into a flow carrying hypochlorous acid (HClO), hydroxyl (OH⁻), and dissolved oxygen that sterilizes and purifies as it flows. It solves the most common annoyance of sealed water paths in smart toilets and floor-scrubber tanks: long-stored water breeds bacterial biofilm, ceramic bodies and pipes can't be cleaned thoroughly, and adding chemical disinfectants risks residue and hassle.

The rear-wash water path and nozzle self-cleaning of smart toilets, and the clean-water tank of floor scrubbers—these scenarios use water daily yet are inconvenient to disassemble and clean often. The D05-01 relies neither on filter cartridges nor chemical additives; it generates active sterilizing substances by electrolyzing the tap water itself, sterilizing on flow with no extra consumables and no worry about disinfectant residue.

The module is rated DC12V, current 300~900mA, outlet flow 200~800ml/min; with ordinary tap water (TDS 160~250ppm) flowing at 500ml/min under DC12V supply, the effective residual chlorine is ≥0.3ppm. Electrodes use pure titanium with a noble-metal coating, epoxy potting, and an ABS shell; the whole unit is only 63×43×28.8mm, taking minimal space once connected to the line. The electrodes endure 40,000 On/Off polarity-reversal cycles while residual chlorine stays ≥0.3ppm after aging; the unit withstands high-low temperature cycling, 7kgf/cm² water pressure, and salt spray, suiting long-term embedded operation in finished products.

Putting it together: the D05-01 gives kitchen & bath water paths "sterilization on flow, zero consumables, small size for easy integration." The following three points are the product values we believe are most worth highlighting.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Electrolyze Tap Water to Generate Hypochlorous Acid for Sterilization

During operation, water molecules decompose into hydrogen and oxygen ions; the oxygen ions combine with surrounding water molecules to form hydroxyl, while trace chlorine in tap water is electrolyzed into hypochlorous acid (HClO), which together with dissolved oxygen disinfects, sterilizes, and purifies organic and inorganic matter. Active sterilizing substances are present on flow, with no need to add a separate disinfectant.

### 2.2 No Chemical Additives, No Consumables

Relying on neither filter cartridges nor disinfectants, the sterilizing substances are generated on-site by electrolyzing tap water—no filter-replacement cost, and no concern over disinfectant residue or secondary pollution. This point is easiest to make when finished-product makers promote a "self-cleaning" selling point.

### 2.3 Sterilize Instantly on Power, Along the Water Flow

With water and power on, an active sterilizing flow is produced—clean whenever used. Every rear-wash and nozzle self-cleaning of a smart toilet, and every tank of clean water in a floor scrubber, can carry electrolytic sterilization, without scheduling a separate disinfection cycle.

### 2.4 Pure-titanium Electrodes with Noble-metal Coating — Corrosion Resistant

Electrodes are pure titanium with a noble-metal coating, in a white ABS shell, internally sealed with epoxy potting. Per GB/T 10125-2012 acid salt spray test for 48h continuously, the appearance shows no rust spots, suiting long-term embedding in humid water paths.

### 2.5 Withstands High-low Temperature Cycling — Stable in Service

Temperature-humidity cycling test: one cycle is -25℃/2h and 65℃, 90%RH/2h; 10 cycles complete with no anomaly. Storage temperature -10~60℃, able to endure the temperature changes of storage, transport, and use alongside the finished product.

### 2.6 Small Size, Easy to Integrate — 63×43×28.8mm

The module is just 63.0×43.0×28.8mm, with SMH250-02 red terminals (customizable) and silicone tubing for water in/out. It occupies minimal water-path space, easily embedding into compact structures such as smart-toilet tanks and floor-scrubber clean-water tanks.

### 2.7 Electrical Safety Isolation — Withstand Voltage and Insulation Up to Standard

Between energized and non-energized parts, AC1500V/1min is applied with leakage current <5mA and no flashover or breakdown; a DC500V insulation resistance meter reads above 100MΩ. With the outlet blocked and 7kgf/cm² applied for one minute, there is no leakage or damage—electrical and water paths are reliably isolated.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm the supply is potable water compliant with GB5749, inlet water temperature not above 50℃
2. A filter must be installed at the inlet front to prevent sand, grit, iron filings, and other debris from entering the module
3. Confirm stable DC12V supply and reserve adequate wire gauge and terminals
4. Fix the module horizontally or per finished-product structure, avoiding knocks to electrodes and potting surfaces

### 5.2 Precautions

- Water must be supplied before power; powering without water is strictly prohibited, otherwise electrodes are easily damaged and the shell may deform
- Do not leave powered on standby in a prolonged water-less state
- Clean the inlet filter periodically to prevent blockage affecting flow and electrolysis effect
- Ensure terminal insertion is firm to avoid poor contact and arcing

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Series-connect the filter and module inlet at the water inlet (silicone tubing)
2. Connect the module outlet to the target water path (toilet spray wand / clean-water tank, etc.)
3. Fix the module in the finished-product cavity and connect terminals to DC12V supply
4. Supply water first to expel air from the line and confirm normal outlet
5. Then power on; observe operating current and outlet residual chlorine (spot check)
6. System joint debugging; verify the sterilization water path follows host start/stop

### 5.4 Power-on and Verification

Water first, then power is an iron rule. After power-on, an operating current in the 300~900mA range is normal; a residual-chlorine tester can spot-check whether outlet effective residual chlorine is ≥0.3ppm. If an anomaly from powering without water occurs, cut power immediately and inspect the electrodes.

### 5.5 Maintenance and Storage

- For long-term shutdown, keep the water path filled and powered, or fully cut power and drain—dry-burning the electrodes is strictly prohibited
- Clean the filter per the finished-product maintenance cycle
- Store the module in a ventilated, dry place, away from corrosive items
