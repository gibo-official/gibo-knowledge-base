## I. Product Introduction

G61901 MINI sensor spout is GIBO's world-first quick-install sensor spout, winner of the 2021 Energy-saving Sensor Faucet Benchmark Award (guided by: Boiling Quality Data / CQC China Quality Certification Center). It uses an extremely slim design; sensor upgrade without replacing the faucet body—unscrew the original spout, mount the MINI spout, ~1 minute to complete the retrofit; the core quick-install structure won the 2015 national invention patent and PCT international patent layout. Equipped with dTOF laser sensing and dual sensors, Type-C charge 1.5h for 9 months, mechanical life 500,000 cycles—an ideal water-saving solution for small public restrooms, high-speed rail / aviation VIP lavatories, and premium spaces.

Small public restrooms, high-speed rail and aviation VIP lavatories, home homestays, age-friendly retrofit—these scenarios either have compact space limiting retrofit, or extreme requirements for energy saving and aesthetics, and most fear major work just to install a sensor faucet. G61901 is built for the need of "don't want to change the faucet, just want it smart." It uses GIBO's dTOF laser sensing, material-agnostic, color-difference-proof, paired with side 1-6cm and bottom 1-10cm dual sensors covering hand-wash and water-fetch postures; built-in 3.7V/350mAh lithium battery, Type-C charge 1.5h for 9 months, no frequent battery change.

Combining the above: G61901 lets any faucet with a standard spout interface "twist once to go automatic, charge once for most of a year." The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the original faucet has a standard spout interface, no faucet body replacement needed
- Prepare a USB Type-C charging cable (charge 1.5h on first use or when low)
- No tools, no pipe change; the whole retrofit is zero-construction

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always disconnect power (Type-C charging port) before installation and repair
- Avoid hard impact on the ABS engineering plastic shell
- Do not hot-plug the sensing module terminals with power on

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Unscrew the original faucet spout
2. Screw the MINI sensor spout into the standard interface
3. ~1 minute, no tools, no pipe change
4. Power-on self-check + periodic self-check, auto power/water cut on anomaly
5. Wave/reach at side 1-6cm or bottom 1-10cm to test water flow

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

Power-on self-check + periodic self-check, auto power/water cut on anomaly; solenoid valve self-cleaning & anti-clogging, low failure rate in complex water.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care

- On first use or low battery, USB Type-C charge 1.5h; daily no battery change, easier centralized O&M
- ABS engineering plastic shell wipe with a soft cloth, stain-resistant easy-clean
- Relying on the dual-chip interchangeable platform, control boards are interchangeable across units, efficient after-sales repair
