## I. Product Introduction

The public-restroom basin is tested most by "lasting": under high-frequency use, sensing must be accurate, it must not run continuously, and it must not clog or break at the drop of a hat. The **WYJ-G003** dual-sensor basin faucet has a front sensor head for instant output (water comes when you reach, stops when you leave) and a side sensor head for long-flow output (sense once to open, sense again to close), plus a mechanical knob for cold/mixed/hot/off four-position temperature control that still delivers water manually when powered down. Unlike the numeric-display model, it puts its effort into "stability" and "ruggedness."

This faucet is built to engineering batch-deployment standards: complete-unit life designed for 500,000 cycles, solenoid coil 15Ω±0.5, 20ms pulse, water hammer suppressed to ≤0.2MPa, and static pressure withstanding 3.5MPa valve-closed for 1 minute with no deformation or leakage. After 30,000 alternating cold/hot (65°C/room temp) pulses, function is still normal—showing the valve core and seals can handle day-to-day thermal expansion and contraction. On flow, at 0.3MPa dynamic pressure the solenoid output is ≥12L/min with whole-unit flow uniformity ≤3.0L/min (Grade 1)—enough force to wash hands without splashing everywhere.

For power, it takes 4 AA alkaline batteries or an AC100~240V-to-DC6V/1A waterproof-plug adapter; complete-unit standby current ≤45μA. Within 3 seconds of power loss the solenoid auto-closes and cannot be re-triggered by sensing, eliminating continuous flow. After 4 hours of module water immersion and boiling-water-plus-cold-water recovery, function is still normal; 6 light-source types at 15~91cm direct/oblique cause no false trigger; ESD level 4, burst ±4KV, normal operation. For contractors, this is a public-restroom basin faucet that is "long-lived, low-hammer, abuse-resistant, and stable wherever installed."

## II. Features

### 2.1 Front Sensor Instant Output—Water at Hand's Reach

When a hand or object enters the front sensing range, water outputs automatically, response ≤0.6s with a red LED flash as prompt; when the hand leaves, water auto-stops, close ≤1.0s. Continuous flow beyond 60±10s auto-closes to prevent waste from long flow.

### 2.2 Side Sensor Long-Flow—Wave to Switch

The side sensor head uses "sense once to open, sense again to close" long-flow logic, response ≤0.6s, suited to filling water, washing hair, and similar scenarios needing continuous flow; beyond 180±10s auto-closes. Side sensor priority is higher than front; on conflicting signals, the side wins.

### 2.3 Mechanical Knob Temperature Control—Usable Even When Powered Down

The side mechanical knob, from front to back, is "cold water—mixed water—hot water—off," controlling both on/off and temperature. With dead batteries or power loss, the knob still delivers cold/mixed water manually—it will not become a dead weight.

### 2.4 Two-Stage Low-Battery Warning—Buffer to Change Batteries

When supply voltage drops to 4.8V it enters the first under-voltage stage: each sensing flashes the red LED 5 times (1.5s interval), but the solenoid still works, leaving property time to change batteries; at 4.5V it enters the second stage: red LED flashes 10 times (0.5s interval) and the solenoid closes to stop work, avoiding failure in front of a customer when fully flat.

### 2.5 3-Second Power-Loss Self-Lock—Never Runs Continuously

Once power is cut (adapter unplugged or batteries removed), within 3 seconds the system auto-closes the solenoid and the product cannot be re-triggered by sensing—it must be re-powered. This eliminates from the root the hazards of "forgot to close water" and "runs continuously after batteries die."

### 2.6 Low Water Hammer + Self-Cleaning Valve—Rugged, Less Clogging

The solenoid is designed for low water hammer; the closing surge is held to ≤0.2MPa, protecting pipe joints. The valve-core structure has self-cleaning anti-clog capability, so it does not easily stick even in hard-water-scaling and silty public networks. With 3.5MPa static valve-closed for 1 minute and 1.6MPa valve-open for 1 minute, there is no deformation or leakage—truly abuse-resistant.

### 2.7 Strong-Light and Strong-EMI Immune—Stable Anywhere

After the module is immersed in 25°C water for 4 hours, then boiled in water 0.5h and recovered at 25°C for 0.5h, function is normal and sensing-distance change is under 10%; 6 light-source types (incandescent, fluorescent, halogen, bathroom heater, hairdryer, etc.) at 15~91cm direct/oblique cause no false trigger; ESD level 4, burst ±4KV, EMI 3V/m normal operation—suited to malls, hospitals, and areas around equipment rooms.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: 500,000-Cycle Life—One Deployment Lasts for Years

A public-restroom basin is used dozens to hundreds of times a day; faucet life directly determines the maintenance bill years later. The G003 complete unit is designed for 500,000 cycles, and after 30,000 alternating 65°C/room-temp pulses function is still normal; the solenoid coil is 15Ω±0.5 and water hammer is held to ≤0.2MPa to reduce pipe impact. For a 50-unit public restroom, this means not replacing a batch every year but stable operation counted in years—both service tickets and spare-part inventory can be pushed down.

### Selling Point 2: Low Water Hammer, No Splash, No Noise—Piping Also Less Damaged

Many sensor faucets "thud" with water hammer on each open/close, and over time joints loosen and pipes leak. The G003 holds water surge to ≤0.2MPa—quiet open/close, pipe-friendly; meanwhile the self-cleaning valve core resists sticking in complex water quality. The two property nightmares—"midnight burst-pipe repair call" and "dripping faucet that won't close"—this unit structurally reduces by more than half.

### Selling Point 3: 3-Second Power-Loss Self-Lock—Safety Red Line Held

What public spaces fear most is equipment that keeps flowing after power loss, or that someone re-triggers when there is no power. The G003 auto-closes the solenoid within 3 seconds of power loss and cannot be re-triggered—it must be re-powered; low voltage also has two-stage warning—still usable at 4.8V with a battery-change reminder, valve closes only at 4.5V. Paired with ≤45μA standby and 4 alkaline batteries, it saves power yet never "fails silently"—giving maintenance peace of mind.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. First flush the pipeline with water to clear sand and rust, avoiding solenoid clogging.
2. Confirm water pressure 0.05~0.8MPa; below 0.05MPa add a booster pump.
3. Confirm power method (battery box or DC6V waterproof-plug adapter); reserve a power point for the adapter in advance.
4. Check the basin hole; standard basin uses M24×1 standard union, kitchen faucet uses M24×1 to M22×1 extension coupling.

### 5.2 Notes

- Always shut water and cut power before install/repair (remove battery box or unplug adapter).
- Use same-brand high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new.
- Do not hot-plug sensor module terminals; avoid scratching the potted surface.
- The mechanical knob's temperature range includes an "off" position; turn to off when unused to prevent accidental opening.
- Lock the union at 20±0.2 N·M held 300s; too loose or too tight affects sealing.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix per faucet-body hole; connect cold/hot inlet pipes and lock the union.
2. Connect the control module; open water and pressure-test to confirm no leak.
3. Install battery box or connect DC6V waterproof-plug adapter.
4. Power on: within ≤30s complete power-on response and enter standby.
5. Test front-sensor instant output and side-sensor long-flow in front of the sensor window.
6. Turn the mechanical knob to confirm cold/mixed/hot/off four positions normal.

### 5.4 Power-On Self-Check

Within ≤30s of power-on it enters sense-ready state; the first sense flashes the red LED once to indicate trigger. To re-power, simply cut and restore power—no need to wait minutes like the numeric-display model.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement / Power-Loss Recovery

When voltage drops to 4.8V, each sense flashes the red LED 5 times to remind battery change (valve still works); below 4.5V flashes 10 times and closes valve. Replacement: shut water → remove battery box → replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-power. If power is lost unexpectedly during operation, the solenoid self-locks and closes water within 3s; it can only be re-sensed after re-powering.

## VI. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Malls / Shopping Centers

Dense weekend crowds; dual sensors cover both hand-washing and filling; 500,000-cycle life and low-water-hammer design mean less maintenance under high frequency; works normally amid atrium strong light and escalator strong EMI.

### 6.2 Office Buildings / Corporate HQ

Sensitive user experience; front-sensor instant output and side-sensor long filling are both handy; 3-second power-loss self-lock holds the safety red line, easy handover for property.

### 6.3 Hospitals / Elderly-Care Institutions

Non-contact reduces cross-infection; mechanical knob still delivers cold water when powered down; two-stage low-voltage warning avoids failing in front of customers when flat—aging-friendly.

### 6.4 Schools / Venues

Students running and splashing—Liteon algorithm filters transient interference to reduce false triggers; timeout auto-close prevents long flow, saving water and worry.

### 6.5 Airports / Transport Hubs / Kitchen Scenarios

24-hour operation, short maintenance windows—adapter permanent power avoids battery changes; standard M24×1 union fits basin, extension coupling to M22×1 can mount on kitchen faucets—one module, multiple scenarios.
