## I. Product Introduction

The Z Series full-feature smart toilet is a smart bathroom solution built for high-rise residences, old communities, and low-water-pressure households. What makes it different is the **recessed water tank** — a built-in all-in-one ceramic tank plus a micro booster pump that flushes waste cleanly without relying on municipal water pressure, solving the long-standing problem of old houses with insufficient pressure that "can't flush clean." From seat-occupancy sensing, rear/feminine wash, warm-air drying, to auto flush on leaving, the whole process is touchless, making the act of using the toilet more hygienic, comfortable, and dignified.

It packs instant hot water (1-second heating to 35℃), seat heating, auto open/close lid and seat, foot-sensor flush, and a blue night light into one body. The base uses a modular water-electricity-separated design for easy future repair and replacement. Whether it is an old, low-pressure home or a newly renovated one aiming to upgrade, the Z Series installs directly without extra plumbing changes or added booster pumps. The three points below are the product values we think are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Recessed Water Tank and Booster Flush System

An all-in-one ceramic tank embedded in the base, with a flat modular design achieving water-electricity separation. A built-in booster pump ensures super-vortex turbine-level flushing force — sweeping waste away like a wave, leaving the bowl clean with no residue. Drain outlet diameter DN100 (minimum), fitting U-PVC pipe DE110 and above. Flush volume 5L, 110mm floor-outlet drainage, Grade-3 water-saving. The proprietary dual-vortex turbine siphon jet-flush technology uses two strong water columns on the left and right inner walls plus a central multi-hole jet column to form an all-around vortex flow, cleaning with no dead corners.

### 2.2 Instant Hot-water Wash System

A ceramic instant heater is the core component; the zero-storage-water design prevents long-stored water from breeding bacteria. An air-self-priming nozzle mixes in air to form a soft water stream, saving water and comfortable. Two independent water channels: rear wash (moving massage wash) + feminine wash (gentle wash). Three adjustable water-pressure levels meet different washing intensities.

### 2.3 dTOF Laser Foot-sensor System

U106 IRM / U106 dTOF foot-sensor module technical parameters: input voltage DC5.0V±10%; response time 256ms (dTOF) / <100ms (infrared); standby power ≤45μA (dTOF) / <60μA (infrared); sensing range 10±0.5cm (dTOF) / 0–18cm (infrared); waterproof rating IP67 (dTOF); cut-out size φ24mm (dTOF) / φ35mm (infrared); service life >0.5 million cycles; core advantage is color-agnostic recognition (equally sensitive to black soles), strong light-interference resistance, and elimination of self-sensing false actions.

### 2.4 Auto Open/Close Lid System

Three modes selectable: (1) microwave sensing auto-opens lid when a person approaches; (2) a toe tap triggers lid open → another tap opens seat; (3) one-touch remote control. Standing operation is especially friendly to men. Double-layer silent slow-close lid prevents pinching, with IPX4 waterproof design.

### 2.5 Seat Heating and Warm-air Drying

PTC constant-temperature seat heating, four levels adjustable 33–37℃, uniform heating across the whole seat with temperature difference <1℃. Warm-air drying uses PTC temperature control + precision motor (4500rpm), four wind-temperature levels, wind speed up to 4m/s, quick drying without stuffiness.

### 2.6 Multiple Safety Protections

A six-fold safety system: leakage protection, overheat protection, anti-dry-burn protection, water-stop protection, low-temperature freeze protection, and flame-retardant fire-resistant materials. A mechanical button allows emergency flush during power outage, ensuring basic function is unaffected.

### 2.7 Blue-light Night Light

A photosensitive blue-light night light, remotely switchable. Auto-dim glow guides nighttime trips, caring for the elderly and insomniacs without harsh light affecting sleep.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Rough-in measurement: distance from tile surface to drain center hole; if tiles not yet laid, subtract 5cm from the measured value; choose 300 rough-in for 300–399mm, choose 400 rough-in for ≥400mm
2. Power confirmation: a 5-hole 10A socket must be reserved in the bathroom, approx. 500mm above floor, away from the shower area
3. Water supply confirmation: reserve a DN15 cold-water pipe and angle valve, ensuring water pressure is within the required range

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- Keep the socket away from the shower area to avoid splashing
- Remove the smart lid by lifting the rear end upward as a whole; install by pressing the front end downward; do not lift from the front end upward
- When unused for a long time, recommend disconnecting power and draining the water stored in the tank

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Check drain pipe is clear → install seal ring → align with the sewer outlet and set down
2. Mark lines → drill holes and install expansion bolts → apply glass glue → press firmly into place
3. Connect water inlet pipe → install filter → clean the floor
4. Glass glue cures approx. 24 hours; avoid water contact during this period
5. Power-on test: power-on self-check → test each function is normal → set user preference parameters

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, enter the self-check flow, sequentially verifying lid open, foot sensor, hot-water heating, seat heating, warm air, flush and other modules; enter standby after confirming each function is normal; user preference parameters can be set via remote control.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- The pre-installed water-purifying filter recommends replacing the cartridge every 3–6 months (depending on water quality)
- Smart lid removal: lift the rear end upward to remove as a whole; install by pressing the front end downward; do not lift from the front end upward
- Periodically check wireless accessory battery level and replace promptly
- If blockage occurs: first confirm whether it is a soft-object blockage (use a clearing tool); hard-object blockage requires professional disassembly and repair
- When unused for a long time, recommend disconnecting power and draining the water stored in the tank

## VI. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 High-rise Residence

Low-water-pressure areas on floors 6–10 need no extra booster pump; the built-in pump solves insufficient flush force; dual rough-in and water-electricity-separated base lower installation and after-sales difficulty.

### 6.2 Old Community Renovation

No need to replace the original drain pipes; 305/400mm dual rough-in fits most old-house reserved conditions; the recessed water tank is unaffected by old-pipe pressure fluctuations.

### 6.3 Low-water-pressure-area Households

When original-water supply on floors 1–6 or end-of-line pressure is insufficient, the recessed water tank + booster pump ensures flush effect; instant hot water without storage is more hygienic.

### 6.4 Age-friendly / Home Elderly Care

Foot-sensor flush eliminates bending, seat heating prevents cold, auto flush prevents forgetting, night light prevents falls — all-around care for the elderly; touchless operation reduces cross-infection.

### 6.5 Hotel Apartment Fit-out

Elevates the guest experience grade, differentiated competition; full-feature configuration matches mid-to-high-end positioning; modular base lowers O&M costs.

### 6.6 Rehabilitation Institution / Nursing Home

Water washing replaces paper wiping for better hygiene, reducing care workload and infection risk; multiple safety protections and emergency mechanical flush ensure basic use.
