## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6128 is GIBO's high-cost-performance SUS304 stainless steel sensor basin faucet for engineering projects that require quality material but are budget-sensitive. The whole unit uses 304 stainless steel for the spout body and waterway system, with no copper contacting the water flow, ensuring pure and safe output; the round-head brushed rose-gold exterior is simple and modern, understated yet textured. The product can pair with GIBO's new dual-valve mixing control box (AD type) to achieve independent hot/cold dual-line control and thermostatic mixing, meeting various project needs from single-cold to hot/cold temperature adjustment. The control box uses an ABS brown shell and a PA66+30%GF reinforced nylon valve seat, with a built-in 121B solenoid coil assembly, supporting both CN and EU power interface specs to fit domestic and overseas project standards.

Chain restaurants, economy hotels, and schools need strong material, controlled budget, bulk deployment, and easy maintenance. The 6128 packs the two core advantages of "full 304 stainless zero-copper leaching" and "dual-valve mixing temperature control" into a highly competitive price—a pragmatic choice for engineering bulk purchase. The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Auto Sensing

Reach into the sensing zone to auto-start flow; leave to immediately shut off. Typical sensing distance 30cm±2cm (vs standard white board), wide coverage, adapting to different mounting heights.

### 2.2 Dual-valve Mixing Control

The AD control box is equipped with hot/cold dual solenoid valve seats (SL-FZ-6128); PA66+30%GF reinforced material ensures long-term water-pressure stability. Each line has an independent check valve design to prevent hot/cold crossflow.

### 2.3 Multi-spec Power Adapter

Supports DC battery and AC adapter dual modes. The AC version offers CN two-pin and EU two-pin form-factor specs, so export projects can select directly without an adapter.

### 2.4 Split Under-counter Installation

The control box is hidden in the under-counter cabinet; only the round-head brushed stainless spout remains on the countertop. Standard G1/2 thread, strong compatibility, install/maintenance without harming the countertop look.

### 2.5 Filter Anti-clog

The inlet is equipped with a rubber washer with filter mesh (19×3mm spec) that effectively intercepts pipe sand/debris and extends solenoid valve life.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Full 304 Stainless Waterway — Pure Output, Zero Copper Leaching

The faucet body and waterway are made entirely of SUS304 stainless steel, eliminating the lead-leaching risk of traditional copper faucets. Stainless hardness is far above copper, with stronger resistance to accidental impact and scratches.

### Selling Point 2: Dual-valve Mixing Control Box — Independent Hot/Cold Precise Control

Paired with the AD dual-valve control box, hot and cold water lines are controlled by independent solenoid valves, with temperature adjusted via a mechanical handle. Supports AC110~220V converted power, optional CN or EU two-pin spec, flexibly fitting the global market.

### Selling Point 3: Excellent Cost-performance — Preferred for Engineering Bulk Purchase

Delivers the SUS304 all-stainless core advantage at a highly competitive price; the classic round-head shape fits most basin holes. Brushed surface is durable, suited to high-frequency bulk deployment in chain restaurants and budget hotels.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole matches the faucet body and control box; prepare G1/2 inlet pipe and angle valve
- Confirm water pressure is within 0.05~0.7MPa
- Pre-select the AD control box power spec (CN two-pin / EU two-pin)

### 5.2 Precautions

- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse or soak with water
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries
- Adjust the hot/cold ratio via the side mechanical handle on the control box (AD type)

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Remove the lock nut and washers from the faucet body, insert into the basin hole and tighten to fix
2. Connect the faucet body hose to the control box OUTLET; the user's own inlet pipe connects to INLET and then to the angle valve
3. Connect the signal wire to the matching control box port
4. DC version inserts batteries; AC version plugs in the power adapter
5. Supply water and test the sensing function; installation is complete when normal

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

Supply water and test the sensing function; installation is complete once normal flow and signal connection are confirmed.

### 5.5 Maintenance / Battery Replacement

- Regularly check whether the inlet filter is clogged with sand; clean promptly
- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse or soak with water
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries
