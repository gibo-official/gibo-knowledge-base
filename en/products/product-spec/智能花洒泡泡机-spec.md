## I. Product Introduction

The Smart Shower Foam Machine is an innovative **shower companion device** for high-end bathroom scenarios, built for children's fun bathing and adults' SPA relaxation. As an external smart accessory for existing shower heads, it uses a built-in air pump and dedicated foam liquid to quickly generate rich, fine foam, upgrading daily showering into a "bubble bath." Triggered by non-contact sensing or one-touch, with adjustable foam output, it fits various shower-head specifications (hand / overhead). It gets children who dislike bathing to enter the bathroom willingly, and lets busy adults enjoy a SPA relaxation at home.

Ordinary households wanting a bubble bath either buy a dedicated bathtub or use bottles and jars to manually whip foam — both space-consuming and troublesome. This foam machine hangs directly on the original shower head, assembles in about 3 minutes, freely switches between bubble mode and normal shower, and loses none of the original shower functions. The three points below are the values most worth highlighting.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Select installation position: hand-shower rod recommended at 1.0–1.4m above floor (child-reachable zone), or at the overhead bracket below
- Confirm compatibility with hand-shower-rod outer diameter and overhead bracket interface

### 5.2 Precautions

- Avoid fully immersing the foam outlet in water
- Wipe the shell with a slightly damp soft cloth; periodically rinse the foam outlet with clean water to prevent residue blockage
- When unused for a long time, recommend draining the liquid tank and removing the batteries for storage

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Position selection: select a suitable height on the hand-shower rod (recommended 1.0–1.4m above floor, child-reachable zone), or fix at the overhead bracket below
2. Fixing method: use the included clamp / threaded adapter per the product manual to ensure firm, non-loose fixation that does not affect the shower's normal angle adjustment
3. Fill foam liquid: open the tank cap and inject appropriate foam liquid (do not exceed the max water line), tighten the seal
4. Power on / install: insert batteries or connect power
5. Test foam: start the device and check foam output is normal, confirming satisfactory bubble size and coverage
6. Usage notes: avoid fully immersing the foam outlet in water; when unused for a long time, recommend draining the liquid tank and removing the batteries for storage
7. Cleaning maintenance: wipe the shell with a slightly damp soft cloth; periodically rinse the foam outlet with clean water to prevent residue blockage

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After inserting batteries or connecting power, start the device and check foam output is normal, confirming satisfactory bubble size, coverage, and sensor/button trigger.

### 5.5 Maintenance
