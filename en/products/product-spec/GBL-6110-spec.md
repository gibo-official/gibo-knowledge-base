## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6110 is GIBO's engineering-classic full-brass sensor basin faucet (split-type), continuously produced and sold for over 20 years since launch, a rare "evergreen" sensor faucet on the market. After two decades of iteration, its circuit design and material craftsmanship have reached a highly mature state with extremely low failure rate. The split structure separates the faucet body from the sensor control box, with high installation flexibility, suited to various basin and wall-mount scenarios.

Schools, factories, stations, parks, economy hotels—these public venues use at high frequency, are budget-sensitive, and their requirements for reliability and maintenance convenience often outweigh fancy features. The 6110 is built for this contract market: full-brass chrome-plated sturdy housing, well-sealed internal components, able to adapt to complex water quality and long-term operation under different climates. With excellent cost-performance, ample spare parts supply, and convenient maintenance, the 6110 is one of the preferred sensor faucets for budget-sensitive public projects and the contract market.

Combining the above: the 6110 proves with 20 years of market validation that a "stable, easy-to-repair, cheap" sensor faucet matters more than anything. The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Split Modular Architecture

The faucet body (with outlet, aerator) and the sensor control box (with power, circuit board, solenoid valve, sensor probe) are connected via hose and can be placed separately. This design gives more flexible installation—the control box can be hidden under the basin or in a cabinet, exposing only the attractive faucet head.

### 2.2 Stable Infrared Sensing

Mature active infrared sensing technology, sensing distance 20cm±2cm. Flow on reach, off on leave, with basic timeout protection against accidental continuous flow. The sensor window is specially optically treated, with strong anti-ambient-light interference.

### 2.3 Full Brass Chrome-plated Shell

Both the faucet head and the control box shell are full brass with multi-layer chrome plating. Copper itself has antibacterial properties; combined with the smooth, dense chrome layer, daily cleaning is easy and dirt/scale buildup is unlikely.

### 2.4 Standardized Interface

The inlet uses standard G1/2 thread, compatible with generic hoses and angle valves. The outlet is equipped with a standard aerator for even, gentle flow. All interfaces follow industry norms, easing later part replacement.

### 2.5 Multiple Power Options

Beyond standard DC6V, an AC110-240V input version can be customized (built-in rectifier/step-down module), or a battery power option (specific battery model to be verified).

### 2.6 Wide Water-pressure Adaptability

Designed for a wide pressure range, it works normally within the common municipal 0.05~0.8 MPa and has a built-in flow regulator ensuring consistent output across pressures.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Plan the positions of the faucet body and control box per basin or wall situation, ensuring enough hose length margin between them
- Confirm the inlet is a G1/2 standard interface; prepare matching hose and angle valve

### 5.2 Precautions

- Fix the control box on the under-basin cabinet or bracket, ensuring it is not soaked by standing water
- After connecting power, confirm the indicator lights up for normal standby

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Pass the faucet through the basin hole (or wall reserved hole), tighten the fixing nut below/behind
2. Fix the sensor control box at a suitable under-basin position (usually screw-fixed), ensuring it is not soaked
3. Use the supplied hose to connect the control box outlet to the faucet body inlet; connect the hot/cold inlet pipes to the control box inlet
4. Connect DC6V power or AC power (custom version); confirm the indicator lights up for normal standby
5. Test flow/shut-off sensitivity within the sensing range; fine-tune the sensor probe angle if necessary

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After connecting power, the lit indicator means normal standby; test flow/shut-off sensitivity within the sensing range and fine-tune the sensor probe angle if necessary.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care (Engineering)

- Check sensor window cleanliness monthly; wipe dust and stains
- Check battery level quarterly (if battery powered)
- Check hose connections for leaks semi-annually
- Inspect/replace the aerator annually (by water quality and frequency)

Function Definition — Wireless Sensor Box
[Image]
Wireless sensor box
1. Within the normal communication range, when an object is detected at the wireless box's sensing interface, the receiver (wireless control box) automatically opens the solenoid valve to release water.
2. When the hand or object is detected again at the wireless box's sensing interface, the receiver (wireless control box) automatically closes the solenoid valve to stop water.
3. If the system outputs water continuously for 180s±6s, it automatically closes the solenoid valve to stop water.
4. When the wireless box receives an open-water sensing signal, the white-light (tentative) LED halo lights up for 3s in a fade-out mode, then lights up once every 5s until the 180s water-output process terminates.
5. When sensing closes the water, the LED halo does not light up.
[Image]

Function Definition — Wireless Control Box
[Image]
Wireless control box
1. Together with the wireless faucet sensor box, within the applicable voltage range, the wireless faucet control box can normally receive signals and operate; the pulse-driven solenoid valve can act normally.
2. Within the normal communication range, when the wireless faucet sensor box detects an object, the receiver (wireless faucet control box) opens the solenoid valve to release water; when the wireless faucet sensor box detects the object again, the receiver closes the solenoid valve to stop water.
3. Continuous water output 180s±10s, automatic protection stops water output.
4. Pairing for the first 30 seconds after each power-on.
5. After the wireless faucet control box loses power, the solenoid valve closes within 20 seconds.
