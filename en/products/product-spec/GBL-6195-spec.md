## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6195 Xiaoxibao 2nd Gen is an innovative product GIBO launched in 2018, condensing complex Sensor Faucet technology into a replaceable spout. Users don't need to replace the entire faucet body — just unscrew the original spout and fit the Xiaoxibao to turn an ordinary faucet from "manual" to "automatic." It addresses the pain point of many households that want a Sensor Faucet but don't want the cost of a full remodel: in a finished kitchen or bathroom, reworking the plumbing and countertop is expensive, whereas swapping the spout takes just one minute.

Family kitchens, bathrooms, rental homes, and guesthouses — these are places that want the convenience of touchless water use without major disruption. The Xiaoxibao is built for exactly this "light retrofit" scenario: it adopts Liteon's optimized downward-sensing chip, sensing downward so water flows when you reach out and stops when you pull away, matching the natural hand-washing posture; a built-in flow restrictor paired with a constant-flow aerator delivers significant water savings. The snap-fit quick connector supports both M22 external-thread and M24 internal-thread fittings, compatible with over 90% of kitchen and basin faucets on the market (excluding pull-out, low-arc, and irregular spouts), meeting diverse household water needs.

Put together, the Xiaoxibao makes "swap the spout = install smart" a reality — no plumbing work, no countertop modification, one minute to give an old faucet sensor-activated flow, water savings, and touchless hygiene. The three points below are the product values we think are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Liteon Downward Sensing Technology

Adopts an optimized downward-sensing optical path: water flows when you reach out and stops when you withdraw your hand, matching the natural hand-washing posture; a built-in flow restrictor combined with a constant-flow aerator delivers water savings, and continuous flow auto-stops after 1 minute to avoid waste from running water. The sensing window features special optical treatment that resists ambient light interference and stays sensitive even in greasy environments.

### 2.2 Neoperl Aerator + LED Indicator

Uses a Neoperl aerator for soft, dense, splash-free flow with no dripping. The blue LED operating indicator lights up to show the spout's working status and illuminate the water level, making it more convenient for nighttime use.

### 2.3 Power-loss Protection Mechanism

Built-in power-loss protection circuit automatically closes the solenoid valve when power is lost or the battery is depleted, ensuring the final shut-off action is completed and avoiding the risk of running water.

### 2.4 Optional Temperature Display / TDS Display

Optional temperature display (real-time water temperature at a glance) or TDS value display (intuitive water-quality status) available, with water temperature and TDS freely switchable.

### 2.5 Two-tone Surface Finish

Two color options: Luxury Chrome (blends perfectly with chrome faucets for a seamless look) and Pearl White (natural bright finish, clean and elegant with a tech feel).

### 2.6 All-brass Adapter Fitting

The adapter fitting is made of solid brass for durability and corrosion resistance. Combined with the snap-fit quick-connect structure, repeated assembly and disassembly won't easily loosen or leak.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Touchless Sensor Flow — Free Your Hands, Effectively Prevent Cross-infection

Water flows when you reach out and stops when you withdraw, with no need to touch the faucet handle throughout. Each time you touch a faucet handle, hundreds of millions of bacteria (up to 180 types) are transferred to your hands; touchless sensing reduces the spread of bacteria among family members at the source, especially suited to kitchen scenarios where hands are covered in grease or flour.

### Selling Point 2: Snap-fit Quick Install, Plug-and-play — One-minute Installation, Fits 90%+ of Faucets

Installation is completed with a gentle press, no professional plumber required. Equipped with both M22 external-thread and M24 internal-thread all-brass adapter fittings, compatible with the vast majority of basin and kitchen faucets on the market. Even a homemaker can complete the installation independently, truly achieving "swap the spout = install smart."

### Selling Point 3: Ultra-low-power Rechargeable Design — 186 Days per Charge, ≥ 32% Water Saving

Built-in 300mAh lithium battery, USB charging (Type-C port), standby power consumption only 80μA, one charge lasts approx. 186 days. The constant-flow Neoperl aerator ensures stable flow, delivering comprehensive water-saving efficiency ≥ 32% versus traditional faucets — saving both electricity and water.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Check the thread specification of the original faucet spout; confirm it is M22 external thread or M24 internal thread
- Pull-out faucets, low-arc faucets, and irregular-spout faucets are not applicable; rule them out first

### 5.2 Precautions

- Before installation, confirm the faucet outlet thread is free of debris and scale
- Use a USB Type-C cable for charging; avoid inferior chargers

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Unscrew the original spout counterclockwise and clean debris and scale from the faucet outlet thread
2. Align the GIBO Xiaoxibao spout of the matching specification with the thread and tighten clockwise until the snap-fit structure makes a "click" sound, indicating it is locked
3. Long-press the power button (if any) or simply approach the sensing area to test the flow; use normally after confirming sensor sensitivity

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

Approaching the sensing area triggers flow detection; the blue LED indicator lighting up means the unit has entered working status. On first use, just confirm sensor sensitivity.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- When the LED indicator blinks abnormally or sensing becomes sluggish, charge with a USB Type-C cable
- Regularly wipe the sensing window with a soft cloth to maintain light transmission; avoid grease buildup that affects sensor sensitivity
