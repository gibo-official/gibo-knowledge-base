## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6170 is GIBO's "single-window dual-sensor" basin sensor faucet first pioneered in 2019, granted an invention patent in 2020. Its core innovation: one sensor window integrates two independent optical paths on the left and right, corresponding to the left and right basin areas—when a person reaches out on the left, only the left side flows; when a person reaches out on the right, only the right side flows, without interference. This design lets a high-traffic dual-station handwashing counter serve two users with just one faucet, saving 50% of countertop hole-cutting and procurement cost, and greatly reducing later O&M workload.

In the public restrooms of malls, office buildings, airports and stations, schools, and hospitals, dual-station handwashing counters are standard and a major share of procurement and maintenance cost. The 6170 is built for such high-frequency, dual-user, maintenance-sensitive environments: it adopts the Liteon single-probe dual-sensor chip, supporting two water-use modes—"long-range hand-present sensing" (flows on presence, stops when hand leaves) and "short-range touch sensing" (tap to start flow, tap again to stop); the top integrates a temperature knob with forced shut-off. The modular tower-type integrated flow channel encapsulates the waterway, solenoid valve, probe, and ceramic cartridge into one sealed unit—looking no different from an ordinary faucet, with identical installation.

Putting the above together: the 6170 lets a dual-station counter "use one faucet as two," halving procurement, installation, and maintenance points while not compromising the experience. The three points below are the product values we think most worth highlighting.

## II. Target Customers

The GBL-6170 basin dual-sensor faucet focuses on "high-frequency, dual-user, maintenance-sensitive" public restroom scenarios, while also entering the home-upgrade market that values aesthetics and smart experience.

### 2.1 Core Customer Segments

- **Public Commercial (main)**: Operators of dual-station handwashing counters in malls, office buildings, airports and stations, schools, hospitals, stadiums and other public restrooms, as well as property and project procurement. Such venues standardize dual-station counters, which are also a major share of procurement and maintenance cost, with the strongest demand for "cost reduction and less maintenance."
- **Home Upgrade (incremental)**: Post-80s/90s/00s families who value quality of life and prefer smart and good-looking bathrooms; women's decision weight in home-consumption has risen significantly, making them core influencers for makeup/removal and parent-child handwashing scenarios.
- **Channel Partners**: Distributors, renovation companies, design firms, and real-estate finished-fitout and hotel-room smartification project parties.

### 2.2 Market Opportunity

- China has about 250 million urban households; sensor faucet penetration in public spaces has exceeded 20%, while household bathroom sensor faucet/tapware usage is below 1%—a huge blank market remains.
- Smart home system penetration is about 5% with an annual growth rate of about 50%; "health, eco-friendliness, safety, personalization, and cost-performance" have become the main theme of bathroom product design, providing a consumption-upgrade window for differentiated products like the single-window dual-sensor.

- Faucets are touched frequently, spreading bacteria among family members—each switch-on picks up hundreds of millions of bacteria of over 180 types.

## III. Features

### 3.1 Single-window Dual Optical-path Patent Technology

Within a single sensor window, two sets of independent infrared emit/receive optical paths are placed left and right, with strong spatial resolution, so left and right handwashing positions operate without affecting each other. It solves the traditional dual-faucet problems of large space occupation and high cost.

### 3.2 Mechanical Temperature Adjustment + Forced Shut-off

The top temperature knob turns right for cold, left for hot; turning the knob fully left forcibly shuts off the water, combining temperature adjustment and emergency shut-off.

### 3.3 Multiple Safety Protections

- **Debris Filtration**: Filter mesh washers at the front of both adapters filter sediment from water
- **Anti-crossflow Check Valve**: Built-in check valve in adapters prevents hot/cold crossflow
- **Power-off Shut-off Protection**: Solenoid valve auto-closes on power loss
- **Low-battery Indicator**: Indicator prompts when battery is low

### 3.4 Three Power Supply Options

- DC supply: 4× AA alkaline batteries, life ≥ 18 months
- External AC power adapter (optional): AC220V input, no battery change needed
- Lithium battery (optional): 2800mAh lithium battery, life ≥ 36 months

### 3.5 Tool-free Installation with Dedicated Sleeve

Comes with a dedicated installation sleeve; the hex-socket end tightens the faucet's fixing nut, and the wrench end tightens the inlet hose nut. No extra tools needed; ordinary users can complete installation.

### 3.6 Broad Basin Compatibility

Supports countertop basin (faucet on the counter), low-rim countertop basin (basin height ≤ 6cm), high-rim countertop basin (basin height > 6cm), undercounter basin and other mounting forms; hole diameter Φ32~40mm all compatible.

## IV. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Single-window Dual-sensor Patent — One Faucet Serves Dual Stations, Saving 50% O&M Cost

Granted invention patent in 2020. One sensor window embeds left/right dual optical paths, triggering independent left/right flow without interference. In dual-station scenarios, procurement is halved, installation labor is halved, and later maintenance points are halved—comprehensive cost reduction of up to 50%.

### Selling Point 2: Liteon Dual-sensor Modes — Long-range Hand-present Sensing + Short-range Touch Sensing, Switch as You Like

Reaching into the sensing zone (3~25cm range) triggers the long-duration flow mode, suited to hand/face washing and other routine scenarios, auto-closing after 1 minute of no operation; lightly touching the sensor window surface (0~3cm range) triggers the short-duration flow mode, suited to quick water fetch and rinsing, auto-closing after 3-minute timeout. The two modes cover all-scenario water needs.

### Selling Point 3: Full Brass Chrome-plated + Integrated Flow Channel Flow Limiting — Water-saving ≥ 30%, Enduring Like New

Full brass chrome-plated faucet body, with luxury chrome / brushed nickel / black-gold / porcelain-white color options. The patented tower-type integrated flow channel uses an all-plastic flow design (lead-free, eco-friendly); the built-in flow-limiting structure achieves water-saving efficiency ≥ 30%. IPX6 waterproof rating, Grade 1 Water Efficiency certified.

## VI. Installation Instructions

### 6.1 Before Installation

- Confirm basin hole diameter within Φ32~40mm
- Select corresponding installation per countertop/undercounter basin type

### 6.2 Precautions

- During installation, ensure the filter mesh washers and check valves of both adapters are seated to prevent clogging and hot/cold crossflow
- Confirm correct polarity before connecting battery or power; solenoid valve auto-closes on power loss

### 6.3 Installation Steps

1. Pass faucet through basin hole, then from below use the hex-socket end of the dedicated sleeve to tighten the fixing nut
2. Use the wrench end of the dedicated sleeve to tighten the hot/cold inlet hose nuts (moderate torque recommended to avoid stripping)
3. Install filter mesh washers and check valves at the front of both adapters before connecting to angle valves
4. Insert batteries or connect power
5. Reach hands to test left/right sensor flow response

### 6.4 Power-on Self-check

After inserting batteries or connecting power, reach hands to test left/right sensor flow response; confirm the dual optical paths do not interfere.

### 6.5 Maintenance & Care

- Inspect filter mesh washers for sediment buildup and clean quarterly
- Inspect check valve sealing annually
- If sensing is sluggish, clean the sensor window
- To fine-tune sensing distance, use the remote control or adjustment screw to suit different basin depths
