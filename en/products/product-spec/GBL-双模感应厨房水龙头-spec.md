## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL Dual-mode Sensor Kitchen Faucet** has two sensing paths on the body—upper and lower: the lower sensing window handles "water on at hand, off when withdrawn," and the upper sensing window handles "wave to open, wave again to close." The two sensing logics each govern their own task, so dishwashing, filling, and sink rinsing in the kitchen need no hand contact even when wet or dirty, and won't keep false-flowing just because the sink is piled with pots and pans.

Home kitchens, restaurant back kitchens, central kitchens, beverage bars—anywhere hands are often occupied at the sink and cross-contamination is feared, dual-mode beats single sensing. Lower sensing suits continuous "rinse while washing" actions; upper sensing suits fixed "fill a pot then close" operations—two habits in one faucet.

It runs on both AC and DC—an AC220V adapter or 4× AA batteries—with static power ≤100μA; the battery solution needs no wiring and retrofits easily. Sensing response instant-on ≤512ms, open/close ≤256ms, continuous flow beyond 3 minutes auto-cuts to prevent long flow from persistent obstacles in the sensing zone. Water-pressure strength to 1.9MPa, protection IP54, durability ≥500,000 cycles—endures the kitchen's humid, oily environment. For engineering and homes alike, this is a sensor kitchen faucet that "feels right once installed and needs no constant wiping."

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Upper-and-lower Dual-mode Sensing, Wash/Rinse/Fill All Handy

The lower sensing window governs "instant on at hand, off when leaving," suiting continuous rinse-while-wash actions; the upper sensing window governs "wave to open, wave again to close," suiting fixed fill-then-stop operations. The two logics don't clash—no false trigger even with hands occupied at the sink and pots piled in it.

### 2.2 Two Flow Modes, Switch by Habit

Supports both "instant on/off" and "open/close toggle" functions. Prototype is configured for instant on/off; the open/close toggle suits scenarios needing long stable flow—two hand waves lock it, no need to keep a hand in the sensing zone.

### 2.3 AC/DC Dual-use, Wiring-free Retrofit

Uses AC220V (switching power adapter) or DC6V (4× AA alkaline batteries). The battery solution needs no rewiring in the kitchen—old houses, tenant kitchens, and back-kitchen add-ons can all go directly, install-and-use.

### 2.4 3-minute Timeout Protection, Prevents Long Flow

Continuous flow beyond 3min±30s auto-cuts, specifically preventing long flow when an obstacle persists in the sensing range (e.g., a pot left in front of the window). Saves both property management and water bills, and avoids soaking the counter and cabinet.

### 2.5 Light and Appliance Interference Resistant, Stable in Kitchen

With a light source 100mm in front of the sensor at 45° obliquely above, sensing-distance error ≤10%; not disturbed by common appliances into false operation, and multiple whole units powered and sensing together don't false-trigger each other. Stable beside kitchen ceiling lights, disinfectors, and blenders.

### 2.6 500,000 Cycles + IP54, Rugged and Humid-resistant

Durability ≥500,000 cycles; protection IP54, splash- and oil-mist-proof. Inlet gradually raised to 1.9MPa closed for 60s, valve body and connections no deformation no leakage; water-tight performance repeatedly tested at 0.05MPa and 1.2MPa with no outlet leakage. Stable for long-term use in the kitchen's humid, oily environment.

### 2.7 Remote Distance Adjustment (Optional), Fits Different Sinks

An external remote adjusts upper/lower sensing distance, fitting sinks of different depths and installation heights. Power-loss protection is optional (not in prototype)—confirm configuration per project need during planning.

The GBL dual-mode withstands 1.9MPa, protection IP54, durability ≥500,000 cycles; at 30 uses per station per day a single unit's cycle life spans decades—in practice years without replacement. A 200-unit chain-restaurant project with return rate pressed down saves on-site labor, downtime, and reputation loss.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm water pressure 0.05~0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa add a booster pump
2. Confirm power method: adapter wiring or 4× AA batteries (wiring-free)
3. Keep the sensing window away from direct strong light and lamps; reserve unobstructed space in front
4. Shut off water and power before installation and maintenance

### 5.2 Precautions

- Each AC-powered unit sets an individual power switch and reliable grounding
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
- Power-loss protection is optional, not in prototype—confirm configuration per need during planning
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix the faucet body per drawing, connect inlet/outlet water pipes
2. Connect power (adapter or battery box), fix the control box
3. Open water and pressure-test, confirm no leakage at 0.05MPa / 1.2MPa
4. Power on, test water on and off in front of upper and lower sensing windows respectively
5. Fine-tune upper/lower sensing distance with remote (optional) as needed

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

After power-up the sensor enters standby and the LED low-voltage indicator logic takes effect; verify instant on/off and open/close toggle by placing a hand/object in the upper and lower sensing ranges. If battery below 4.5±5%, the LED flashes twice per second as a prompt.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Battery Replacement

Below 4.5±5% battery voltage the LED flashes a prompt and protection closes flow. To replace: shut off water → open battery box → replace 4× same-brand new alkaline batteries → re-seat and re-power. For long disuse, cut power to prevent battery leakage.

## 6. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Home Kitchen

Dual-mode sensing means no touching the faucet with wet or dirty hands while cooking; lower sensing rinses, upper sensing fills—easy for elderly and children; battery version needs no wiring, direct add-on in old houses.

### 6.2 Restaurant Back Kitchen / Central Kitchen

High-frequency hand washing and vegetable washing with hands often occupied; dual-mode non-contact water pickup reduces cross-contamination; 3-minute timeout prevents long flow, IP54 resists oil mist—easy to manage back-kitchen hygiene and cost.

### 6.3 Beverage Bar / Milk Tea Shop

Small bar space, dense operation; upper sensing wave open/close suits quick filling, lower sensing suits rinsing utensils; wiring-free battery version enables quick deployment in rented shops.

### 6.4 Hotel / Club Kitchen

High demand for hygiene image; dual-mode non-contact operation is dignified; 500,000-cycle durability endures high frequency, easy centralized deployment and O&M.

### 6.5 School / Vocational Training Kitchen

Students operate clumsily and use heavily; dual mode is fault-tolerant, timeout protection prevents waste; low-power prompt lets property staff replace batteries in time, no sudden water loss.

### 6.6 Outdoor / Temporary Food Stations

Battery power needs no wiring, quick build with mobile sinks; IP54 protection handles dusty, humid outdoor environments.
