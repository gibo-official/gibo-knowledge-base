## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6150 is GIBO's minimalist full-brass sensor basin faucet for modern minimalist bathrooms, design hotels, and high-end commercial complexes. Design is simplified to the extreme—the swan-neck shaped spout cleverly hides the sensor below the outlet; flow on reach creates a "magical" interaction; no exposed handles or knobs on the countertop, a single wipe cleans with no dead corners. The whole unit uses full brass chrome-plated material, with a warm texture and excellent durability; temperature is handled by an under-counter thermostatic valve, leaving only pure water output on the countertop—a benchmark of minimalist bathroom design. It supports DC battery and AC adapter dual power, flexibly configured per project.

Modern minimalist bathrooms, design hotels, and high-end commercial complexes—countertop cleanliness is itself part of the design language. The 6150 hides the sensor in the spout and moves temperature control under-counter, leaving only a swan-neck outlet on the countertop, achieving minimalism through "no controls" and experience through "flow on reach." ## II. Features

### 2.1 Single IR Outlet Sensing

The sensor window hidden below the spout covers about 15±1cm sensing distance; flow on reach, off on leave, fully non-contact.

### 2.2 Minimalist Spout Design

The swan-neck spout is the only visible countertop part, with no redundant lines or controls—a pure, integrated look.

### 2.3 Under-counter Thermostatic Temperature

Temperature is handled by an under-counter thermostatic valve; no temperature handle on the countertop, keeping only pure water output.

### 2.4 Dual-power AC/DC Switch

Supports DC6V battery and AC110~240V adapter dual power modes; auto-switches to DC backup when AC fails, ensuring uninterrupted use.

### 2.5 Timeout Water-stop Protection

Continuous use beyond the set duration auto forces shut-off, preventing waste from obstruction-caused continuous flow.

### 2.6 Low-battery Smart Prompt

The indicator blinks when power is low to prompt replacement; forces the solenoid valve closed when depleted, preventing continuous leaking from power loss.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole matches the faucet body and control box; prepare G1/2 inlet pipe and angle valve
- Confirm water pressure is within 0.05~0.7MPa and applicable water temperature 4~60℃
- Plan the under-counter thermostatic valve and power method (DC battery / AC adapter) in advance

### 5.2 Precautions

- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse directly with water or soak
- Wipe the spout surface with a soft cloth and neutral cleaner; no steel wool or strong acid/alkali cleaners
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries to ensure system reset

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Insert the full-brass chrome faucet body into the basin hole; fit washers in order and tighten the lock nut
2. Connect the outlet pipe to the control box OUTLET; connect the inlet pipe to INLET and then to the angle valve
3. Connect the signal wire to the matching control box port
4. Insert DC batteries or connect the AC power adapter
5. Install the under-counter thermostatic valve for hot/cold mixing; supply water and power and test sensing normal to complete installation

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After supplying water and power, test sensing normal; installation is complete once flow, thermostatic mixing, and signal connection are confirmed correct.

### 5.5 Maintenance / Battery Replacement

- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse directly with water or soak
- Wipe the spout surface with a soft cloth and neutral cleaner; no steel wool or strong acid/alkali cleaners
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries to ensure system reset
- Regularly check whether the inlet filter is clogged; remove and clean promptly to restore flow
