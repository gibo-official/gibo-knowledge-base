## 1. Product Introduction

The most unpleasant part of using the toilet in winter is that instant when you sit down—the cold seat touches skin and you shiver all over. GIBO's **Smart Heating Toilet Seat** is built exactly for this: the seat ring is made heatable, holding a constant 32~35℃ once powered, so it feels warm, not cold, when you sit—especially suited to public restrooms in hotels, hospitals, and offices that demand comfort, and to home scenarios where the elderly, pregnant women, and children fear the cold.

The seat has a built-in 12V/25W heating wire powered by a CCC-certified power adapter, which converts wall 100~240V mains to safe 12V DC before feeding the wire—fundamentally avoiding the electric-shock risk of 220V direct heating. Temperature is not blindly adjusted by a manual switch but monitored in real time by an NTC thermistor: it stops at 35℃, resumes below 31.5℃, cycling so the seat is always "warm" not "hot." A blue indicator at the rear base lights while heating and goes out when at temperature and on standby—status visible at a glance to O&M and cleaning staff.

It uses a PP plastic seat ring—non-toxic, odorless, heat- and flex-resistant—with stainless steel screws, 304 polished decorative caps, brass-nut expansion rubber fittings, and an eccentric adjustable base, withstanding 150KG sitting pressure long-term without deformation. The whole unit is IPX4 waterproof, 1800V dielectric strength and 500MΩ insulation, and endures -25℃~50℃ thermal cycles and 90% high humidity—stable for long-term use in humid restrooms. For engineering, wide-voltage input, three units on one wall without interference, and cutting the adapter power when unused make batch deployment essentially "install and forget."

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Auto Constant Temp, Seat Always Warm

The seat uses an NTC thermistor to watch the ring temperature in closed-loop control: powers on to heat, blue LED lights; stops heat at 35℃, blue LED off; resumes heating below 31.5℃, cycling. The specification recommends enabling heating when ambient ≤23℃, with the most comfortable seated temperature about 32.5℃—so in winter it is warm, not icy.

### 2.2 12V Safe-power Heating

The heating part is a 12V/25W heating wire, the whole seat powered by a CCC-certified adapter that first steps mains down to 12V DC. Even if the heating element anomalies, the voltage acting on the human body is safe—no electric-shock risk like 220V direct-heat pads.

### 2.3 Blue-light Indicator, Status at a Glance

A monochrome blue LED at the lower rear of the base: light on means the heater is powered and working; light off means at temperature, heating stopped, on standby. Cleaning and engineering staff can tell if it is working without opening the unit.

### 2.4 PP Seat, 150KG Load

The seat ring and lid use PP plastic—non-toxic, odorless, high surface gloss, heat- and flex-resistant, good hydrophilicity; mounting screws are stainless steel, decorative caps 304 polished stainless, plus brass-nut embedded expansion rubber fittings and a high-strength adjustable eccentric base with anti-slip pad. At normal sitting posture it bears 150KG long-term without deformation or creasing.

### 2.5 Wide-voltage Adaptable, Unfazed by Voltage Swings

Input supports 100~240V, 50/60Hz global voltage; performance is still met with rated voltage fluctuating ±10%. Whether domestic 220V or export projects, one adapter covers it—no separate model for different voltages.

### 2.6 Withstands Humidity and Thermal Cycling

Whole-unit waterproof rating IPX4 (oscillating tube 180° swing, continuous spray 10 min no ingress); type-tested through -25℃ (30min) → room temp (3min) → 50℃ (30min) thermal cycle 3 times, and -10℃~55℃, 90% RH temperature-humidity cycle 3 times, returning to normal function afterward.

### 2.7 Three Units on One Wall Without Interference

Three products installed on the same wall in use state at 60cm spacing for sensing tests showed no mutual interference or abnormal operation; daily use is also not disturbed by common appliances—suited to a row of adjacent stalls in public restrooms.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Measure the two mounting holes on the toilet; hole spacing should be 125mm, confirm fit if not
2. Shut off the local water supply and unplug the adapter power before starting
3. Prepare a Phillips screwdriver and a dry cloth (to wipe the mounting surface)
4. Take care not to chip the ceramic edge when removing the old seat

### 5.2 Precautions

- Must use the supplied CCC-certified power adapter; do not substitute a ordinary 220V direct-heat pad
- IPX4 is splash-proof not immersion-proof; do not soak the whole unit in the tank or basin
- For long disuse (off-season, long vacancy), cut the adapter power
- Keep the mounting surface dry and flat; use the eccentric adjustable base to align

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Remove the original seat, clean the mounting holes and ceramic surface
2. Insert the brass-nut embedded expansion rubber fitting from below into the holes
3. Align the seat to the holes and place it, fit the high-strength eccentric adjustable base and initially position
4. Tighten with stainless steel screws (decorative caps are 304 polished, snapped on last)
5. Mate the body lead (female end 200mm) with the adapter lead (1500mm) via the lock-card terminal fully
6. Plug in the adapter to power on, observe the blue indicator

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

After plugging in, the blue LED lights immediately and the heater powers on; the ring rises to 35℃ and stops heating, LED off; temperature drops below 31.5℃ and restarts, LED on, entering the constant-temp cycle. After new install, touch the ring with the back of the hand—feeling warmth within a few minutes is normal.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Handling |
|------|---------|------|
| No heat, LED off | Power supply / adapter output and connection / main board | Check socket and adapter first, then the mating terminal |
| Heats but LED off | LED / main board / lead | Check LED and lead continuity |
| LED on, no heat | LED / main board / heating wire, thermal fuse | Check heating wire and fuse |
| Constant temp fails | Main board | Control logic abnormal, return main board for repair |

## 6. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Hotels / Clubs

Guest-room and public-area toilets most fear the "icy-seat" bad review. The 32~35℃ constant-warm ring pulls the feel straight into the comfort zone; with 12V safe power it passes review easily—a low-cost differentiator for star hotels.

### 6.2 Hospitals / Elderly Care

The elderly, post-op patients, and pregnant women are cold-sensitive; an icy seat easily triggers discomfort. Auto constant temp + safe low voltage both improves comfort and holds the safety red line—fits aging-friendly retrofits and medical spaces.

### 6.3 Offices / Public Restrooms

Winter office-building restrooms see high frequency; ordinary seats are frozen for hours daily. Wide-voltage adapter, IPX4 waterproof, maintenance-free—property batches install and manages uniformly, one less worry on the O&M list.

### 6.4 Home / Smart Bathroom Upgrade

For those not wanting to replace the whole smart toilet, swapping in a heating seat is the lightest upgrade: PP seat, eccentric adjustable base fits most hole spacings, plug-and-use—enough for a quieter, more private space.
