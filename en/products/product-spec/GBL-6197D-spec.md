## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6197D is the kitchen-specific version within GIBO's "Xiaoxibao II Gen" series — a quick-install Sensor Spout that brings smart upgrades to an old kitchen. Its core idea is clever: instead of replacing the whole faucet, just unscrew the original aerator/spout and fit the Xiaoxibao, and the kitchen instantly gains touchless sensor flow.

The product uses a chrome-plated ABS cylindrical shell, with an M22 external-thread / M24 internal-thread universal threaded interface on top and an integrated black elongated infrared sensing window at the bottom. The overall shape is refined and compact, blending seamlessly with the original faucet after installation. It integrates LITEON Smart Dual-sensing Technology: reaching a hand downward triggers short-term flow for hand washing or drawing a small amount of water; sweeping the hand across the side sensing zone triggers a continuous always-on mode, suitable for scenarios requiring prolonged water use such as cooking and dishwashing.

For power, it adopts a rechargeable lithium battery design, conveniently charged via a Type-C USB port; one charge lasts up to 186 days. The full product reference price is approx. ¥147 (approx. $21), making it one of the most cost-effective kitchen smart-upgrade solutions on the market.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Dual Sensing Zones

- **Downward sensing zone**: Palm reaches from above toward below the spout → triggers short-term flow, stops when hand leaves. Suitable for hand washing, rinsing tableware, quick water draw.
- **Side sensing zone**: Hand quickly sweeps past the side → triggers continuous always-on flow; a second sweep closes it. Suitable for dishwashing, vegetable washing, filling pots and other prolonged water use.
- Dual safety protection: downward sensing auto-closes at 1 minute, side sensing auto-closes at 3 minutes, preventing waste from forgetting to turn off the water.

### 2.2 Neoperl Aerator

Built-in German Neoperl aerator delivers soft, splash-free flow with significant water savings. The constant-flow design ensures stable, consistent flow under different water pressures, achieving an actual water-saving efficiency ≥32%.

### 2.3 Blue LED Operating Indicator

When working, the LED emits a soft blue light to indicate status — both signaling the spout position and illuminating the water level at night for greater convenience.

### 2.4 Power-loss Protection Technology

When the battery is depleted or power is unexpectedly lost, the final operation ensures the shut-off action is executed, with no continuous leakage after power loss.

### 2.5 Grease-resistant, High-temperature-steam-resistant Sensing Window

The sensing window is specially optimized for the kitchen environment: the surface coating resists edible grease contamination and high-temperature steam. Daily grease can be wiped clean with a damp cloth without affecting sensor sensitivity.

### 2.6 Multiple Appearances Available

Two surface finishes are offered: Luxury Chrome and Pearl White. The chrome version blends seamlessly with most chrome-plated kitchen faucets; the white version is clean and elegant, suited to light-colored decor styles.

### 2.7 Optional Functions (some models)

Higher-tier versions can be equipped with temperature display or TDS water-quality value display; water temperature and TDS value can be switched via button for real-time awareness of water quality.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Check the existing faucet aerator thread specification (M18/M20/M22/M24 all adaptable; M22 external thread and M24 internal thread account for approx. 80% of the market)
- Confirm it is not a pull-out, extremely low-arc, or irregular non-standard spout faucet (these are not applicable)
- For first charge, recommend a full charge of 4+ hours to activate the battery

### 5.2 Precautions

- Not applicable to pull-out faucets, extremely low-arc faucets, and irregular non-standard spout faucets
- After installation, check for leaks; if any, re-tighten or replace the seal ring
- For first charge, recommend a full charge of 4+ hours to activate the battery
- If unused for a long time, charge once every 3 months to protect the battery

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Confirm compatibility: check the existing faucet aerator thread specification (M18/M20/M22/M24 all adaptable)
2. Remove old spout: unscrew the original faucet aerator/spout counterclockwise
3. Select fitting: choose the M22 external-thread or M24 internal-thread fitting according to the original faucet thread type
4. Install Xiaoxibao: screw the selected fitting into the faucet thread, then align the Xiaoxibao body with the fitting and press gently until locked
5. Test: before first use, recommend a full charge, then test downward sensing and side sensing separately for normal operation

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

Before first use, recommend a full charge, then test downward sensing (flow on reach, stop on withdraw) and side sensing (hand-wave to switch always-on/off) separately for normal operation; put into use after confirming correct.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- When the sensing window has grease, wipe with a damp cloth; do not use steel wool or organic solvents
- If sensing becomes insensitive, check battery level (abnormal LED blinking frequency may indicate under-voltage)
- If the aerator flows poorly, remove and clean the filter screen
- If unused for a long time, charge once every 3 months to protect the battery
