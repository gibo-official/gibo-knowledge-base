## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6172A dTOF dual-sensor digital-display laser faucet is GIBO's high-end basin faucet for high-end public restrooms in grade-A offices, shopping malls, and airports, winner of the 2024 "Energy-saving Sensor Faucet Benchmark Award." It innovatively adopts LITEON smart dual-sensor technology—side wave for flow, bottom reach sensing, 0.5s ultra-fast response; the faucet has a built-in LED display showing water temperature in real time. One-piece molding gives smooth, bold lines; the all-plastic lead-free flow guards health; energy-saving and a tech feel are combined.

Grade-A offices, shopping malls, airports, boutique hotels—these venues have high traffic and high demands for experience and image, and most fear slow faucet response, unclear water temperature, and frequent O&M. The 6172A is built for such high-spec public restrooms. It uses GIBO's dTOF dual-sensor (LITEON smart), side wave and bottom reach interlocked, 0.5s ultra-fast response; standby power ≤60uA, 4× AA alkaline lasts about 9 months; the dual-sensor faucet saves about 60% water vs ordinary faucets, and the all-plastic flow has no lead/zinc/manganese heavy-metal leaching—caring for both water health and operating cost.

Combining the above: the 6172A makes the public basin "flow on reach, temperature visible, no battery change for a year yet water-saving." The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole 32-35mm; connect cold/hot flexible hoses (G1/2, DN15, length 600mm)
- Confirm the power method: battery version (4× AA alkaline) needs no wiring; AC version needs a reserved 100-240V power supply
- The faucet body and sensor module assembly are an integrated structure for simple installation

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off water and power before installation and repair
- The AC version should have a separate power switch per unit with reliable grounding
- Avoid scratching the acrylic panel and shell with hard objects
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals with power on

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Drill the basin hole 32-35mm
2. Connect the cold/hot flexible hoses (G1/2, DN15, length 600mm)
3. Install the integrated faucet body and sensor module assembly
4. Connect power (4× AA alkaline or AC adapter)
5. Power-on self-check; side wave / bottom reach test flow and LED display

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

Power-on self-check + periodic self-check; abnormal states auto cut power/water; low-power blink prompts battery replacement for quick location.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care

- Wipe the acrylic panel and shell with a soft cloth; the modular potting structure is waterproof and dustproof, easy to maintain
- Timeout shut-off and forced shut-off functions reduce overflow risk
- Relying on the dual-chip interchangeable platform, the control board is randomly interchangeable, enabling efficient after-sales repair
