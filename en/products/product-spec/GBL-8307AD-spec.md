## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-8307AD is a single-button surface-mounted integrated sensor flush valve launched by GIBO for commercial restrooms and public toilets, as well as renovation of older projects. Its biggest highlight is the **single-button manual override**: a reserved mechanical button on the panel resists false triggering and ensures immediate response in emergencies — when power is lost or sensing fails, a press still flushes. The integrated design requires no separate control box; just connect the water pipe and it is ready to use, greatly simplifying installation and reducing the failure points of scattered components.

The product adopts GIBO's mature sensor solution with **intelligent compensation** — automatically adapting to ambient light and water-pressure changes to keep sensing stable. Combined with the slow-close structure and IPX7 triple waterproof, it is durable and reliable, suited to high-frequency commercial and public scenarios.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Single-button Manual Override

A reserved mechanical button on the panel resists false triggering and ensures immediate response in emergencies; when power is lost or sensing fails, a press still flushes, guaranteeing basic functionality.

### 2.2 Integrated Surface-mounted Design

The valve body and control module are integrated into one; no separate control box is needed. Surface-mounted installation exposes all components, making inspection and repair convenient and lowering the failure points of scattered components.

### 2.3 Intelligent Compensation

The built-in algorithm automatically adapts to ambient light and water-pressure changes, keeping sensing stable and reducing missed or false triggers from environmental fluctuations.

### 2.4 Slow-close Shock Absorption

The valve closes in stages, slowly released, absorbing water-hammer energy and reducing noise, protecting the pipeline system.

### 2.5 IPX7 Triple Waterproof

The module adopts triple waterproof treatment, reaching IPX7 Waterproof (national standard IPX6), adapting to humid restroom environments.

### 2.6 Anti-odor Flush

Supports an automatic anti-odor flush once every 24 hours, keeping the trap sealed and suppressing odors.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction match the product
- Confirm the power supply method (DC battery / AC); for first use, recommend a full charge (battery version)
- Confirm the reserved pipe diameter matches (subject to official materials)

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- The sensing window must be kept clean to avoid affecting sensitivity
- Do not use steel wool on the stainless steel panel

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction
2. Mount the surface-mounted integrated body and connect the water inlet/outlet pipes, checking for leaks
3. Install the battery or connect AC power
4. Power on, then test sensor auto flush and single-button manual override
5. Confirm anti-odor flush and slow-close operation are normal

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test sensor auto flush (triggered on departure) and single-button manual override; confirm the sensing window responds normally and the manual button flushes reliably.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Keep the sensing window clean; avoid dust/buildup affecting sensitivity
- When the indicator blinks abnormally, charge / replace the battery promptly
- For high-frequency-use locations, recommend checking the solenoid valve filter periodically
