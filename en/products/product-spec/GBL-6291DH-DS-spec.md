## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6291DH-DS is an **economy-type ABS surface-mounted urinal Sensor Flush Valve** developed by GIBO for old public-restroom renovations, belonging to the economy-type retrofit solution of the fourth-tier renovation series, mainly targeting old shopping malls, schools, parks, and public restrooms. The product is designed specifically for the retrofit of traditional manual push delayed-action valves: mainstream manual push valve hole spacings were surveyed at the R&D stage, achieving **direct removal of the old unit and replacement with the new one without modifying wall water pipes**. The ABS engineering-plastic body is rust-free, corrosion-resistant, flexible, and not easily cracked; the surface-mounted structure exposes all components, making daily inspection and repair extremely convenient.

After a person leaves the sensing zone, flushing is automatically triggered — the whole process is touch-free and maintenance-free, effectively reducing water waste and cross-infection risk in public restrooms, making it a cost-effective choice for low-cost smart renovation of public toilets. Battery direct supply or optional AC power allows old public toilets with no reserved power to upgrade with zero wiring; combining "damage-free old renovation, maintenance-free, wiring-free" is the most tangible value this product delivers to property managers.

### 2.4 Pipe-modification-free Installation

Compatible with mainstream manual push delayed-action valve hole spacings; directly replace the old valve without modifying the original wall water pipes.

### 2.5 Anti-false-trigger Smart Delay

A person walking continuously within the sensing zone does not falsely trigger; flushing logic starts only after departure is confirmed, avoiding disturbance and wasted water.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the original manual push delayed-action valve hole spacing is compatible with GBL-6291DH-DS, requiring no modification of wall water pipes or tiles
- Verify the GBL-6291DH / GBL-6291DS model (water-inlet method and left/right version subject to official materials)
- Confirm DC battery power or AC power (AC voltage specification to be verified)

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; recommend periodic preventive replacement (model and quantity to be verified)
- Do not clean the ABS body with steel wool; keep the sensing window transparent and clean to avoid affecting sensitivity

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Confirm the original manual push delayed-action valve hole spacing matches
2. Remove the old valve parts
3. Install the GBL-6291DH/DS surface-mounted body and connect the water inlet/outlet pipes
4. Install batteries or connect AC power
5. Power on and test flushing

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test sensor flushing: a timer starts when a person enters the sensing zone; automatic flushing triggered after departure indicates normal operation.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Battery replacement: replace alkaline batteries per configuration (model and quantity to be verified); recommend periodic preventive replacement
- Cleaning: wipe the ABS body with a neutral cleaner, no steel wool; keep the sensing window transparent and clean to avoid affecting sensitivity
- Common fault troubleshooting: no flush → check battery level → check solenoid coil continuity → check control-module signal; continuous running water → check whether solenoid is stuck → check whether seal ring is aged; sluggish sensing → clean sensing window → check whether battery voltage is insufficient
