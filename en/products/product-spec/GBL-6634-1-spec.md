## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL-6634-1 Sensor Soap Dispenser + Knee Control** adds a knee-control switch on top of the 6634's infrared sensor soap dispensing—when hands are occupied, a knee nudge dispenses; when hands are free, a reach dispenses; the two triggers switch by scenario. It targets back kitchens, workshops, and medical operating rooms where "hands are often occupied yet soap must be available at any time."

In back-kitchen prep, hospital handling, food processing, and lab operations, hands are either greasy or gloved—pressing the sensing window or a press bottle is awkward and risks secondary contamination. The 6634-1 offers both "reach to dispense" and "knee-tap to dispense," whichever is convenient. It is likewise non-contact, foam-output, 1.2ml precise dose, circuit standby ≤0.2mW, stays closed on power loss with no leakage, and protection reaches IPX5 plus module submersion/boiling without failure. For back kitchens and clean rooms, the 6634-1 is the soap solution that "is reachable even in a flurry."

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Sensing, Dispense at Reach

When a hand enters the sensing range, the red LED lights for 1s and the liquid pump dispenses for 2s±0.5s, response ≤1s. Smoothest when hands are free—zero-contact soap pickup, avoiding cross-contamination from repeatedly touched soap bottles.

### 2.2 Knee-control Switch, Dispense With Hands Occupied

A knee or leg nudge on the knee-control switch actuates the motor to dispense; it stops when the switch pops out. During prep, carrying, or gloving when hands are unavailable, a knee tap dispenses soap—no hand shaking, no asking for help, no break in aseptic rhythm.

### 2.3 Dual Triggers in Parallel, No Conflict

IR sensing and knee-control switch are two independent trigger logics; whichever triggers dispenses, and knee-control stops on pop-out. The two modes switch freely without false shutdown or signal contention—stable even in busy back-kitchen peaks.

### 2.4 Precise Adjustable Dose, Works for Thin or Thick

Factory dispense time 2s, about 1.2ml each, error no more than ±15% of nominal. The remote adjusts in 0.5s steps between 0.5~5s, fitting different soap viscosities. Back-kitchen and medical scenarios vary widely in usage—one step adjustment suffices.

### 2.5 Low-power Long Life, Must-close on Power Loss, No Leak

Standby power ≤0.2mW, operating energy ≤2W, long life on 4× AA alkaline batteries. On power loss it stays closed whether dispensing or standby—no pump-running leakage soaking the counter; below 4.5±0.1V the LED flashes four times per second to prompt replacement.

### 2.6 Triple Waterproof, Withstands Humid Steam

Whole-unit IPX5; the sensing window and sealed compound parts individually submerged 20cm/4h show no water droplets or fog and function normally; they can also be boiled 70℃ 0.5h and work after cooling. Long-term use in hot, humid, steamy back-kitchen environments won't cause water ingress failure.

### 2.7 Large-capacity Bottle, Fits Various Soaps

Bottles 500ml / 1500ml; high-frequency venues use the large bottle for fewer refills. Fits foam hand soap, and supports dish soap mixed with water at 1:4—flexible and low-cost procurement and replacement.

In a 200-unit chain-restaurant project, lowering the leakage return rate saves parts money, on-site labor, and food-safety complaint risk.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm mounting positions for body and knee-control switch: body on counter/wall within hand reach, knee-control switch at a convenient knee height
2. Confirm use of foam hand soap, or pre-mix dish-soap water solution at 1:4
3. Choose bottle capacity (500ml or 1500ml) and prepare the corresponding soap bottle
4. Disconnect power before installation and maintenance

### 5.2 Precautions

- Secure the knee-control switch extension wiring (V1.1 update item) to avoid false triggering from kicks
- Each AC-powered unit sets an individual power switch and reliable grounding
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix the body, bottle, and knee-control switch per drawing; connect power (battery box or adapter) and knee-control wiring
2. Fill soap, re-seat the bottle, confirm line connectivity
3. Power on; within 4s the LED flashes 3 times (0.5s frequency) to enter normal mode
4. Extend hand in front of the sensing window to confirm 2s dispense; press the knee-control switch once to confirm dispense, pop out to confirm stop
5. Use the remote to fine-tune sensing distance and dispense time to a suitable step

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

Within 4s of power-up the LED indicator flashes 3 times (0.5s flash frequency), then enters normal mode. If either IR or knee-control triggers abnormally, check sensing-window obstruction, knee-control wiring, and battery polarity.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Battery Replacement

When battery voltage drops below 4.5±0.1V the LED flashes four times per second as a prompt. To replace: disconnect power → open battery box → replace 4× same-brand new alkaline batteries → re-seat and power on for self-check. Periodically check the knee-control switch rebound is smooth; refill the bottle promptly when soap is nearly out to avoid idling.

## 6. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Restaurant Back Kitchen / Central Kitchen

Hands are often occupied with prep and carrying; the 6634-1's knee control lets chefs pick up soap without shaking hands; IPX5 plus boil resistance stays stable in hot, humid, steamy environments, and soap viscosity is adjustable to fit dish-soap dilution.

### 6.2 Food Processing / Clean Room

Aseptic operation requires hands to touch as little as possible; knee control provides a non-hand-contact soap path, paired with IR dispense-at-reach—both hygienic and without breaking operational rhythm.

### 6.3 Hospital Handling / Lab Departments

Medical staff often wear gloves or hold instruments; knee control fills the soap gap when "hands are unavailable"; stays closed on power loss, no leakage contamination when unattended.

### 6.4 Laboratory / R&D Benches

During experiments hands are occupied and cross-contamination is taboo; the 6634-1's dual trigger keeps soap pickup without interrupting work; 1.2ml precise dose gives controllable, predictable usage.

### 6.5 Office / Commercial Restrooms

Ordinary hand washing uses IR dispense-at-reach; occasionally with hands full of heavy items use knee control—both habits covered; large bottle reduces refill frequency, easy for property staff.

### 6.6 Schools / Vocational Training Kitchens

Students operate clumsily and use heavily; the 6634-1's dual mode is fault-tolerant and its 200,000-cycle life endures; the low-power prompt light lets property staff replace batteries in time.
