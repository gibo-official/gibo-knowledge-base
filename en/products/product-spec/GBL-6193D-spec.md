## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL-6193D** is a split-type sensor spout from GIBO's "Mini-Wash" series, built around one word—"small." At just 92mm×70mm×73mm, it sits directly on the basin outlet, taking no space and fitting any basin. It differs from an all-in-one sensor faucet in form: the faucet body can stay minimal while sensing and flow-control modules are concentrated in this small spout module, so old basins, irregular basins, and cramped wash points can all be equipped.

It uses "spout sensing + side sensing" dual-zone control: spout sensing (water on when hands arrive, off when leaving) handles quick hand washing, while side sensing (one trigger to open, another to close) handles scenarios needing continuous flow, such as filling or washing rags. One device covers both usages—fitting public lavatories, back-kitchen wash areas, and small wash stations alike.

Powered by 3× AA alkaline batteries, standby current is pressed below 60μA and whole-unit energy ≤0.2mW, enabling wiring-free installation even where no power is pre-installed. At low voltage the LED fast-flashes 5 times to prompt replacement; below 3.6±0.1V flow is cut off directly, so it never runs until fully dead. Multiple units installed side by side do not falsely trigger each other, and common luminaires at 1m 45° oblique illumination cause distance drift below ±10%—stable even in heavily lit lavatories. Service life is rated 500,000 cycles, withstanding high-frequency commercial use.

Bringing the above together: the GBL-6193D brings dual-zone non-contact washing to "small basins, old basins, and narrow spaces"—it fits, lasts, and is not picky about power. The three points below are the product values we believe are most worth telling.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Dual-zone Sensing, Both Usages Handy

Spout sensing: hand enters the sensing range to start flow (response ≤1.0s) with one indicator flash, leaves to stop (≤1.0s); continuous flow beyond 180±10s auto-stops. Side sensing: hand enters to start (≤1.0s), re-enters to stop (≤1.0s); likewise auto-stops beyond 180±10s. Use the underside for quick washing and the side for continuous flow—one spout covers two scenarios.

### 2.2 Ultra-low Standby, Battery No Wiring

Standby current ≤60μA; per CJ/T 194-2014 measurement, whole-unit energy ≤0.2mW (DC4.5V). Powered by 3× AA alkaline batteries—old restrooms without pre-installed power and mobile wash points install wiring-free, essentially maintenance-free day to day.

### 2.3 Low-voltage Alarm, Never Runs to Death

Operating voltage dropping to 3.6±0.1V enters the low-voltage alarm; each sensing LED fast-flashes 5 times to prompt replacement; below that, flow is cut off to avoid stopping only when fully dead. Voltage adaptability 3.6V~4.5V.

### 2.4 Stable Distance, Same in Winter/Summer and New/Old Batteries

From voltage 4.5V down to 3.6V and from -10℃ up to +55℃, sensing-distance variation stays within ±10%. Installed across seasons and battery states, the sensing range remains stable—no over-sensitive in summer and dead in winter.

### 2.5 No Crosstalk in Rows, Stable Under Light

Multiple units powered on together produce no false operation; incandescent, T5, fluorescent, and LED lights at 45° oblique illumination beyond 1m cause control-distance variation of less than ±10%; compliant with burst interference test and ESD interference test at Class A. A row of Mini-Wash units side by side in a brightly lit lavatory each behaves properly.

### 2.6 Small Size, Fits Old Basins Too

Standard size is only 92mm (L) × 70mm (W) × 73mm (H), connection thread internal M22×1, external M24×1, fitting the vast majority of standard basin outlets. Simply avoid direct strong light and mirror reflection during controller installation—no constraint on countertop space.

### 2.7 Vibration & Shock Resistant, Securely Mounted

Vibration resistance: amplitude 0.35mm, frequency 10~55Hz, three mutually perpendicular axes swept 10 cycles, appearance and performance meet requirements; G1/2 thread withstands 20N·m torque held 60±5s with no cracks, no damage, no thread stripping. Withstands transport jolts and daily knocks.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, washing away sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid
2. Confirm water pressure 0.05MPa~0.8MPa; below 0.05MPa add a booster pump
3. Confirm the basin outlet thread is M22×1 / M24×1
4. Keep the sensing window away from direct strong light and mirror reflection

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off water and power before installation and maintenance
- Use same-brand high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals
- Avoid direct strong light and mirror reflection in the installation environment

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fit the adapter (M22×1 / M24×1) on the basin outlet and fix the spout body
2. Open water and pressure-test, confirm no leakage at 0.05/1.0MPa
3. Insert 3× AA alkaline batteries, install the sensing panel
4. On power-up the indicator enters standby; test dual-zone sensing per the function guide
5. Extend hand under and beside respectively to test spout sensing and side sensing

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

After inserting batteries the spout enters normal operation; on first sensing the indicator flashes once to signal a trigger. Under low voltage the LED fast-flashes 5 times as a prompt. After installation, verify both spout sensing and side sensing modes one by one.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement

When operating voltage drops to 3.6±0.1V, each sensing LED fast-flashes 5 times to prompt battery replacement and cuts off flow. To replace: shut off water → open panel → take out battery box and replace 3× same-brand new alkaline batteries → re-seat and power on again.

## 6. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Small Public Venues / Community Stations

Limited space and small basins where an all-in-one faucet won't fit. The Mini-Wash sits on the outlet, taking no counter space; battery no-wiring enables rapid deployment at community stations, nucleic-acid points, and convenience-service wash stations—temporary or simple locations.

### 6.2 Back Kitchen / Food Workshop Wash Areas

Dual-zone sensing covers both quick rinsing and filling/cleaning; non-contact reduces hand cross-contamination; small size fits stainless steel sinks without getting in the way.

### 6.3 School / Office-floor Lavatories

A row of Mini-Wash units side by side produce no false operation; battery solution needs no wiring, ideal for old-building retrofit without pre-installed power; 500,000-cycle life withstands class-break and commute peaks.

### 6.4 Hospitals / Waiting Areas

Touch-free sensing meets infection control; dual mode fits different hand-washing motions; undervoltage prompts before cutting off, no mid-way water loss.

### 6.5 Hotels / Guesthouses

Concealed or exposed, both compact and unobtrusive against minimalist decor; Sustained-flow mode lets guests rinse and fill conveniently; low standby power means the battery solution is worry-free even in guest-room areas.
