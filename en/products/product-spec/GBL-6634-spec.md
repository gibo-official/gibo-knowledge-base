## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL-6634 Sensor Soap Dispenser** mounts on the counter or wall; extend a hand and a puff of foam hand soap is automatically squeezed out, retract the hand and it stops—no touching the body throughout. It solves the old problem of soap bottles in public restrooms—"everyone touches them, they get dirtier with use, and often go missing"—while also removing the contact step of "press once, smear bacteria on the hand."

Office restrooms, mall wash areas, hotel rooms, restaurant back kitchens, hospital outpatient—wherever people wash hands, non-contact soap dispensing is both more hygienic and more dignified than a press bottle. The 6634 dispenses foam: one pump is an even puff, saving versus liquid and more even than pressing, acceptable to children and the particular alike.

It uses active infrared sensing plus GIBO's Smart Sensing algorithm (core tech #7): when a hand enters the sensing range, the red LED lights for 1s and the liquid pump dispenses for 2s, with response ≤1s. Factory dispense volume is 1.2ml; the remote can adjust from 0.5~5s in 0.5s steps to fit different soap viscosities. Bottles come in 500ml and 1500ml; high-frequency venues use the large bottle for fewer refills. Circuit standby power ≤0.2mW, 4× AA batteries last long; on power loss it stays closed whether dispensing or standby—no leakage. For property management, the 6634 is the kind of soap device that "once installed, needs no constant watching."

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Sensing, Dispense at Reach

When a hand enters the sensing range, the red LED indicator lights for 1s and the liquid pump dispenses for 2s±0.5s, response time ≤1s. No touching the body throughout, avoiding cross-contamination from repeatedly touched soap bottles, and suited to scenarios where hands are dirty and inconvenient to press.

### 2.2 Precise Adjustable Dose, Works for Thin or Thick

Factory dispense time 2s, about 1.2ml each, error no more than ±15% of nominal. The remote adjusts in 0.5s steps between 0.5~5s, fitting different soap viscosities—shorter for thin, longer for thick—neither wasteful nor insufficient. Especially useful in back kitchens and hospitals where usage varies widely.

### 2.3 Low-power Long Life, Hassle-free Batteries

Standby power ≤0.2mW, operating energy ≤2W, dispensing no-load current ≤260mA, loaded ≤360mA. Powered by 4× AA alkaline batteries, it lasts long in daily public scenarios without frequent cover opening for replacement.

### 2.4 Power-loss Protection, Absolutely No Leakage

Cutting power during soap dispensing keeps the dispenser closed; cutting power when not dispensing also keeps it closed. No matter when power drops, there is no "pump still running, soap flowing" leakage embarrassment—walls and counters won't be soaked.

### 2.5 Low-power Prompt, Timely Replacement

When battery voltage drops below 4.5±0.1V, the LED indicator flashes at a rate of four times per second to signal low battery; it flashes continuously while sensing and stops when leaving. Property staff know at a glance to replace batteries, with no sudden stop mid-use.

### 2.6 Triple Waterproof, Withstands Humid Environments

Whole-unit waterproof rating IPX5; the sensing window and sealed compound parts individually submerged 20cm for 4 hours show no water droplets or fog and function normally; they can also be placed in 70℃ boiling water for 0.5h and still work after cooling. Long-term use in high-humidity, steamy restroom environments won't cause water ingress failure.

### 2.7 Large-capacity Bottle, Fits Various Soaps

Bottles come in 500ml and 1500ml; high-frequency venues use the 1500ml large bottle to reduce refill frequency. Fits foam hand soap, and supports dish soap mixed with water at 1:4—flexible and low-cost procurement and replacement.

In a 200-unit project, lowering the leakage return rate saves not just parts money but also on-site repair labor and reputation loss.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm a flat mounting surface that can fix the bottle and body, with wiring space reserved on counter or wall
2. Confirm use of foam hand soap, or pre-mix by ratio (dish soap : water = 1:4)
3. Choose bottle capacity (500ml or 1500ml) and prepare the corresponding soap bottle
4. Disconnect power before installation and maintenance

### 5.2 Precautions

- Each AC-powered unit sets an individual power switch and reliable grounding
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals
- On first use confirm soap is filled and the line has no air bubbles, to avoid idling

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix the body and bottle per drawing, connect power (battery box or adapter)
2. Fill soap, re-seat the bottle, confirm line connectivity
3. Power on; within 4s the LED flashes 3 times (0.5s frequency) to enter normal mode
4. Extend hand in front of the sensing window, confirm LED lights 1s and dispenses 2s
5. Use the remote to fine-tune sensing distance and dispense time to a suitable step

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

Within 4s of power-up the LED indicator flashes 3 times (0.5s flash frequency), then enters normal mode. If no flash appears or frequent false dispensing occurs, check whether the sensing window is obstructed and whether battery polarity is correct.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Battery Replacement

When battery voltage drops below 4.5±0.1V the LED flashes four times per second as a prompt. To replace: disconnect power → open battery box → replace 4× same-brand new alkaline batteries → re-seat and power on for self-check. Refill the bottle promptly when soap is nearly out, to avoid idling damage to the pump.
