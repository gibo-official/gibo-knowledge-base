## 1. Product Introduction

**dTOF Laser Sensing Module** is GIBO's TOF (Time-of-Flight) laser sensing probe developed for pull-out kitchen faucets and basin faucets. Paired with the existing 9160 control box, it delivers water the moment a hand approaches and stops flow when the hand retreats—fully touchless throughout. Faucet OEMs use it as the "sensing eye": drop it into a kitchen faucet and you get a laser pull-out faucet; connect it to a basin faucet and you get a laser sensing faucet; it also works for instant-heating faucets and kitchen/bath sinks. It solves the two most troublesome pain points of ordinary infrared probes at the sink edge—false triggers or failures caused by mirror-finish stainless steel and glossy countertop reflections, and the one-to-one pairing of probe and main control board that requires re-calibration whenever a unit is replaced.

This module uses dTOF laser ranging (Core Technology #2, Low-power dTOF Laser Ultra-sensing Technology), directly measuring the time-of-flight of the laser bouncing off an object to determine distance, with a theoretical sensing-window response time of 250ms. Across different reflective surfaces such as mirrors, glossy stainless steel plates, whiteboards, and blackboards, the sensing-distance deviation stays within 15%; its ingress protection reaches IP67, with no water ingress after 24 hours immersion in 25℃ water and a sensing-distance change of no more than 10% after 0.5 hour boiling in 75℃ water. What gives OEMs the most peace of mind is interchangeability: any sensing probe and under-counter main control box can be paired at random, and the sensing distance always meets factory settings—no per-unit calibration required.

Standby power consumption ≤260μA, powered by either DC6V four alkaline batteries or an AC110~220V-to-6V1A adapter; on power-up the solenoid valve is normally closed, the sensing LED lights for 15 seconds then enters standby, the product's average service life is 10 years, and the electronic assembly carries a 5-year warranty. The three points below are the product values we believe are most worth highlighting.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 dTOF Laser Ranging—No Picky Reflective Surfaces

Directly measures photon time-of-flight. Across different reflective surfaces such as mirrors, glossy stainless steel, whiteboards, and blackboards, the sensing-distance deviation stays within 15%, so mirror finishes and glossy countertops at the sink edge no longer cause sensing failures or false triggers.

### 2.2 15-Second Learning on Power-Up, Normally-Closed Start

Each time power is applied, the solenoid valve is in the normally-closed state and the sensing LED stays lit for 15 seconds (no sensing during this period), then enters sensing standby after 15 seconds. A safe start avoids accidental water flow at the instant of power-up.

### 2.3 Re-sensing Toggle, Cyclic Flow

When the sensing window detects a reference object the solenoid valve opens; when it detects again the solenoid valve closes—and so the cycle continues: extend the hand to release water, retract to stop, extend again to release again.

### 2.4 Random Probe–Mainboard Interchangeability

The sensing probe and the under-counter main control box can be randomly combined in any pairing, and the sensing distance always meets factory settings with normal function. OEMs no longer need one-to-one probe–mainboard pairing, saving a process step on both the production line and in after-sales service.

### 2.5 IP67 Fully Sealed, Waterproof

The module's overall ingress protection is IP67. After 24 hours immersion in 25℃ water there is no seepage; after 0.5 hour boiling in 75℃ water and cooling, the sensing-distance change does not exceed 10%. Sink splashes and steam environments pose no problem for long-term use.

### 2.6 Valve Closes Within 1 Second of Power Loss + Reverse-Polarity Protection

Regardless of battery or adapter power, the solenoid valve must be in the closed state within 1 second after power loss; reversing the power and solenoid wires causes no heating or other faults, so even careless wiring will not burn the board.

### 2.7 Low-Battery Alert and 4-Minute Auto Shutoff

When the battery voltage drops below 4.5V, the solenoid valve closes and the LED indicator flashes 10 times (1s interval) as an alert; when the faucet is activated by sensing, the valve auto-closes after 4 minutes and can be reopened by sensing again, eliminating continuous running water.

## 3. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: dTOF Laser—Stable on Both Light and Dark Reflective Surfaces — Mirror Stainless Steel and Blackboard Deviation Both Under 15%, 250ms Response

IR probes at the sink edge most easily fail on reflective surfaces: mirror-finish stainless steel bounces light straight back, and glossy countertops make the sensing distance drift, resulting in no flow when there should be, and false flow when there shouldn't. The dTOF module directly measures time-of-flight, keeping the distance deviation across different reflective surfaces within 15%, with a response of just 250ms. OEMs making pull-out kitchen faucets or stainless-steel-sink sensor faucets need no separate tuning for countertop material; end users wash hands and dishes at the sink with ease.

### Selling Point 2: Random Interchangeability, Calibration-Free at Factory — Probe and Main Control Box in Any Combination Hold the Same Distance, Eliminating Production-Line Calibration

Traditional sensing probes and main control boxes are often paired one-to-one; replacing one unit requires re-calibration, which is a hassle for the production line and forces after-sales spare parts to match specific models. The dTOF module's probe and under-counter main control box can be randomly paired in any combination, and the sensing distance always meets factory settings. An OEM can stock one probe part number and one main control box part number to fulfill orders, streamlining both inventory and spare parts; field service can swap components at will without re-calibration, shortening on-site resolution time.

### Selling Point 3: IP67 Defies Moisture, 10-Year Life — Survives 24h Immersion / 0.5h Boiling, 10-Year Average Life, 5-Year Warranty

The module is IP67 overall—no seepage after 24 hours in water, and sensing distance barely changes after boiling in 75℃ water—withstanding sink splashes, steam, and accidental immersion. With a 10-year average product life and a 5-year electronic-assembly warranty, it runs maintenance-free long-term even under high-frequency commercial use. For a project managing dozens of water points, replacing modules less often and suffering fewer water-shutoff repairs translates directly into real O&M savings.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm power is DC6.0V (battery box) or AC110~220V-to-6V1A adapter, and matches the 9160 control box
2. Orient the sensing window toward the usage direction; keep no obstruction larger than 1cm within about 18cm directly in front
3. Do not aim the sensing window directly at sunlight or strong lamps
4. Verify probe and main control box wire sequence and terminals; confirm correct polarity of power and solenoid wires

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always cut water and power before installing or servicing
- Pay attention to reverse-polarity protection for power and solenoid wires (reverse connection causes no damage but correct wiring is required for operation)
- Each AC-powered unit must have its own power switch and reliable grounding
- Do not hot-plug terminals
- Probe and main control box can be randomly interchanged, but during assembly the latch must be fully seated and the sensing window must face the usage direction

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Assemble the sensor faucet body and fix the dTOF probe at the sensing-window position
2. Connect the 9160 under-counter control box to the solenoid valve drive wire and power wire
3. Power on; observe the solenoid valve normally closed, sensing LED lit for 15 seconds, then enter standby
4. Run a 12-hour water test with a kitchen/bath sink to confirm no self-activation and no false triggers from adjacent walls
5. Hand test: sense to open valve, sense again to close valve; normal cycling

### 5.4 Power-on Self-Check

Each time power is applied, the solenoid valve is normally closed and the sensing LED lights for 15 seconds (no sensing function during this period), then enters normal sensing standby. The installation adaptability test requires 12 hours of powered operation with a sink attached, with no self-activation.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Battery Replacement

When the battery voltage drops below 4.5V, the solenoid valve closes and the LED flashes 10 times as an alert. Battery replacement: cut water supply → remove battery box and replace with 4 new alkaline batteries of the same brand → reassemble and power on for self-check.
