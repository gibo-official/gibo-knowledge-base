## I. Product Introduction

The BC-KZH-9168 is the "water-path actuator" of a basin sensor faucet, integrating a pulse solenoid valve, valve-body flow channel, and sealing structure. It receives the signal from the sensor module and opens and closes water within milliseconds, long-term withstanding the pressure, temperature, and quality of the water in the pipe. Simply put, the sensor module is responsible for "thinking" while the control box is responsible for "acting."

It pairs with the GBL-9166 basin sensor module to form a complete basin sensor water-outlet system. Externally, it is a core component for complete-unit manufacturers and ODM customers, installed under the counter or in a pre-embedded box—invisible to the user, yet the stability and leak-tightness of the faucet depend largely on it.

What a control box competes on is "ruggedness": the 9168 achieves a burst pressure of 2.5MPa (about 25 times standard water pressure), still delivers ≥8L/min at a dynamic pressure of 0.1MPa, and has a service life of over 500,000 cycles. The valve-body flow channel features low-water-hammer and self-cleaning anti-clogging treatment—no pipe slamming on close, and no easy clogging even in complex water quality.

For complete-unit manufacturers, a control box with sufficient pressure resistance, long life, and standard interfaces means shorter integration cycles and lower after-sales rates. The three points below are what we believe are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Pulse Solenoid Valve, Millisecond Switching

Solenoid parameters: DC 4.5V / 20ms short-pulse drive. Upon receiving the sensor module signal, the valve core actuates and releases within milliseconds—opening within 1s, closing within 1.5s (with sensor module)—with extremely low energy consumption.

### 2.2 High-Flow Water Path, Good Output Even at Low Pressure

Flow ≥8L/min at dynamic pressure 0.1MPa, abundant hand-washing water without weak low-pressure output. The valve-body flow channel is fluid-optimized, balancing flow and low water hammer.

### 2.3 Fully Sealed Pressure Resistance, Multi-Level Leak Protection

Verified from 0.05MPa micro-pressure to 2.5MPa extreme pressure: no leakage at 0.05/0.6MPa sealing, no deformation at 0.9MPa valve-body strength, no deformation or leakage at 2.5MPa burst for 60s. Eliminates "dripping" and "burst pipes."

### 2.4 Low Water Hammer + Self-Cleaning Anti-Clogging

The water-flow impact at the moment of closing is gentle (peak difference ≤0.2MPa), protecting pipe joints; the valve core self-cleans on every actuation, resisting sticking even in hard-water scaling and pipeline silt environments.

### 2.5 All-Condition Weather Resistance

Resists high/low temperature (-10°C~55°C) and humidity (40°C/95%RH for 48h, per CJ/T 194-2014), adapting to complex kitchen and bathroom environments. Usable water temperature 4°C~65°C.

### 2.6 Standardized Interface with Sensor Module

Connects with the GBL-9166 basin sensor module via a standardized interface: power is supplied uniformly by the module (complete-unit DC 4.5~6.0V), signal drives the module's pulse actuation, structurally forming a complete basin sensor system. System-level synergy endows the complete unit with low power, strong-light immunity, and military-grade EMC capabilities.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: 2.5MPa Burst Pressure—Confident Water-Path Safety

Daily supply pressure is generally 0.1~0.4MPa, while the 9168's burst pressure reaches 2.5MPa (about 25x), leaving ample margin. It withstands unstable pressure in old neighborhoods and transient high pressure from variable-frequency pump start/stop, so property managers need not worry daily about "which day a pipe bursts and floods the ceiling."

### Selling Point 2: 500,000 Cycles + Self-Cleaning Anti-Clog—Worry-Free After-Sales

The valve core is the most failure-prone part of sensor sanitary ware. The 9168 has a service life of over 500,000 cycles—equivalent to nearly 3 years at 500 uses per day; paired with the self-cleaning structure, it resists sticking and clogging in complex water quality. For complete-unit makers and property managers, lower service frequency means lower lifecycle cost.

### Selling Point 3: ≥8L/min High Flow—Good Output Even at Low Pressure

In low-pressure floors and peak water-use periods, basin sensor faucets with the 9168 still deliver abundant water, so users need not repeatedly wave to "urge" the water. The valve-body flow channel is optimized—no decay at low pressure, no screaming at high pressure—for a consistent experience.

## V. Installation Instructions

> The 9168 control box is part of the GBL-9166 basin sensor module; it is typically installed with the sensor module and faucet body under the counter or in a pre-embedded box.

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. First flush the pipeline with water to clear sand and rust and prevent solenoid clogging.
2. Confirm water pressure 0.05MPa ~ 0.8MPa.
3. Inlet interface matches the faucet body and sensor module (standard pipe thread).
4. Install the control box in a ventilated, dry place; do not submerge long-term; leave service space.

### 5.2 Notes

- Shut off water before installation or repair.
- The control box is powered uniformly by the sensor module; do not apply non-standard voltage directly.
- Do not hot-plug the solenoid drive terminal.
- Wrap PTFE tape or add sealing gaskets at pipe connections to ensure no leakage.
- Keep the to-be-installed control box in a ventilated, dry indoor area to avoid moisture.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Connect the control box inlet to the supply pipe, outlet to the faucet body.
2. Open water and pressure-test; confirm no leakage at all interfaces from 0.05~0.9MPa.
3. Connect the solenoid drive terminal to the corresponding sensor module interface (mind polarity).
4. Power on with the sensor module for self-check: module sends signal, control box solenoid actuates once.
5. Test in front of the sensor window; confirm open/close time and action are normal.
6. Secure the control box, leaving service space.

### 5.4 Coordination Self-Check

The control box itself has no independent power supply; it is driven by the sensor module. After power-on, the module drives the solenoid to actuate for self-check; if the solenoid does not move, check module power, drive-terminal connection, and control-box inlet pressure.
