## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL-9122** is a sensor faucet with an instant-heating function: extend a hand and warm water comes out—no need to first drain half a basin of cold water waiting for heating. It solves the winter hand-washing pain point in public lavatories: a conventional cold-water sensor faucet delivers a gush of ice water in winter, forcing people to either tough it out or hunt for a hot-water point. The 9122 integrates a 3000W instant-heating module in the faucet; with the handle set to a non-cold position, heating starts the moment the sensor opens the valve, delivering warm water.

It uses Single-window Dual-sensor technology: entering the sensing range from far to near gives instant on/off (water on at hand, off when leaving); entering from close range at the side (about 2~4cm from the sensing window) gives sustained on/off (one trigger to open, another to close). Both modes in one window keep the faucet exterior clean. Each valve opening lights the digital-display module to show current water temperature and turns off on close; while heating, a small dot at the lower-right of the display lights in sync, making the hot-water status visible at a glance.

Power is AC 220V, standby power ≤2W; multiple safety protections are thorough: NTC electronic over-temperature protection above 59℃, thermostat forced disconnect above 120℃, no heating when water pressure is below 0.07MPa, residual-current device cuts whole-unit power when leakage exceeds 10mA, and auto valve-close within 1 minute after power loss. Whole-unit waterproof IPX6, insulation resistance ≥50MΩ, withstand voltage AC1500V—safe for commercial use.

Bringing the above together: the GBL-9122 turns public-lavatory hand washing from "drain cold water first" into "warm water at a hand's reach," and covers heating, temperature control, leakage, over-temperature, and low-pressure safety in one go. The three points below are the product values we believe are most worth telling.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Single-window Dual-sensor, One Window Two Usages

Entering the sensing range from far to near, the solenoid opens the valve for water and closes on leaving (instant on/off); entering from close range at the side about 2~4cm, the valve opens for water and only closes after re-entering from the side on leaving (sustained on/off). Each sensing opening makes the water indicator flash once. One sensing window covers both interactions, keeping the panel clean.

### 2.2 Instant-on Heating, Warm Water at a Hand's Reach

With the handle at a non-cold position, sensing valve-open starts heating simultaneously, and the small dot at the lower-right of the display lights to indicate heating; at the cold position it only outputs water without heating. The 3000W instant-heating module needs no water storage preheat, so no draining half a basin of cold water, and winter hand washing is not icy.

### 2.3 Digital Temperature Display, Visible Status

Each valve opening shows the current output temperature on the digital-display module and turns off on close; the lower-right dot lights in sync while heating. Temperature error no more than ±2℃ (measured at 30℃/40℃/49℃), NTC response within 5s—users know the water temperature clearly.

### 2.4 Multiple Safety, Safe for Commercial Use

Over-temperature protection: NTC detects above 59℃ to start electronic over-temperature protection and stop heating, above 120℃ the thermostat disconnects to force heating stop. Low-pressure protection: even at a non-cold position, no heating when pressure is below 0.07±0.02MPa. Leakage protection: when leakage current exceeds 10mA the residual-current device auto-disconnects whole-unit power. Power-loss protection: sudden power loss during opening or closing auto-closes the valve within 1 minute.

### 2.5 Fully Protected Unit

Waterproof rating IPX6 (GB/T 4208-2017); insulation resistance L/N to ground ≥50MΩ at DC500V; withstand voltage L/N to ground AC1500V for 3~5s with leakage ≤5mA; leakage current between L and N at 1.06× rated input ≤0.75mA. Electric shock protection meets the Class II controller requirement of GB 14536.1-2008.

### 2.6 Stable Sensing, Strong Anti-interference

ESD contact discharge 8kV, air discharge 15kV (1s interval, 10 times each polarity) meets Class A; temperature shock 55±2℃/-10±2℃ 100 cycles functions normally; salt spray NSS 48h rating 10 (NTC probe); digital display life ≥1000h with no flicker or light leak.

### 2.7 Wide Adjustable Distance, Fits Different Basins

Factory sensing distance 150mm±10 (referenced to 29.7×29.7cm standard white board), adjustable range 30~200mm, fine-tuned on site by basin depth and usage habit; 60±5s timeout auto valve-close prevents forgetting to close water.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm supply water pressure is not below the low-pressure protection threshold; add a booster pump if needed
2. Confirm an AC 220V reliably grounded power point on site (Class II electric-shock protection requires grounding)
3. Confirm the basin outlet thread is G1/2
4. Keep the sensing window away from direct strong light and mirror reflection

### 5.2 Precautions

- Cut AC 220V power and water before installation and maintenance
- Each AC-powered unit sets an individual power switch and reliable grounding
- Heating element resistance and NTC probe must not be hot-plugged
- Never perform water on/off or potting while powered

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix the faucet body on the basin, connect the G1/2 inlet pipe
2. Open water and pressure-test, confirm no leakage at connections under 0.05/0.8MPa
3. Connect AC 220V power with reliable grounding, install the digital-display sensing panel
4. On power-up the segment display shows "88℃" for about 3s then turns off, entering standby
5. Set handle to a non-cold position, sense valve-open to verify heating and display
6. Test instant on/off and sustained on/off modes at far and near respectively

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

Each re-power-up closes the valve and the segment display shows "88℃", turning off after 3s to enter standby. On first sensing valve-open the indicator flashes once, the display lights to show current water temperature, and the lower-right dot lights while heating.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Troubleshooting

No heating at low pressure is normal protection; if the display is abnormal or leaks, first cut AC 220V power and check grounding and sealing. A tripped residual-current device indicates leakage—must be inspected by a professional before reset. For long-term disuse, cut power and water.
