## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6178 is GIBO's flagship 2-in-1 smart sensor faucet for high-end residences and quality spaces, belonging to the fifth-tier residential smart series (consumption-upgrade selection) and the top choice for high-end residences / boutique homestays. The product integrates sensor water flow and sensor soap dispensing into a single faucet body, equipped with Tri-sensor-window coordination technology (top digital-display sensor + lower infrared water-flow sensor + side soap-dispensing sensor window). Combined with the lower infrared water-flow sensor and the side soap sensor window, it realizes a complete touch-free handwashing flow of "wave for soap—reach to rinse."

The top LED display presents the water temperature value in real time (accurate to 0.1°C), paired with a mechanical temperature-adjustment handle for precise hot/cold control—elderly and child users can see the temperature directly, effectively avoiding scald risks. A zinc-alloy body with a black tempered-glass panel, a built-in 500ml large-capacity soap bottle; only one faucet remains on the countertop for an extremely minimalist, clean look. Combining "tri-window coordination, soap 2-in-1, digital-display scald prevention" is the most tangible value this product delivers to high-end users.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Countertop hole diameter ≥35mm (standard basin hole)
- Reserve a power outlet (AC 100–240V)
- Reserve under-counter cabinet or service space for the control box

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off water and power before installation and servicing
- Wipe the glass panel with a soft cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners; do not use acidic cleaners on the zinc-alloy plating to prevent corrosion
- Recommended to use low-viscosity foam-type hand soap to ensure smooth pumping

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Countertop hole diameter ≥35mm (standard basin hole)
2. Secure the faucet body and connect the hot/cold dual-line water pipes (G1/2 standard inlet)
3. Place the control box in the under-counter cabinet or reserved service space
4. Connect AC 100–240V power
5. Power on and test the tri-sensor windows and display

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

The sensing distance and sensitivity are pre-set at the factory; after power-on, if the LED display lights up normally and the three sensor windows dispense soap and water normally, the unit is working correctly. If adjustment is needed, contact after-sales or refer to the installation manual for fine-tuning.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care

- Soap refill: unscrew the bottom or side soap bottle cap to refill; recommended to use low-viscosity foam-type hand soap to ensure smooth pumping
- Cleaning & care: wipe the glass panel with a soft cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners; do not use acidic cleaners on the zinc-alloy plating to prevent corrosion
- Troubleshooting: if the LED does not light up, first check the power connection; if no water/soap, check whether the line is clogged or soap is depleted; if sensing is abnormal, restart power and retry
