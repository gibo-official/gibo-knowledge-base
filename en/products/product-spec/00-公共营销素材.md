Precautions for Sensor Toilet Flush-valve Selection
Sensor toilet flush valves come in two installation forms: concealed (product installed inside the wall) and exposed (product installed outside the wall). The key to selection lies in:
1. Concealed or exposed?
If the bathroom is a new project, concealed products are recommended for better aesthetics. If the bathroom is a renovation project and you don't want to break the wall, use exposed products — convenient install, directly replace the original flush valve.
2. Which appearance to choose?
Commonly used concealed sensor urinal flush-valve models are GBL-6210, GBL-6212, GBL-6213 and GBL-6215; commonly used exposed sensor urinal flush-valve models are GBL-6200, GBL-6202.
Commonly used concealed sensor squat-pan flush-valve models are GBL-6300, GBL-6313 and GBL-6315; commonly used exposed sensor urinal flush-valve model is GBL-6301.
3. Battery-powered or mains-powered?
If it belongs to a public place with high foot traffic, mains-powered is recommended, avoiding the trouble of battery replacements. If it is an office, with not-high daily use frequency, battery-powered can be chosen, replacing batteries once a year.
4. According to the wall thickness of the application site, choose the product; generally the pre-buried part thickness is 8–11cm. If wall thickness is below 8cm, be sure to choose a product with an ultra-thin pre-buried part, to avoid damaging the wall effect.
Excellence Chapter

5. Why choose products with filter valve and regulating valve?
The filter valve easily filters all impurities, preventing solenoid-valve blockage;
The regulating valve can adjust water volume; you can choose flush volume according to use frequency, and it also acts as a water-stop valve — when cleaning the filter valve, no need to close the main water valve.
6. What's the difference between GIBO squat-pan valves and ordinary squat-pan valves?
Currently the most failure-prone sensor products on the market are squat-pan valves; ordinary squat-pan valves use diaphragm type, easily causing failures due to diaphragm aging, and additionally, without filter screen and water-volume adjustment, lead to solenoid-valve blockage and thus long-flow.
GIBO squat-pan valves adopt piston-type design, cast in fine brass, with higher precision and larger flow, overcoming the diaphragm-aging problem, with faster water on/off and greater stability.
7. How to judge the material of the pre-buried box? What are the pros and cons of different materials?
Generally iron, rust-proof iron, ABS engineering plastic and stainless steel are used. Iron pre-buried boxes generally use paint treatment to extend service life; rust-proof iron and stainless steel both have bright surfaces and can be judged with a magnet — stainless steel is non-magnetic. Iron-painted was mainly used in early products; the pre-buried box easily rusts, generally showing corrosion in about 2 years; rust-proof iron has slightly poorer hardness; ABS engineering plastic, after about 3 years of use, will oxidize and cause internal molecular hardening.

Sales-guide Sales Theory III
Customer Psychological Analysis: by different methods, there are different types.
I. By purchase attitude and requirements
1. Habitual type
This type of customer generally relies on past purchase experience and consumption habits to take purchase behavior; they either patronize a certain store long-term or use a certain brand long-term; environmental changes, age increase etc. won't change this purchase habit. When buying products they decide decisively, unaffected by fashion.
2. Rational type
This type of customer is good at observing, analyzing and comparing. They have widely collected needed product information before purchase, understand market conditions, and only make a purchase decision after careful weighing of pros and cons; at purchase they behave rationally and cautiously, unaffected by others and advertising, selecting products carefully and patiently. Throughout the purchase process they maintain high autonomy, always governed by reason.

Sales-guide Sales Theory IV
3. Economic type
The economic type is also called the price type. This type of customer is very sensitive to product price. Customers who judge product quality by price level often think high-priced products are high-quality, the higher the price the more they want to buy; customers interested in cheap products are extremely sensitive to price differences among similar products — as long as the price is low they consider it cost-effective; sale prices, preferential prices and clearance prices have strong attraction for this consumer segment.
4. Impulsive type
This type of customer is sensitive to external stimuli, with active psychological reactions; under the stimulation of external product advertising, salespersons and others' influence, they don't analyze and compare, but buy quickly based on intuitive feeling; new products and fashionable goods have the greatest attraction for them.

Sales-guide Sales Theory V
5. Emotional type
This type of customer has rich psychological activity, is easily excited, loves imagination, and is also fairly rich in imagination and association; therefore, when purchasing they are easily dominated by feelings and easily infected and induced by the external environment, often determining purchase by whether the product meets their emotional needs.
6. Doubting type
This type of customer is introverted, cautious in word and deed, suspicious. They think thrice before acting before purchase, and after purchase still suspect whether they've been cheated.
7. Casual type
This type of customer either lacks experience, lacks opinion, or buys under orders; when selecting they mostly appear indecisive and lacking opinion, generally hoping for the sales guide's hints and help. Some customers are undemanding and unpicky in life, which also shows in casual purchase behavior — this type also belongs to the casual type.

Sales-guide Sales Theory VI
II. By purchase behavior
1. Composed type
This type of customer, due to calm and slow nerve processes, is steady in reaction; therefore, once the purchase motive is determined, it's not easily changed, and rarely affected by external factors. In purchase activities, except for the language necessary to buy products, they keep silent, feelings not shown outwardly, strongly inhibited, moderately sociable but not very easygoing.
2. Lively type
This type of customer has balanced and highly flexible nerve processes, is good at adapting to various environments, has broad interests and hobbies but easily changes; in terms of purchase behavior, appears talkative and lively, willing to approach and chat with others when buying and selecting products, actively exchanging opinions with customers or sales guides.

Sales-guide Sales Theory VII
3. Gentle type
This type of customer reacts slowly and timidly when guided. In purchase, they value service attitude, trust the sales guide's reception, often make purchase decisions based on the sales guide's introduction and opinions, and rarely re-check product quality themselves.
4. Resistant type
These customers are highly sensitive and alert when purchasing. They don't trust the sales guide's introduction and refuse to accept others' opinions. This type may be a professional expert in some field, or may be someone worried about being cheated.
5. Arrogant type
This type of customer appears extremely proud in both speech and expression when selecting products, even makes demands in a commanding tone, has high requirements for products and sales-guide service, and if slightly dissatisfied, quarrels with the sales guide, irascible and uncontrollable.

Sales-guide Sales Theory VIII
III. By purchase orientation
1. Fixed-purchase type
This type of customer, when the sales guide hands the product to them, will ask a few questions and then pay. The sales guide generally needn't introduce the product in detail, unless the customer requests. This type hopes to purchase the needed product quickly; they enter the store with fast pace, concentrated gaze or pointing to a specific product.
2. Hesitant type
This type has purchase desire but hasn't determined the purchase target. They enter the store with slow pace, fairly concentrated gaze, interested in a certain product category, appearing thoughtful. This type worries about buying the wrong thing, hesitates when selecting, often taking a long time.

["Yes, but" method]
Agree with one aspect of the customer's opinion, and on the other hand explain the reason for the customer's objection.
E.g.: Customer: "This faucet is more expensive than other similar brands." Sales guide: "Yes, this model's price is higher, but GIBO faucets use national-standard 59-1 brass, plating passed grade 9, and were the chosen product for the Expo China Pavilion, different from other domestic products, and GIBO is a provincial famous trademark."
[Patient-explanation method]
When the customer's objection comes from wrong information or misunderstanding, explain patiently, but in expression pay attention to method and skill, the tone must be tactful and gentle.
E.g.: Customer: "GIBO's sensors are of poor quality." Sales guide: "This is just an individual case; all brands have similar situations. We are responsible for quality — whatever problem you have can be solved by us per the Three-guarantee regulations."

Sales-guide Sales Techniques
2. Sales Techniques (2)
[Guidance method]
Sometimes you can guide the customer by asking them questions, letting the customer resolve their own doubts and find the answer, which may work better than directly answering the question.
[Introduce others' purchase experiences]
Use the customer's feelings to move dissatisfied customers; generally customers are willing to hear other buyers' evaluations of the product. ["Show the trend" method]

E.g.: Customer: "Is this product this year's new model?" Sales guide: "Yes, this is our newly added product in this year's catalog; we also run this product's advertisement in China Ceramics Magazine."

Ceramic Product Selling Point I
Issues to Note When Choosing Ceramic Sanitary Ware
Choose ceramic sanitary ware by looking, touching and listening; important reference indicators are glaze smoothness, brightness and ceramic water-absorption rate.
1. Look: Products with high smoothness have pure and correct color, are not easily stained and fouled, easy to clean, self-cleaning. When judging, choose strong light, carefully observe the product surface reflection from the side, the better one has no tiny pinholes and pockmarks, or very few pinholes and pockmarks. High-brightness-index products use high-quality glaze materials and very good glazing craft, with good light reflection, uniform, thus good visual effect, appearing high-grade.

Ceramic Product Selling Point II
2. Touch: When choosing, you can gently stroke the surface with your hand; the one feeling very smooth and fine is good. You can also touch the back, the better one feels a subtle "sandy" friction.
3. Listen: You can also tap the ceramic surface with your hand; generally good ceramic material gives a relatively crisp sound when tapped.
4. Compare: When choosing you can also put products of different brands together and compare and observe from the above aspects, also easily judging high-quality products.

Ceramic Product Selling Point III
Additionally, the water-absorption-rate indicator refers to the ceramic product's certain adsorption and permeation ability for water; the lower the water-absorption rate the better the product. If water is absorbed into the ceramic, the ceramic will expand somewhat, easily causing the glaze surface to crack from expansion. Especially for toilets, high-water-absorption products easily absorb dirt and odor in the water into the ceramic; after long use, incurable odor will be produced.

Ceramic Product Selling Point VIII
Toilet Flush Methods
High-efficiency siphon and direct-drop flush:
Toilet flush methods are divided into siphon type and direct-drop type. Siphon type can be divided into ordinary siphon, jet siphon and whirl siphon.
Direct-drop type is flush force produced by water fall height; its characteristic is low water bottom and large pipe diameter, not easily blocked; additionally, this flush is fairly water-saving, and can be paired with 3/6-liter dual-stage-flush water fittings.

Ceramic Product Selling Point IX
Ordinary siphon refers to making water reach a certain level through the bottleneck design of the pipe to cause the siphon effect; this design has good flush-force effect.
Jet siphon refers to a jet hole at the water-storage of the base; when flushing, water sprays from the jet hole, accelerating siphon formation and thoroughly replacing the stored water.
Whirl siphon refers to a large water outlet at the water-storage of the base. When flushing, most water gushes from here, raising the base water level and causing siphon. This design has a very good silent effect. The quality of the toilet flush function mainly depends on the base pipe design, but has nothing to do with the cistern water-level height.
