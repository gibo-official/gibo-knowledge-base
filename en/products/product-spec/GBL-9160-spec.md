## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-9160 is a concealed integrated sensor urinal launched by GIBO for commercial restrooms, public toilets, and high-end hotel washrooms. Its core highlight is the **integrated concealed structure**: the sensing module and water path are integrated into one unit, with only a concealed panel exposed on the wall and pipelines and valve body hidden inside, clean and tidy. It requires no separate control box, greatly reducing installation and maintenance complexity.

The product adopts GIBO's mature sensor solution with **Low Water Hammer Design** for quiet, stable operation, effectively suppressing water-hammer shock. Touchless sensing enables touchless flush, freeing hands and reducing cross-infection; the automatic anti-odor flush keeps the trap sealed and suppresses odors — an integrated smart urinal solution for public restrooms.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Integrated Concealed Structure

The sensing module and water path are integrated into one unit; only a concealed panel is exposed on the wall, with pipelines and valve body hidden inside, clean and tidy and with no separate control box.

### 2.2 Low Water Hammer Design

GIBO's mature solenoid valve solution combined with Low Water Hammer Design effectively suppresses water-hammer shock, delivering quiet, stable operation and protecting the pipeline system.

### 2.3 Touchless Sensor Flush

Water flushes when a person leaves the sensing zone and stops automatically, with no need to touch the device throughout, reducing the spread of bacteria among users at the source.

### 2.4 Automatic Anti-odor Flush

Supports an automatic anti-odor flush once every 24 hours, keeping the trap sealed and suppressing odors.

### 2.5 Power-loss Protection

Built-in power-loss protection circuit automatically closes the solenoid valve when power is lost or the battery is depleted, ensuring the final shut-off action is completed and avoiding running-water risk.

### 2.6 IPX7 Waterproof

The sensing module adopts triple waterproof treatment, reaching IPX7 Waterproof, adapting to humid restroom environments.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction match the product
- Confirm the power supply method (DC battery / AC); for first use, recommend a full charge (battery version)
- Confirm wall thickness meets the concealed installation requirement (subject to official materials)

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- The sensing window must be kept clean to avoid affecting sensitivity
- Do not use steel wool on the panel surface

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction
2. Install the concealed body into the wall and connect the water inlet/outlet pipes, checking for leaks
3. Install the battery or connect AC power
4. Power on, then test sensor auto flush
5. Confirm anti-odor flush and Low Water Hammer operation are normal

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test sensor auto flush (triggered on departure); confirm the sensing window responds normally and the flush is stable.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Keep the sensing window clean; avoid dust/buildup affecting sensitivity
- When the indicator blinks abnormally, charge / replace the battery promptly
- For high-frequency-use locations, recommend checking the solenoid valve filter periodically
