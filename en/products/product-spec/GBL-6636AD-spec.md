## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6636AD is an integrated digital-display non-contact basin faucet from GIBO, designed for family bathrooms and quality commercial spaces that need both intelligence and safety. Its core highlight is the **integrated digital temperature display**: the spout shows the real-time water temperature via LED digits, so the whole family — from the elderly to children — can see it at a glance, avoiding scalding from misjudged temperature.

The product adopts an all-brass body with a premium ceramic disc cartridge, paired with GIBO's mature solenoid valve solution and Low Water Hammer Design, delivering quiet, stable operation and effectively suppressing the "bang" of water-hammer shock. The non-contact sensing enables touchless flow, freeing hands and reducing cross-infection — a smart, safe basin faucet solution for the whole family.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Integrated Digital Temperature Display

The LED digits on the spout show the real-time water temperature, so the user can read it at a glance and adjust in time, avoiding scalding from misjudged temperature — specially friendly to elders and children.

### 2.2 All-brass Body + Ceramic Disc Cartridge

The all-brass body is durable and corrosion-resistant; the premium ceramic disc cartridge ensures smooth, long-life switching of hot/cold water, with no dripping after closing.

### 2.3 Low Water Hammer Design

GIBO's mature solenoid valve solution combined with Low Water Hammer Design effectively suppresses water-hammer shock, delivering quiet, stable operation and protecting the home water pipeline.

### 2.4 Non-contact Sensing, Touchless Flow

Water flows when you reach out and stops when you withdraw, with no need to touch the faucet handle throughout, reducing the spread of bacteria among family members at the source.

### 2.5 Hot/Cold Compatible

Suitable for hot/cold water supply, meeting the daily washing needs of family bathrooms and quality commercial spaces.

### 2.6 Power-loss Protection

Built-in power-loss protection circuit automatically closes the solenoid valve when power is lost or the battery is depleted, ensuring the final shut-off action is completed and avoiding running-water risk.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin pre-drilled hole size and countertop opening meet installation requirements
- Confirm the power supply method (DC battery / AC); for first use, recommend a full charge (for battery version)
- Confirm the cold/hot water inlet pipes are correctly connected (left hot, right cold)

### 5.2 Precautions

- For first use, recommend a full charge to activate the battery (battery version)
- Confirm the sensing window is free of obstructions to avoid affecting sensitivity
- Do not use steel wool or organic solvents on the body surface

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Install the faucet body and secure it with the mounting nut
2. Connect the cold/hot water inlet pipes (left hot, right cold), checking for leaks
3. Install the battery or connect AC power
4. Power on, then test sensing flow and digital temperature display
5. Confirm hot/cold switching and Low Water Hammer operation are normal

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test reach-and-flow / withdraw-and-stop; check that the digital temperature display lights up normally and shows the real-time water temperature; confirm hot/cold switching is correct.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Wipe the sensing window with a soft cloth; avoid grease buildup affecting sensitivity
- When the LED indicator blinks abnormally, charge / replace the battery promptly
- Clean the body with a neutral cleaner; do not use steel wool
