## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6177 is GIBO's core 2026 residential model of the 4D Luxury series (4DeLuxe Pro)—the dTOF laser digital-display dual-sensor faucet. It deeply fuses dTOF laser sensing, an LED HD display, and D-shape aesthetic design; a front IR + laser hybrid sensing (U940 front sensor) and side IR sensing (U856-S1 side sensor) form the dual-sensor system. Front sensing triggers short immediate flow (hand wash/rinse), side sensing triggers long continuous flow (face wash/brush/collect). The top of the curved spout integrates an H10 digital-tube module that shows the real-time water temperature (±1℃) on each flow and goes dark when closed; red/blue backlight intuitively distinguishes hot/cold status.

New middle-class families pursuing quality life want more than "just flows"—they want good-looking, easy-to-use, and safe. The 6177 is built for such home bathroom upgrades: the spout supports a 2-in-1 mode of shower water (powerful rinse) and stream water (soft anti-splash) with one-touch switch, and gesture control of water and temperature; a mechanical handle adjusts temperature, and a remote fine-tunes the sensing distance. The whole unit follows the "Design + Digital + Deluxe + Distinctive" 4D design philosophy, breaking the dull pattern of traditional round faucets with a minimalist D-shape symbol as its exclusive visual mark.

Combining the above: the 6177 makes the home faucet both a design piece and delivers laser sensing, temperature display, and dual-mode outlet—giving family members, especially the elderly and children, a more reassuring wash experience. The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Dual-sensor Smart Flow

- **Front sensing flow**: Flows immediately on object detection, auto stops on leave, auto closes after continuous sensing 60s±5s. If the side sensing is also triggered during front-sensing activation, it switches to the side long-flow mode.
- **Side sensing flow**: One sense opens, one more sense closes (a "switch" logic), auto closes after continuous flow 180s±10s. Front sensing is disabled during side-sensing activation to prevent conflict.

### 2.2 LED Temperature Display

The digital tube shows the current water temperature on each flow and does not show when flow stops. Minimum resolution 1℃, error ±1℃. During power-on self-check it shows "88℃" then goes dark after completion. The temperature sensor uses an SL11x17 (R100=3.3K) NTC thermistor.

### 2.3 Mechanical Handle Temperature

The side mechanical handle freely adjusts the output temperature, intuitive and simple, with real-time feedback of temperature change during flow.

### 2.4 Strong Anti-interference Capability

- Electrostatic discharge (ESD): air discharge ±15KV, contact discharge ±8KV (level 4)
- Electromagnetic radiation (EMI): 80MHz~1000MHz field strength 3V/m (level 2)
- Electrical fast transient (EFT): ±4KV normal operation
- Light interference: no false action under incandescent, fluorescent, halogen, daylight, or bathroom heater light sources

### 2.5 High-reliability Waterway Performance

- Leaktightness: no leak at 0.05MPa and 0.80MPa
- Strength: 0.9MPa water pressure for 30 s, no deformation or leak
- Burst pressure: static 2.5MPa tested for 60 s, no leak
- Load-resistant installation: G1/2 thread withstands 20N·m torque without damage

### 2.6 Dual-power Redundant Supply

Main power is an AC110V~220V to DC6V/1A power adapter; backup power is 4× AA alkaline batteries (independent sealed battery box). AC standby power ≤ 2W, battery standby current ≤ 100μA. The control assembly waterproof rating is IPX5.

### 2.7 Extremely Wide Environmental Adaptability

Operating temperature 1℃~55℃, applicable water temperature 4℃~60℃, relative humidity 10%~95%RH, storage temperature -20℃~75℃. After high-low temperature cycling (+55℃/-10℃ for 4 hours each) and humidity testing (40℃/95%RH/48 hours), it still maintains leaktightness.

### 2.8 2-in-1 Outlet Mode

The spout supports a one-touch switch between shower water (powerful rinse) and stream water (soft anti-splash), paired with dTOF gesture control—palm hover to switch shower/stream, wave left/right to adjust water temperature—covering hand wash, face wash, and water collection across multiple scenarios.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole size; GBL-6177 uses a DN32 standard interface, fitting most basins
- Prepare the AC110~220V power adapter and 4× AA alkaline batteries (backup)

### 5.2 Precautions

- Even when using AC as main power, the battery box must still contain batteries (batteries slowly consume even on AC)
- Low-voltage alarm in two levels: below 4.8V slow blink warning, below 4.5V fast blink and valve close
- Do not use strong acid/alkali solvents when cleaning, to avoid damaging the microcrystal glass surface coating

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Pass the faucet through the basin hole; tighten the fixing nut below
2. Connect the hot/cold inlet hoses (note hot/cold markings)
3. Connect the AC110-220V power adapter output DC6V, and also insert 4× AA alkaline batteries in the battery box as backup power
4. After power-on, the digital tube shows "88℃" for self-check, then goes dark; the solenoid valve is in the closed state
5. Test the front and side sensing flow functions separately; to adjust distance, aim the remote at the corresponding sensor window and press +/- to fine-tune

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, the digital tube shows "88℃" for self-check, then goes dark; the solenoid valve is in the closed state. Keep the sensor window unobstructed during learning/self-check.

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care

- Regularly wipe the microcrystal glass panel and sensor window with a soft cloth
- Check the battery box status every 6 months (batteries slowly consume even on AC)
- When voltage is below 4.8V the indicator blinks slowly 5 times to prompt battery replacement; below 4.5V it blinks fast 10 times and stops flow
- Do not use strong acid/alkali solvents when cleaning to avoid damaging the microcrystal glass surface coating
