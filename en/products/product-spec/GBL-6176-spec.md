## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6176 is GIBO's single-sensor LED display basin faucet for five-star hotels, high-end clubs, top-grade offices, and boutique model rooms. The whole unit uses a zinc alloy body with bold, smooth lines and strong modern architectural beauty; the spout integrates an LED water-temperature light ring and a digital display—changing color by real-time temperature and showing the exact degrees; a built-in high-precision thermostatic cartridge ends sudden hot/cold, combining tech feel with practicality. The product uses single IR sensing—flow on reach, off on leave—fully non-contact, blocking cross-infection at the source; supports DC battery and AC adapter dual power, flexibly configured per project.

The 6176 uses the LED ring plus display to make "temperature visible" an experience highlight, and the thermostatic cartridge to make "temperature stable" a hard guarantee—a tech-feel choice for high-end spaces. ## II. Features

### 2.1 Single IR Auto Flow

Reach into the sensing zone to auto-start flow; leave to immediately shut off. Default sensing distance 13±2cm, fast response, effectively avoiding false action.

### 2.2 LED Water-temp Light Ring + Display

During flow, the LED ring shows blue/orange/red by water temperature while the digital display shows the real-time temperature—water status at a glance.

### 2.3 High-precision Thermostatic Cartridge

Built-in thermostatic cartridge auto-balances the hot/cold mix for stable, comfortable output; the mechanical handle fine-tunes the preferred temperature.

### 2.4 Dual-power AC/DC Switch

Supports DC6V battery and AC100~240V adapter dual power modes; auto-switches to DC backup when AC fails, ensuring uninterrupted use.

### 2.5 Timeout Water-stop Protection

Continuous use beyond the set duration (default 60 s) auto forces shut-off, preventing waste from obstruction-caused continuous flow.

### 2.6 Low-battery Smart Prompt

The indicator blinks when power is low to prompt replacement; forces the solenoid valve closed when depleted, preventing continuous leaking from power loss.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole matches the faucet body and control box; prepare G1/2 inlet pipe and angle valve
- Confirm water pressure is within 0.05~0.7MPa and applicable water temperature 4~60℃
- Plan the power method (DC battery / AC adapter)

### 5.2 Precautions

- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse directly with water or soak
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries to ensure system reset
- If sensing is abnormal, first check battery level and sensor window cleanliness
- Regularly clean the spout and display surface to keep the display clear

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Insert the full-brass/zinc-alloy faucet body into the basin hole; fit rubber washer → stainless washer → lock nut in order and tighten
2. Connect the outlet pipe to the control box OUTLET; connect the inlet pipe to INLET and then to the angle valve
3. Connect the signal wire to the matching control box port
4. Insert DC batteries or connect the AC power adapter
5. Supply water and power, then test sensing and display normal to complete installation

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After supplying water and power, test sensing and display normal; installation is complete once flow, temperature display, and signal connection are confirmed correct.

### 5.5 Maintenance / Battery Replacement

- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse directly with water or soak
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries to ensure system reset
- If sensing is abnormal, first check battery level and sensor window cleanliness
- Regularly clean the spout and display surface to keep the display clear
