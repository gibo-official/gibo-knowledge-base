## 1. Product Introduction

The **GBL-6170D** mounts on basins in public restrooms: water flows when hands approach and stops when they leave, with no contact with any switch throughout. What sets it apart from ordinary single-sensor faucets is its "dual-sensor mode": one is Instant-sensing flow (water on when hands arrive, off when they leave—ideal for quick hand washing), and the other is Sustained-flow sensing (one trigger to start, another trigger to stop—ideal for face washing, rinsing, and filling where continuous flow is needed). One faucet, two usages switched freely by habit—front-desk hand washing and back-counter filling both feel natural.

Lavatory areas in office buildings, malls, airports, schools, and hospitals see constant foot traffic all day, and demand both high "sensitivity" and "no false triggering." Too sensitive and flying insects or splashes trigger it; not sensitive enough and an outstretched hand gets no response—the GBL-6170D is built precisely around that balance point.

It uses GIBO's infrared dual-sensor solution, with standby power pressed below 45μA and 4 AA alkaline batteries lasting a long time; at low voltage the LED flashes rapidly as a prompt, and when the battery is insufficient it cuts off flow directly—no sudden breakdown mid-use. Distance stability is also well managed: from 6.5V down to 4.6V and from -10℃ up to 55℃, sensing-distance deviation stays within ±10%, giving consistent performance across seasons and between new and old batteries. Multiple units installed side by side do not falsely trigger each other, and incandescent, fluorescent, and LED lights at 45° oblique illumination 1m away cause distance variation of less than ±10%—stable even in heavily lit lavatories.

Bringing the above together: the GBL-6170D makes the public basin "flow at hand, run long when needed, long battery life, and no crosstalk even in a row." The three points below are the product values we believe are most worth telling.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Dual-sensor, Two Usages in One Unit

Sustained-flow sensing: hand enters the sensing zone to start flow (response ≤0.6s) with one indicator flash, re-enters to stop (response ≤0.5s); continuous flow beyond 180±10s auto-stops. Instant-sensing flow: hand enters to start (≤0.6s), leaves to stop (≤1s); continuous flow beyond 60±5s auto-stops. Covers both front-desk hand washing and back-counter filling with one faucet.

### 2.2 Ultra-low Standby, Long Battery Life

Static power ≤45μA; whole-unit energy measured per CJ/T 194-2014 with average standby current ≤45μA. Powered by 4× AA alkaline batteries—installable without wiring even in old restrooms without pre-installed power, essentially maintenance-free day to day.

### 2.3 Low-voltage Alarm, No Mid-way Breakdown

Operating voltage dropping to 4.6±0.1V enters the low-voltage alarm: the LED fast-flashes during sensing and continues fast-flashing 10 times after leaving to signal battery replacement; flow is forcibly cut off to avoid stopping only when fully dead. Voltage adaptability 4.6V~6.5V.

### 2.4 Stable Distance, Same in Winter/Summer and New/Old Batteries

From supply voltage 6.5V down to 4.6V, sensing-distance variation stays within ±10%; from temperature -10℃ up to +55℃, distance variation also stays within ±10%. Installed across seasons and battery states, the sensing range remains stable—no over-sensitive in summer and dead in winter.

### 2.5 No Crosstalk in Rows, Stable Under Light

Multiple units powered on together produce no false operation; incandescent, T5, fluorescent, and LED lights at 45° oblique illumination beyond 1m cause control-distance variation of less than ±10%; compliant with burst interference test and ESD interference test at Class A. A row of faucets side by side in a brightly lit lavatory each behaves properly.

### 2.6 Vibration & Shock Resistant, Securely Mounted

Vibration resistance: amplitude 0.35mm, frequency 10~55Hz, three mutually perpendicular axes swept 10 cycles, appearance and performance meet requirements; G1/2 thread withstands 20N·m torque held 60±5s with no cracks, no damage, no thread stripping. Withstands transport jolts and daily knocks.

### 2.7 Compact Structure, Fits Standard Basins

Standard size 47mm (L) × 47mm (W) × 79mm (H), inlet thread G1/2 single-barbed steel-wire hose, operating water pressure 0.05~0.8MPa, operating water temperature ≤75℃, fitting the vast majority of engineering basins. During controller installation, simply avoid direct strong light and mirror reflection.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, washing away sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid
2. Confirm water pressure 0.05MPa~0.8MPa; below 0.05MPa add a booster pump
3. Keep the sensing window away from direct strong light and mirror reflection; avoid large obstructions in front
4. Use same-brand high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off water and power before installation and maintenance
- For AC-powered units, set an individual power switch and reliable grounding per unit
- Do not hot-plug the sensor module terminals
- Avoid direct strong light and mirror reflection in the installation environment

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Drill the basin per drawing, fix the faucet body, connect the G1/2 inlet pipe
2. Open water and pressure-test, confirm no leakage at 0.05/1.0MPa
3. Insert 4× AA alkaline batteries (or connect adapter), install the sensing panel
4. On power-up the LED flashes once and enters standby; test dual modes per the function guide
5. Place hand / wave in front of the sensing window to test Instant-sensing and Sustained-flow

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

After inserting batteries or powering on, the faucet enters normal operation; on first sensing the indicator flashes once to signal a trigger. Under low voltage the LED fast-flashes as a prompt. After installation, verify both Instant-sensing flow and Sustained-flow modes one by one.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement

When operating voltage drops to 4.6±0.1V, each sensing LED fast-flashes 10 times to prompt battery replacement and forcibly cuts off flow. To replace: shut off water → open panel → take out battery box and replace 4× same-brand new alkaline batteries → re-seat and power on again.
