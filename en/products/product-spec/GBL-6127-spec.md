## I. Product Introduction

GBL-6127 is GIBO's stainless steel antibacterial sensor basin faucet for high-hygiene scenarios such as hospital wards, nursing homes, kindergartens, and food processing plants. The whole body uses food-grade SUS304 stainless steel with antibacterial elements added to the base material, effectively inhibiting E. coli, Staphylococcus aureus and other common bacteria; the surface is brushed and integrally welded, dense and smooth with no dead corners, leaving bacteria and dirt nowhere to attach. The product uses infrared auto sensing—flow on reach, off on leave—with no contact with any switch component, blocking cross-infection risk at the source; the split structure separates the control box from the faucet body, leaving only a simple round-head brushed stainless spout on the countertop for easy maintenance and a clean look. It supports DC battery (4× AA alkaline) and AC 220V dual power, flexibly switched per project need.

Hospitals, nursing homes, kindergartens, and food processing plants demand near-strict hygiene levels and most fear equipment that harbors dirt and causes cross-infection. The 6127 uses antibacterial SUS304 plus non-contact sensing plus an under-counter split easy-clean structure to deliver "hygiene, safety, easy-care" in one go—a solid choice for high-hygiene-sensitive venues. The three points below are the product values most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Auto Sensing

Reach into the sensing zone to auto-start flow; leave to immediately shut off. Sensing time under 0.6 s does not trigger flow, effectively avoiding false action.

### 2.2 Timeout Water-stop Protection

When wash time exceeds 60 s, the system automatically cuts the water supply, avoiding long continuous flow waste caused by obstruction or false sensing.

### 2.3 Dual-power AC/DC Switch

Supports DC6V battery and AC220V adapter dual power modes. Auto-switches to DC backup when AC power fails, ensuring uninterrupted use.

### 2.4 Low-battery Smart Prompt

When power is low, the indicator blinks 5 times consecutively to prompt replacement; when depleted, it blinks 10 times and forces the solenoid valve closed, preventing continuous leaking from power loss.

### 2.5 G1/2 Standard Interface + Removable Filter

Inlet/outlet pipe diameter is G1/2 standard thread, strong compatibility. The inlet has a removable filter mesh; when sand clogs a newly installed pipe, it can be quickly cleaned to restore flow.

### 2.6 Split Installation Structure

Control box size 83×40×54mm, can be hidden in the under-counter cabinet. The faucet body connects to the control box via hose; only the outlet component remains on the countertop, with minimal space occupation.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin hole matches the faucet body and control box; prepare G1/2 inlet pipe and angle valve
- Confirm water pressure is within 0.05~0.7MPa
- Plan to hide the control box in the under-counter cabinet, reserving service space

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off water and power before installation and servicing
- Use same-brand alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
- Connect the signal wire to the matching port; no hot-plugging with power on
- The control box is an electronic part; do not rinse with water

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Remove the lock sleeve, stainless washer, and rubber washer from the faucet in order
2. Insert the faucet body into the basin hole; fit the rubber washer → stainless washer → lock sleeve in order and tighten
3. Screw the faucet body G1/2 hose to the control box outlet; the user's own G1/2 inlet pipe screws to the control box inlet, the other end to the angle valve
4. Connect the black signal wire to the control box black port; DC version inserts 4× AA alkaline and connects the red power wire, AC version connects the adapter red wire
5. Open the angle valve to supply water; sensing test to confirm normal flow

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After inserting batteries or connecting power, reach into the sensing zone to test flow; normal indicator status enters standby, abnormal blink prompt see maintenance instructions.

### 5.5 Maintenance / Battery Replacement

- Clean with a clean dry soft cloth; do not rinse the control box with water
- Do not wipe with strong acid/alkali detergents
- When flow drops, promptly remove and clean the inlet filter (close the angle valve before removal)
- Wait 5 minutes before inserting new batteries to ensure proper system reset
