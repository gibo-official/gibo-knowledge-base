## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-6213AD is a high-end smart faucet in GIBO's "Aishang" series, built around a high-arc pull-out spout. Its biggest highlight is the **dual-outlet + dual-sensor** design: the downward outlet handles daily washing, while the upward outlet is specially designed for flushing the basin and rinsing large items. The whole family — from the elderly to children to caregivers — can use it with ease.

It adopts a **dual-sensor (side + downward)** configuration: the side sensor responds to a hand-wave for effortless switching, and the downward sensor handles the natural "reach-and-flow, withdraw-and-stop" washing posture. An integrated **intelligent temperature display** eliminates guessing water temperature, protecting elders and children from scalding. Combined with a **three-speed outlet adjustment** (bubble stream / shower stream / toothbrush stream) plus one-touch full-flow flush, it covers the full range of household washing scenarios — a pull-out smart faucet truly built for mother-and-baby and whole-family use.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Dual-outlet Pull-out Design

The high-arc pull-out spout flexibly rotates to reach the entire basin; the downward outlet handles hand/vegetable/fruit washing and cup rinsing, while the upward outlet is for flushing the basin and rinsing large items — no dead corners in daily cleaning.

### 2.2 Dual-sensor (Side + Downward)

The side sensor triggers a hand-wave for effortless switching between modes; the downward sensor handles the natural "reach-and-flow, withdraw-and-stop" washing posture. The dual-sensor combination adapts to both quick hand-washing and hands-busy scenarios.

### 2.3 Intelligent Temperature Display

The spout integrates an intelligent temperature display so users can read the real-time water temperature at a glance, avoiding scalding from misjudged temperature — specially friendly to elders and children.

### 2.4 Three-speed Outlet Adjustment

Three outlet modes are available: **bubble stream** (soft, splash-free, for washing), **shower stream** (wide coverage, for rinsing), and **toothbrush stream** (concentrated narrow stream, for rinsing cups/toothbrushes); plus one-touch full-flow flush for quick basin cleaning.

### 2.5 All-brass Mother-and-baby Grade Body

The body is all-brass with a lead-free, safe, mother-and-baby-grade finish — reassuring for families with infants and young children, combining durability with health safety.

### 2.6 Power-loss Protection

Built-in power-loss protection circuit automatically closes the solenoid valve when power is lost or the battery is depleted, ensuring the final shut-off action is completed and avoiding running-water risk.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the basin pre-drilled hole size and countertop opening meet the pull-out faucet installation requirements
- Confirm the power supply method (rechargeable built-in lithium battery); for first use, recommend a full charge
- Confirm the cold/hot water inlet pipes are correctly connected (left hot, right cold)

### 5.2 Precautions

- The pull-out hose should avoid sharp bending to prevent kinking affecting retraction
- For first use, recommend a full charge to activate the battery
- Confirm the sensing window is free of obstructions to avoid affecting sensitivity

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Install the faucet body and secure it with the mounting nut
2. Connect the cold/hot water inlet pipes (left hot, right cold), checking for leaks
3. Fit the pull-out hose and spout, confirming smooth retraction
4. Power on, then test side sensing and downward sensing separately
5. Confirm temperature display and three-speed outlet switch normally

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test side hand-wave switching and downward reach-and-flow; check that the temperature display lights up normally and the three-speed outlet switches correctly.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Wipe the sensing window with a soft cloth; avoid grease buildup affecting sensitivity
- Check the pull-out hose periodically for aging; replace promptly if abnormal
- When the LED indicator blinks abnormally, charge with the dedicated charger
