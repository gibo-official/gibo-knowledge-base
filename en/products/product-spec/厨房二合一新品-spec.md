## I. Product Introduction

GIBO's Kitchen Pull-out Sensor Faucet (new product) is a **water + foam 2-in-1** smart faucet built for high-end kitchens pursuing ultimate convenience. It combines a traditional Sensor Faucet and a Sensor Soap Dispenser into one: sensor water flow for hand/vegetable washing, sensor foam for degreasing cleaning, with only one faucet left on the countertop for a clean look and no hygiene dead corners. The pull-out spray head can stretch flexibly to rinse the four walls and corners of the sink — a new choice for the "one-stop" clean experience in high-end kitchens.

The most annoying thing in the kitchen is having greasy hands yet still needing to twist the faucet, or having both a faucet and a soap dispenser cluttering the countertop — messy and prone to hiding dirt. This new product combines the two tasks into one faucet, with touchless operation eliminating cross-contamination and improving kitchen hygiene and efficiency (specific sensing method and digital-display configuration to be verified). The three points below are the values most worth highlighting.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Water + Foam 2-in-1 — One Faucet Handles Pre- and Post-cleaning

Integrates sensor water flow and sensor foam (foam/soap) into the same faucet body: a light sensor touch delivers water for rinsing, another sensing zone triggers dense foam for degreasing — the whole hand-wash/vegetable-wash process completed in one step, with no separate soap dispenser needed on the countertop.

### Selling Point 2: Flexible Pull-out — No Dead Corners in the Sink

The pull-out spray head stretches flexibly, easily covering the four walls, corners, and the inside/outside of utensils; combined with different outlet modes, it adapts to diverse tasks like vegetable rinsing, dish cleaning, and countertop wiping.

### Selling Point 3: Touchless Operation — Zero Contact in Greasy Scenarios

With greasy hands or while handling raw food, there is no need to touch the faucet body; sensor delivers water/foam, eliminating cross-contamination and improving kitchen hygiene and efficiency (specific sensing method and digital-display configuration to be verified).

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the countertop has a reserved standard installation hole (specific cut-out dimensions to be verified)
- Confirm the hot/cold water inlet pipes are in place

### 5.2 Precautions

- Clean the faucet surface with a soft cloth dipped in a neutral cleaner; do not use steel wool or strong acid/alkali solvents
- Use a low-foam, easy-rinse soap formula to avoid clogging the pump lines
- If sensing distance needs adjustment, use the included remote control (if any) and fine-tune per instructions

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Countertop cut-out: confirm the countertop has a reserved standard installation hole (specific cut-out dimensions to be verified)
2. Secure the faucet: insert the body from below the countertop, fit the seal gasket and fastening nut, and connect the standard threaded interface
3. Connect water and power: connect hot/cold water inlet pipes and the power adapter or battery box (method to be verified)
4. Fill soap: inject foam-type / cleaning-type soap to the recommended water line
5. Power-on test: test the water-flow and foam sensing zones, confirming normal water flow and foam

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test the water-flow and foam sensing zones, confirming normal water flow and foam and accurate sensing triggers; fine-tune the sensing distance with the remote control (if any) per instructions.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Use a low-foam, easy-rinse soap formula to avoid clogging the pump lines; drain residual liquid if unused for a long time
- Clean the faucet surface with a soft cloth dipped in a neutral cleaner; do not use steel wool or strong acid/alkali solvents
- If sensing distance needs adjustment, use the included remote control (if any) and fine-tune per instructions
