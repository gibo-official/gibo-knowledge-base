## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-9165D is a surface-mounted integrated sensor urinal launched by GIBO for commercial restrooms, public toilets, and older-project renovation. Its biggest highlight is the **integrated surface-mounted structure**: the sensing module and water path are integrated into one unit, with no separate control box — just connect the water pipe and it is ready to use, greatly simplifying installation and reducing the failure points of scattered components.

The product adopts a **stainless steel panel** that is moisture-proof and corrosion-resistant, suited to humid restroom environments. It adopts GIBO's mature sensor solution with **intelligent compensation** — automatically adapting to ambient light and water-pressure changes to keep sensing stable. Combined with the slow-close structure and IPX7 triple waterproof, it is durable and reliable, suited to high-frequency commercial and public scenarios.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Integrated Surface-mounted Design

The sensing module and water path are integrated into one; no separate control box is needed. Surface-mounted installation exposes all components, making inspection and repair convenient and lowering the failure points of scattered components.

### 2.2 Moisture-proof Stainless Steel Panel

The stainless steel panel is moisture-proof and corrosion-resistant, maintaining a clean appearance even in long-term humid restroom environments, with a long service life.

### 2.3 Intelligent Compensation

The built-in algorithm automatically adapts to ambient light and water-pressure changes, keeping sensing stable and reducing missed or false triggers from environmental fluctuations.

### 2.4 Touchless Sensor Flush

Water flushes when a person leaves the sensing zone and stops automatically, with no need to touch the device throughout, reducing the spread of bacteria among users at the source.

### 2.5 Slow-close Shock Absorption

The valve closes in stages, slowly released, absorbing water-hammer energy and reducing noise, protecting the pipeline system.

### 2.6 IPX7 Triple Waterproof

The module adopts triple waterproof treatment, reaching IPX7 Waterproof (national standard IPX6), adapting to humid restroom environments.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction match the product
- Confirm the power supply method (DC battery / AC); for first use, recommend a full charge (battery version)
- Confirm the reserved pipe diameter matches (subject to official materials)

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- The sensing window must be kept clean to avoid affecting sensitivity
- Do not use steel wool on the stainless steel panel

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Confirm the wall water pipe position and inlet/outlet direction
2. Mount the surface-mounted integrated body and connect the water inlet/outlet pipes, checking for leaks
3. Install the battery or connect AC power
4. Power on, then test sensor auto flush
5. Confirm anti-odor flush and slow-close operation are normal

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, test sensor auto flush (triggered on departure); confirm the sensing window responds normally and the flush is stable.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Keep the sensing window clean; avoid dust/buildup affecting sensitivity
- Wipe the stainless steel panel with a soft cloth; do not use steel wool
- When the indicator blinks abnormally, charge / replace the battery promptly
