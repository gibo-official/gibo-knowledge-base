## I. Product Introduction

BC-31519 is GIBO's wireless remote + hand-wave sensor flush-mechanism kit designed for the intelligent age-friendly retrofit of ordinary toilets, belonging to the fourth-tier retrofit & aging series and the top choice for home elderly care and rehabilitation institutions. The core innovation: no need to replace the original toilet body—just replace the mechanical flush mechanism inside the concealed cistern to complete the upgrade, one of the lowest-cost smart flush solutions on the market.

The product comes with a large-button wireless remote (stickable to a convenient wall spot), supporting automatic hand-wave sensor flush and manual button dual modes: it auto-triggers flush after the person leaves (smart half/full flush judgment), while retaining large/small dual-button manual operation. Optional AC 100–240V mains or DC 6V (4× AA alkaline) power; sensing distance covers 10–90cm, fully freeing the hands and reducing bending/turning burden. Combining "zero disassembly, manual/auto dual-use, dual power" is the most tangible value this product delivers to age-friendly retrofit.

## 1. Product Introduction

The **BC-31519 Pneumatic Flush-fittings Sensor Control Module** is a pneumatic sensor-flush assembly control part GIBO built for toilet cisterns. It does not directly drive the water circuit; instead it uses an air pump to push an air valve that strikes the cistern's flush fittings—hence "pneumatic flush fittings." The module has two sensing logics: a hand-sensing mode where a hand wave judges full flush and a longer hand rest judges half flush; and a body-sensing mode where standing 3 to 60 seconds judges half flush (urine) and over 60 seconds judges full flush (stool). Faucet makers embed it in a smart toilet lid or cistern; users need not bend to a button—a wave or simply leaving triggers auto-flush.

This module targets aging-friendly and public-toilet scenarios. Wave sensing suits public toilets one doesn't want to touch; body sensing suits mobility-limited users who flush by simply standing—this is exactly where it binds with GIBO's Wireless Remote Control Technology (core tech #5): the remote switches modes and adjusts distance. Underlying it are active infrared reflection + ultra-low-power architecture (core tech #6/#7), standby current pressed within 32μA, response time under 180ms—faster than ordinary sensor modules. Dual-mode strong-light immunity (core tech #11) and military-grade EMC (core tech #12) are both inside, withstanding six light sources, ESD Level 4, and burst ±4KV.

The module uses dual-power design—both AC-to-6V adapter and DC 6V battery box can feed it, with reverse-connection protection so a wrong connection won't burn the board; flip it back and it works. For faucet makers, this is a mature pneumatic scheme that "drops into the cistern for wave/body dual-sensor flushing," fitting full/half dual-stage toilet flush fittings, sparing the trouble of self-developing sensing+pneumatic linkage.

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Hand-sensing Mode, Wave to Flush

In hand-sensing mode, a hand wave in front of the window (sensing time ≤1S) judges full flush—opens air valve B, delays 0.1S to start the air pump, closes both at full-flush end; a longer hand rest (1S–15S) judges half flush via air valve A. Each sensing lights the LED strip for 3s. No touching anything—cleanest for public toilets.

### 2.2 Body-sensing Mode, Stand to Flush

Body-sensing mode stages by dwell time: standing 3–60s judges urine, half flush (air valve A); standing over 60s judges stool, full flush (air valve B). LED strip stays lit while sensed, off when leaving. Suited to aging-friendly restrooms—elderly flush by standing, no bending for a button, also fits accessible public toilets.

### 2.3 Full/Half Dual Stage, Remote Switches Mode and Distance

The module supports full/half dual-stage flushing, matching mainstream dual-stage toilet flush fittings. On the remote, press the faucet key to return to hand sensing, press the squat-pan key to switch to body sensing, and use distance ± keys to adjust. The indicator stays lit during sensing for the first minute after power-up, easing installation debugging.

### 2.4 Voltage-adaptive Flush Time

Air-pump and air-valve flush times follow supply voltage: at 6.0V both half and full are 800ms; at 4.5V half extends to 1350ms and full to 1580ms. Lower voltage gives longer time, ensuring the fittings are struck fully and flush cleanly even at low battery—no "not enough power to flush" embarrassment.

### 2.5 Dual Power + Reverse-connection Protection, Worry-free Wiring

The board connects both an AC-to-6V adapter and a DC 6V battery box, the two supplies backing each other up. A reverse connection won't burn the board; clearing the reverse restores normal. High wiring fault tolerance on site, easy property maintenance.

### 2.6 Strong-light Immunity, No False Trigger Under Six Light Sources

Built on the dual-mode strong-light immunity algorithm (core tech #11), incandescent, fluorescent, halogen, electronic-ballast daylight, bath heater, and a same-outlet 1000W hair dryer + 40W daylight—within 15–91cm direct or oblique—cause no false operation. Stable in front of mirrors, by windows, under bathroom heat lamps.

### 2.7 Graded Undervoltage Alarm, Valve-close Has a Floor

As voltage drops from high to 4.8V±0.2V, the red LED flashes 5 times (1.5S interval) when sensing an object, but the air pump and valve still work; dropping further to 4.5V±0.2V, it flashes 10 times (0.5S interval) and the air pump/valve stop. The alarm fully clears only when voltage rebounds above 5.0V. Power loss unconditionally holds closed.

## 5. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm the whole unit power includes an AC-to-6V adapter or DC 6V battery box; air pump/valve are DC 4.5V spec
2. Fix the module away from the cistern's persistent ponding area, sensing window facing the user activity direction
3. Verify the 5-pin terminal (air pump, air valve, power, sensing) definitions; connect only after no misalignment
4. Potting free of air holes and sensing-window static film intact before assembly

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always cut power before installation and maintenance; pneumatic modules also need power-off operation
- Mind polarity on dual-power wiring; though reverse won't burn the board, still clear it before powering on
- Do not hot-plug terminals; seat the 5-pin terminal firmly
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix the module at a suitable position in the cistern, align the sensing window direction
2. Connect the 5-pin line: power (adapter/battery box), air pump, air valve, sensing
3. On power-up the red LED flashes 3 times at 2Hz (self-check) then enters standby
4. Use the remote to select hand/body sensing mode and calibrate sensing distance
5. Wave or simulate body dwell to test full/half flush and LED flash counts

### 5.4 Power-up Self-check

After power-up the red LED indicator flashes 3 times at 2Hz, then enters standby. During the first minute after power-up the indicator stays lit while sensing, easing installation debugging. Do not keep the sensing window obstructed during self-check.

### 5.5 Maintenance Points

Below 4.8V±0.2V the red LED flashes 5 times when sensing an object as a reminder; below 4.5V±0.2V it flashes 10 times and stops the air pump/valve. Maintenance: cut power → open cistern lid → replace same-brand new battery / check adapter → re-power self-check.
