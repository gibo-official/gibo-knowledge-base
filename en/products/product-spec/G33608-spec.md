## I. Product Introduction

G33608 is GIBO's countertop sensor foam soap dispenser for modern homes, belonging to the fifth-tier residential smart series (consumption-upgrade selection), suited to kitchen/bathroom countertop scenarios, nicknamed "小蛮腰 (Slim-Waist)"—with an elegant waist-cinched cylinder shape and white minimalist look that fits various home styles. The product has a built-in precision foam generator that turns ordinary liquid soap into dense, fine foam; each reach auto-dispenses foam with precise, non-wasteful dosing.

It uses a rechargeable lithium battery (3.7V / 1200mAh); one full charge lasts months, with no frequent battery change or cord constraint. The compact 76×115×185mm fits by the kitchen sink, bathroom counter, or dining prep area; the transparent soap window keeps track of the remaining level anytime. Combining "minimalist shape, dense foam, lithium battery no-install" is the most tangible value this product delivers to home users.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Sensing Auto-foam

Reach to ~5–10cm below the sensing window, auto-pump dense foam within 0.5s; stops when the hand leaves, no waste.

### 2.2 Precision Foam Generator

Gas-liquid mixing foaming structure; foam diameter uniform and fine (~50–100μm), large hand coverage, easy to rinse.

### 2.3 320ml Large-capacity Soap Bottle

One fill meets 2–4 weeks of daily family use (est. 20–30 uses/day); wide-mouth design for easy refill.

### 2.4 Rechargeable Lithium Battery

3.7V / 1200mAh lithium battery, low-power MCU control, typical life 3–6 months (to be verified, subject to actual test); supports Type-C or Micro-USB charging.

### 2.5 Modular Internal Structure

Six parts—top cover, control module, power board, liquid-feed mechanism (micro pump), shell, and liquid bottle—are independently detachable for easy maintenance and cleaning.

### 2.6 Anti-drip Design

Integrated pump-valve structure ensures no dripping when not dispensing, keeping the countertop tidy.

### 2.7 Multi-scenario Compatibility

Suited to kitchen dish soap / hand soap, bathroom body wash (diluted), restaurant disinfectant, and other liquid media (recommend low-viscosity liquid for best foaming).

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Countertop design, no hole drilling; prepare any flat countertop to place it
- Recommend dedicated foam hand soap or low-viscosity liquid soap (high-concentration original may clog the foaming mesh)
- Confirm charging port type (to be verified, expected Type-C / Micro-USB)

### 5.2 Precautions

- High-concentration original may clog the foaming mesh; use low-viscosity liquid
- A stained sensing window reduces sensitivity; wipe regularly with a dry soft cloth
- For long-term disuse, recommend emptying the soap and storing fully charged to avoid battery over-discharge damage

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Take out the main unit
2. Add an appropriate amount of foam hand soap via the bottom fill port
3. First power-on self-check (if a power button exists)
4. Place on a flat countertop to use

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

First power-on self-check (if a power button exists); low-battery indicator blinks to remind (specific prompt to be verified); connect the charging cable to a USB power adapter, full charge takes ~2–4 hours (to be verified).

### 5.5 Maintenance & Care

- Soap change: unscrew the bottom soap bottle cap counterclockwise or refill via the fill port; recommend dedicated foam hand soap or low-viscosity liquid soap
- Daily cleaning: wipe the shell with a damp cloth; wipe the sensing window regularly with a dry soft cloth
- Long-term storage: if unused for a long time, recommend emptying the soap and storing fully charged; re-fill with fresh soap before reuse
