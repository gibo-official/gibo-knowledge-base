## I. Product Introduction

The GBL-K6238 Integrated Urinal Sensor Flush Valve is GIBO's industry-first integrated-design product launched in 2012, highly integrating the sensor, power supply, and water pipe into one compact body. Installation requires connecting only one water pipe, shortening installation time by over 70% versus traditional split-type; no exposed screws, no separate control box, greatly eliminating hygiene dead corners. It supports AC/DC dual use and remote adjustment, with optional dTOF laser / millimeter-wave sensing, a service life of up to 0.5–1.0 million cycles, making it an ideal choice for large public restrooms such as stadiums, exhibition centers, and transport hubs.

Stadiums, exhibition centers, transport hubs, and commercial complexes — these large public-restroom spaces have huge foot traffic and short maintenance windows, and fear most cumbersome installation, frequent equipment failure, and hard-to-clean hygiene dead corners. The K6238 is built for such large-scale, high-load public environments. It combines sensor, power, and water pipe into one, installed by connecting one water pipe; AC/DC dual use auto-switches to battery on mains outage; optional dTOF laser (accuracy 1–3mm, distance error <5% across materials) or automotive-grade millimeter-wave (no sensing window, avoiding scratches/damage), with two-stage flush plus 24h-once anti-odor flush to maintain the pipeline water seal.

To sum up the above: the K6238 lets large public restrooms "install with one water pipe, keep running even on power outage, last ten years without failure and with no dead corners." The three points below are the product values we think are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Integrated Structure

Sensor, power supply, and water pipe are integrated into a single body, with no separate control box, a clean appearance, easy cleaning, and no hygiene dead corners.

### 2.2 AC/DC Dual Power Supply

Input power AC100–224V or DC6V (4× AA batteries); the power adapter and battery box are connected in parallel; on mains outage it automatically switches to battery power, ensuring continuous operation.

### 2.3 Two-stage Flush

The working mode is two-stage flush (front 2s + rear 6s), adjustable via remote control; an additional 24h-once anti-odor flush maintains the pipeline water seal.

### 2.4 Remote Intelligent Adjustment

Outlet mode, outlet time, and sensing distance are all adjustable via remote control; the memory mode is not reset after restart, convenient for operation and maintenance.

### 2.5 Power-loss Protection and Waterproofing

Auto shut-off within 10–20 seconds of power disconnection; the sensing module adopts triple waterproofing, rated IPX7 or above (national standard IPX6).

### 2.6 Ultra-low-power Smart Sensing Technology

Exclusive LITEON Smart Sensing Technology with sensor standby power ≤10μA, below the national standard 33μA (0.2mW), extending battery life by 50%.

### 2.7 Strong Anti-interference and High Protection

Enhanced EMC testing, 25 light-interference tests, full-band immunity and ESD testing; panel salt spray grade ASS 24H/grade 10, panel material is all-brass/chrome or 304 stainless steel.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

- Confirm the inlet pipe is a G1/2 interface; the integrated body needs no separate control box or exposed wiring
- Confirm the power supply method: mains (AC100–224V) and battery box (DC6V) in parallel; the power adapter is CCC-certified
- Applicable water pressure 0.05–0.7MPa; panel material optional all-brass/chrome or 304 stainless steel

### 5.2 Precautions

- Always shut off the water path and power before installation and maintenance
- The AC version has a separate power switch per unit and must be reliably grounded
- Avoid hard-object scratches on the panel; do not hot-plug the sensing module terminals with power on

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Connect the integrated body to one inlet pipe (G1/2); no separate control box or exposed wiring needed
2. Connect mains (AC100–224V) and battery box (DC6V) in parallel; auto-switches on outage
3. Set outlet mode, outlet time, and sensing distance via remote control (memory retained)
4. Power-on self-check: the solenoid valve opens and closes once, then enters standby
5. Wave a hand in front of the sensing window to test outlet and two-stage flush

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After powering on, the solenoid valve opens and closes once instantly, then enters standby; the remote control can set outlet mode, outlet time, and sensing distance with retained memory.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- The sensing module is triple waterproof (IPX7 or above), panel salt spray grade 10; daily wiping with a soft cloth is sufficient
- Auto shut-off within 10–20 seconds of power loss; solenoid valve self-cleans and anti-clogs, stable and reliable under complex water quality
