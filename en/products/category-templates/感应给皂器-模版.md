## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Soap Dispenser is installed on public restroom wash counters or wall brackets—when a hand is placed under the outlet nozzle, the device auto-extrudes a fixed amount of hand soap or foam, never pressing buttons or touching the body throughout. It solves the three most awkward and realistic problems at the public hand-washing step: everyone touches the manual press soap dispenser, posing big cross-infection risk; inconsistent press force causes variable dispensing, both wasteful and dirtying the counter; and the pump clogs after a while, the soap bottle empties unnoticed, and batteries always need replacement. Shopping malls, office buildings, airports and stations, star-rated hotels, hospitals, schools, and restaurant back kitchens—these places have large foot traffic, high hygiene demands, and most fear equipment that "fails to dispense when it should, wild-drips when it shouldn't." The Sensor Soap Dispenser is built for such high-frequency, open, decor-conscious environments.

GIBO's approach to the Sensor Soap Dispenser cores on "stable sensing, accurate dispensing, no dripping, low maintenance." For sensing, it uses infrared reflection with the Liteon Agile algorithm, filtering water splash and flying-insect false triggers, dispensing on reach with response under half a second; high-end water-soap integrated models (e.g., GBL-6178) further use dTOF Laser Time-of-Flight ranging, recognizing people normally even under strong light. For dispensing, it uses a micro DC metering pump or pressure-balanced dispensing structure, single dose 0.5–1.5ml (liquid type) adjustable, foam type 0.5–2.5ml adjustable, enough without waste. The circuitry uses ultra-low-power management, 4 AA batteries lasting over 12 months, installed under the counter or in a wall box requiring essentially no attention; the pump body has a self-cleaning anti-clogging structure, resisting thicker hand soap and complex water quality without jamming. In summary: the Sensor Soap Dispenser makes public wash counters "soap on reach, quantitative no waste, no worry for a year after installation." The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

### 2.1 Dispense on Reach, Non-contact No Soiling

When a hand is placed under the outlet nozzle, infrared or laser sensing triggers the pump to dispense, never touching the body or pressing buttons throughout. The Liteon Agile algorithm monitors environmental noise in real time, dynamically adjusting the detection threshold—higher sensitivity in quiet environments, lower false triggering in noisy ones—so water splash and flying insects won't cause wasted dispensing. For public wash counters, this is the most direct link cutting hand cross-contact.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Dispense on Reach Non-contact, No Dirty Hands Cross-infection in Public Restrooms

Manual soap dispensers are pressed by everyone; the bacteria on the button equal those of the hand washers, making hand washing itself a cross-infection step. The Sensor Soap Dispenser dispenses on reach, never touching throughout, fundamentally cleaning the "last step of hand washing." This is most direct for hospitals, schools, and restaurant back kitchens: infection control and food-safety audits both recognize non-contact, easing both janitorial and acceptance work.

### Selling Point 2: Quantitative Dispense No Drip, One Bottle Lasts a Month Longer

Old press dispensers dispense inconsistently; one extra press floods the counter, both wasteful and hard to clean. The Sensor Soap Dispenser quantifies 0.5–1.5ml per dispense, plus outlet sealing no continuous drip; a 500ml soap bottle at an office wash basin with hundreds of daily users lasts a month longer, lowering refill frequency and saving both consumables and labor. For facilities managing dozens of restrooms, the savings add up substantially.

### Selling Point 3: Four Batteries Last a Year, Fewer Trips for Facility Staff

4×AA alkaline batteries last over 12 months, wiring-free, no power connection, old-building renovations can be stuck on directly. Do the math: a commercial complex with 50 wash basins, if soap dispensers needed monthly battery replacement, would require considerable labor hours a year just climbing ladders; the long-life solution replaces once a year, essentially zeroing that labor. The battery solution also solves the old-toilet renovation dilemma of no reserved power.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. Unpack and verify main unit, soap bottle, bracket/installation accessories, power (battery or adapter).
2. Confirm power supply: battery model insert 4 same-brand alkaline AA; AC model confirm transformer spec AC100–240V.
3. Confirm soap type (liquid/foam) matches model, do not mix.
4. Installation position on wash counter or wall flat and load-bearing; countertop model reserve hole size.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Always disconnect power before installation and maintenance; AC model each unit separate power switch and reliable grounding.
- ⚠️ Do not fill soap bottle with strong acid/alkali or granular abrasive liquid, to avoid clogging pump.
- ⚠️ Use same-brand alkaline batteries; do not mix old/new, do not mix rechargeable and alkaline.
- ⚠️ Do not point sensing window directly at sunlight or strong lamps; no obstruction larger than 1cm within 15cm in front.
- ⚠️ Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Select countertop/wall position, mark drilling or adhesive fix.
2. Fix bracket/base, install soap bottle and tighten.
3. Insert batteries or connect adapter, connect sensing module.
4. Power-on self-check (see 5.4).
5. Reach to test dispense, adjust dispense volume gear.
6. Reinstall panel/decorative cover.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → pump body actuates once (exhaust self-check) → enters standby. For first bottle installation, suggest multiple empty presses to expel air, ensuring continuous dispense without flow-break bubbles. During learning/exhaust, do not continuously block the sensing window.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- For long-term disuse, empty soap bottle and rinse pump body with clean water to prevent drying and clogging.
- After low-battery prompt, replace batteries promptly: disconnect power → open cover → remove old batteries → insert same-brand new batteries → re-self-check.
- Decreased or interrupted dispense is mostly pump air intake or overly thick soap; expel air or replace with thinner liquid.
- Outlet seal ring suggested replacement every 1–2 years (corresponding to ZL2022 2 2532561.9 sealing structure).

## VI. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Shopping Mall / Shopping Center

Weekends and holidays bring dense crowds; quantitative dispensing extends a bottle's life, noticeably lowering refill frequency; countertop all-brass model matches high-end decor, laser model works normally under mall atrium skylight direct light; battery solution wiring-free, tenant-area renovation install-and-use.

### 6.2 Office Building / Corporate Headquarters

Experience-sensitive users, dispense on reach, no button no soiling; dispense volume personalizable by floor usage frequency; concealed stainless steel/all-brass panel matches high-end office decor, decent and hygienic.

### 6.3 Airport / High-speed Railway Station

24-hour operation, short maintenance windows. Full-condition adaptability, stores at -10℃~55℃, works at 0℃~45℃; stable under direct sunlight, escalators, security equipment; large-capacity wall-mounted model reduces refill frequency, suitable for high-frequency public areas.

### 6.4 Star-rated Hotel / Club

Non-contact dispense with no visible action, quiet and elegant; all-brass chrome model matches high-end decor; dual power compatibility, rooms can use adapter permanent power, public areas battery wiring-free; anti-drip design keeps counter dry long-term.

### 6.5 Hospital / Elderly Care

Fully automatic non-contact meets infection control; unaffected by clothing material, dispense on reach; low-soap and low-battery protection plus anti-overflow power cutoff, auto cutoff on abnormality prevents leakage; elderly-friendly places can use larger dispense gear for easier hand washing.

### 6.6 School / Venue

Quantitative dispensing prevents students from playful over-pressing waste; battery solution wiring-free, suitable for old school-building renovation; outlet sealing anti-drip keeps counter easy to maintain; 500,000-cycle pump life rugged, not easily broken during vacation vacancy.

### 6.7 Food Factory / Restaurant Back Kitchen

Back-kitchen hand washing is a hard food-safety audit indicator; non-contact dispense eliminates hand secondary contamination; lower single gear in high-frequency usage extends soap period; pump body self-cleaning anti-clogging structure tolerates kitchen humidity and oily-mist environments, resisting pump jam.
