## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Foam Hand Washer is installed at hand-hygiene points in public restrooms, food workshops, and medical facilities—foam hand soap auto-extrudes when a hand reaches in, never touching the pump head throughout. It solves the most practical problems in these high-frequency hand-washing scenarios: cross-contamination from pressing pump heads, uncontrolled dispensing volume causing consumable waste, the maintenance burden of alkaline batteries dying in months, and the awkwardness of multiple devices falsely triggering each other when installed side by side.

Shopping malls, office buildings, airports and stations, star-rated hotels, hospitals, schools, food factories, and laboratories—these places see heavy daily foot traffic with high hand-washing frequency and sensitivity to both hygiene and consumable cost, and most fear equipment that "fails to dispense when it should, wild-dispense when it shouldn't." The Sensor Foam Hand Washer is built for such high-frequency, open, cost-controlled environments.

It uses infrared sensing (high-end models optional dTOF Laser ranging), extruding foam when a hand approaches, factory sensing distance about 10 cm, response 256 ms; the output is foam, paired with dedicated foam hand soap, covering more evenly and saving more than dilute liquid. The control board standby current is suppressed to ≤33μA (converted power ≤0.2mW), 4 alkaline batteries lasting 6~12 months, no monthly ladder climbs for facility staff. Foam dispense has two gears: 0.8s and 1.3s, factory default 0.8s, double-click to switch gear; the pump body has a self-cleaning anti-clogging structure, resisting even complex soap liquids without jamming. Combined with the Liteon Agile algorithm and dual-mode strong-light immunity, multiple units side by side don't cross-talk and work normally under window-side strong light; power loss auto-stops the pump, no awkward leakage flooding the counter.

In summary: the Sensor Foam Hand Washer makes public hand-hygiene points "foam on reach, hygienic without contact, no battery change for a year, no cross-talk with multiple side-by-side units." The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Infrared Dual-zone Sensing, Non-contact Foam

Foam auto-dispenses when a hand reaches in front of the sensing window, never touching the device. Reduces cross-infection in public, and avoids the jamming and hand-soiling of press pump heads. Dual-sensing models have upper and lower sensing zones respectively managing "reach to dispense" and "supplement/dispense long," logically interlocked so a hand wave won't wildly spill a puddle of foam.

### 2.2 Dual-gear Foam Dispense, 0.8 / 1.3 Second Adjustable

Foam dispense time set in two gears: gear 1 0.8s, gear 2 1.3s, factory default gear 1. Default gear 1 on startup, double-click to switch, blue light indicates gear 2. High-frequency places use short gear to save liquid, heavy-soil scenarios use long gear for more foam, controlling usage themselves.

- Gear 1 0.8s: regular hand washing, single dispense about 0.5~0.8ml
- Gear 2 1.3s: heavy oil / food processing scenarios, single dispense about 1.0~1.5ml
- Switching: double-click power key, blue light on indicates gear 2

Control board standby current ≤33μA, converted standby power ≤0.2mW, with 4 alkaline batteries or rechargeable lithium battery, can go half a year to a year between replacements in daily use. A major item can be crossed off the facility maintenance list.

### 2.4 Graded Low-voltage Alarm, Reminder for Battery Change

When battery voltage drops below 4.5V±0.1, the current gear light flashes twice per use (once every 0.5s) to remind battery replacement; on sensing undervoltage the gear light flashes 5 times. Graded early warning lets operation arrange battery replacement in advance, no sudden stop mid-use.

- Low-battery Early Warning: voltage < 4.5V±0.1, gear light flashes 2 times
- Undervoltage Protection: continuous below threshold auto-stops pump, avoiding dry-run pump damage
- Maintenance Suggestion: replace batteries in batches to avoid scattered downtime

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. Confirm power supply: DC6V (4×AA alkaline battery) or DC3.7V lithium battery or AC100-240V adapter, batteries same batch, no old/new mixing.
2. Confirm use of dedicated foam hand soap; ordinary hand soap must be diluted 1:4, soap with particles will clog pump.
3. Do not point sensing window directly at strong light source, avoid continuous strong light within 1 meter.
4. Verify control board and liquid pump action by separate power-on before full assembly.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Disconnect power before assembly/disassembly; do not plug/unplug battery box / power terminals under power.
- ⚠️ Connect liquid pump inlet/outlet pipes per drawing; reversed connection causes no dispense or leakage.
- ⚠️ Remove battery for long-term disuse to prevent leakage corroding board; for long-term disuse suggest emptying soap circuit to avoid drying.
- Sensing window protective film from factory, remove as needed after installation.
- Soap contains no impurities, otherwise easily clogs aerator.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix control board / main unit into cavity, connect liquid pump and battery box terminals.
2. Insert liquid bottle, confirm pump tube inserted below liquid surface, no bending.
3. Install sensing window component, align with control board sensing zone.
4. Insert 4 same-batch alkaline batteries (or connect adapter), close battery compartment.
5. Tap power key 3 seconds to turn on (green light on, default gear 1).
6. Reach in front of sensing window to verify foam, double-click to verify switch to gear 2 (blue light).
7. For multiple units side by side, verify no mutual false trigger one by one.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, green light steady indicates gear 1 ready; double-click power key blue light indicates switch to gear 2. For first use, suggest continuous sensing 5~8 times until stable foam, expelling circuit air bubbles. At low battery the gear light flashes per rules—normal early warning, not fault.

### 5.5 Maintenance

When voltage drops below 4.5V±0.1, gear light flashes 2 times to remind battery replacement. Replacement: shut down → open battery compartment → remove old batteries, replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → restart and verify. Replenish soap bottle promptly when near empty, avoiding dry-run pump damage; if outlet clogged, first sense empty several times to clear, then use formally. Suggest batch battery replacement and refill to avoid scattered downtime.

**Solution Advantages**: About 2s false-trigger rejection window filters brief obstructions, no false trigger from play; battery solution wiring-free, suitable for old school-building renovation; pump body life ≥100,000 cycles, whole unit ≥250,000 cycles, rugged and durable.

### 6.7 Food Factory / Restaurant Back Kitchen

**Typical Needs**: Must wash hands before entering workshop, hands often oily or floury, press bottles inconvenient and unhygienic.
**Solution Advantages**: Non-contact soap take, chefs need not shake hands; soap adjustable thickness, fits detergent dilution, flexible cost control; dual-gear dispense calls volume by heavy-oil scenarios, neither wasteful nor insufficient.

### 6.8 Laboratory / Clean Room

**Typical Needs**: High cleanliness requirements, zero tolerance for cross-contamination.
**Solution Advantages**: Fully zero-contact soap take, reducing pollution source introduction; quantitative foam easy to rinse, less residue; power-loss pump stop and overtime protection, avoiding unattended continuous flow polluting counter and environment.
