## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Spout is a miniaturized non-contact water-out terminal installed on basin faucet and kitchen faucet outlets—water auto-flows when hands approach and delays close when withdrawn, never touching any switch throughout. It doesn't solve the basic question of "whether there is sensing," but the three most troublesome engineering issues of old-style sensor faucets: the whole basin faucet must be disassembled and replaced to become smart, kitchen and medical countertop rewiring and re-plumbing is too disruptive, and batteries need ladder-climbing replacement every two or three months. The Sensor Spout compresses the sensing module, solenoid valve, and battery box into a palm-sized quick-install shell, directly screwed onto the original faucet outlet, turning an ordinary faucet into a sensor faucet in thirty seconds.

Home kitchen prep, office restrooms, hospital examination rooms and lab benches, laboratories and industrial hand-washing points—these places need not a fancy whole unit but a precise, reliable, install-anywhere water point. Kitchens need directional no-splash, medical and labs need reach-in without cross-contamination, public basins need maintenance-free fewer complaints; the Sensor Spout fits right into these scenarios.

It uses GIBO's self-developed Low-power Multi-stable Agile Sensing Technology (standby power just over 0.2mW, 18μA level), with a dual-sensing layout—side short-distance sensing prevents false trigger, bottom long-distance sensing prevents missed flow; the two logics cooperate to make "flow when should, no flow when shouldn't" solid. High-end models feature dTOF Laser ranging, material- and strong-light-independent; the solenoid valve uses a self-cleaning anti-clogging structure, resisting both northern scaling and southern sediment; the whole unit also has 60-second overtime auto water-cutoff anti-overflow protection, cutting water first if circuits fail. The charging version uses Type-C lithium battery, one charge for nine months, no need to remember battery replacement.

In summary: the Sensor Spout turns "making an ordinary faucet smart" from a plumbing renovation into a threaded quick-install. The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. Confirm original faucet outlet spec is M22 / M24 (basin) or G1/2" (kitchen pull-out), select corresponding adapter ring.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa – 0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa needs boost.
3. Lithium version confirm sufficient charge or charge once first; battery version prepare same-brand new batteries.
4. No fixed obstruction within 30cm in front of sensing window, no direct strong lamp.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Before installation, first close the faucet inlet angle valve; prepare to catch water when unscrewing the original aerator, avoiding residual water flow.
- ⚠️ Lithium version must be fully charged before first use, to avoid frequent low-voltage valve close in half-charged state.
- ⚠️ Install the adapter ring sealing ring correctly; crooked tightening causes leakage.
- ⚠️ Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power; power on only after shell is installed.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Close angle valve, unscrew original faucet aerator/nozzle, clean outlet threads.
2. Fit corresponding adapter ring (M22/M24 or G1/2"), install sealing washer.
3. Screw the Sensor Spout body into the adapter ring, tighten by hand until not loose.
4. Lithium version connect battery compartment or charge first; battery version insert same-brand new batteries.
5. Open angle valve to test water, confirm no leakage at connection.
6. Power-on self-check (see 5.4), wave in front of sensing window to test water start/stop.
7. Fine-tune sensing distance by usage habit (knob or remote, by model).

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → solenoid valve opens/closes once instantly → enters about 1 minute learning mode (LED steady on) → transitions to normal standby. During learning mode, do not continuously block the sensing window, allowing it to calibrate the environmental baseline.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement / Charging

- Lithium version: when low, LED flashes to prompt; charge with Type-C cable about 1.5 hours for about 9 months use; close angle valve → remove body → charge → reinstall and self-check.
- Battery version: when voltage below threshold, LED flashes to remind battery replacement; close angle valve → open shell → remove battery compartment, replace same-brand new AAA batteries → reinstall and re-self-check.
