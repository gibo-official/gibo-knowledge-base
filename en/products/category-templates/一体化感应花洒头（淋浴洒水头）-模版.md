## I. Product Introduction

The Integrated Sensor Shower Head (Shower Sprinkler) integrates the sensor controller, solenoid valve, and shower spray body into a single terminal—water flows when you reach in or wave your hand, and automatically shuts off after a delay when you leave, with no need to touch any switch or knob throughout. It solves the most aggravating problems in public bathrooms, gyms, and pool changing rooms: switch surfaces covered in limescale and soap, cross-infection from many people sharing one showerhead, wasted water from forgetting to turn it off, and the elderly or children being unable to reach or twist the knob.

Shopping malls, office buildings, transportation hubs, star-rated hotels, hospitals, schools, and sports venues have shower areas with heavy daily foot traffic and high demands for hygiene and reliability, yet they most fear equipment that "fails to deliver when it should, or sprays wildly when it shouldn't." The Integrated Sensor Shower Head is built precisely for such high-frequency, open, maintenance-sensitive, and often high-humidity steam environments.

By default it features GIBO's 3rd-generation dTOF Laser Sensing Technology, which is fundamentally different from old-style infrared sensing: infrared judges presence by "seeing how strong the reflected light is," and easily misfires when facing dark tiles, stainless steel shower rods, or steam and mist; dTOF directly measures the time-of-flight of a laser bouncing off the human body, achieving millimeter-level accuracy that is unaffected by material and unfazed by water mist or steam. The circuitry employs ultra-low power management—four AA batteries last over 12 months, and once installed inside the showerhead cavity or a concealed ceiling junction box, it essentially requires no attention. Options also include Smart Thermostatic Control (temperature accuracy ±1.5°C) and a wireless remote—the former locks the water temperature steady even when multiple points draw water simultaneously in a public restroom, while the latter lets facility staff set modes and temperatures right in front of the shower area without removing any panel.

In summary: the Integrated Sensor Shower Head makes public showering "spray accurately when it should, waste nothing when it shouldn't, and run for a year with little attention." The three product values below are what we consider most worth highlighting.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. First open the water to flush the pipeline, removing sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid valve and shower nozzle.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa ~ 0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa requires a booster pump; thermostatic models require confirmation of dual hot/cold water supply.
3. Do not point the sensing window directly at strong light or bright lamps; avoid fixed obstructions larger than 1cm within the forward sensing cone.
4. For concealed/ceiling models, install the pre-embedded box and piping before tiling or sealing the ceiling.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Always shut off the water circuit and power before installation and maintenance.
- ⚠️ Each AC-powered unit must have a separate power switch and reliable grounding; never plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power.
- ⚠️ Thermostatic models must not reverse hot and cold water lines, otherwise temperature control fails or delivers scalding water.
- ⚠️ Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new, or reverse polarity.
- ⚠️ After installation in high-humidity steam environments, confirm the sensing window potting is intact to avoid long-term condensate infiltration.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Connect the hot and cold water inlet/outlet pipes and shower rod/overhead spray per the reserved interfaces.
2. Level and fix the showerhead or overhead spray body.
3. Open water for pressure test, confirm no leakage at piping and connections.
4. Install the sensing module (built-in dTOF laser window facing the sensing zone), connect power (battery box or adapter).
5. After power-on, LED flashes once, solenoid valve opens/closes once (self-check) then enters standby.
6. Use remote to set sensing distance, outlet mode, and set temperature (thermostatic models).
7. Test water-out and shutoff by reaching/waving in front of the sensing cone.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → solenoid valve opens/closes instantly → enters about 1 minute learning mode (LED steady on) → transitions to normal standby. During learning mode, do not continuously block the sensing window, allowing the module to complete environmental baseline calibration.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement (for battery models)

When voltage drops below the reminder threshold, the LED flashes several times per sensing to remind battery replacement; below the valve-close threshold it flashes more times and auto-closes the valve. To replace: shut water → remove battery box → replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-self-check. Adapter-powered models need no battery replacement, but must confirm grounding and waterproof connector sealing.
