## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Shower is installed in public bathrooms, gyms, hotels, and pool facilities—water flows when a person approaches and stops when they leave, never touching any switch throughout, and can stabilize the water temperature to a set value before outflow. It solves the three major troubles of public showering: water sprays wildly before the person is even positioned, water temperature fluctuates hot and cold with multiple alternating users, and the cross-infection and continuous-flow waste from manual switches in public places. Mall attached bathrooms, office fitness floors, high-speed station rest areas, star-rated hotel room bathrooms, hospital care rooms, school dormitories, and sports venue changing rooms—these places have high usage frequency, mixed personnel, and sensitive maintenance, most fearing "no flow when should, wild flow when shouldn't, scalding on outflow." GIBO's approach to this product category core-combines three things: non-contact sensing start/stop (using dTOF Laser or triangulation, material- and strong-light-independent), Smart Thermostatic Control (PID algorithm adjusting hot/cold ratio in real time, accuracy ±1.5℃), and wireless remote centralized parameter setting (one remote adjusts temperature, sensing distance, and mode through walls). The circuitry uses a Low-power Multi-stable architecture, whole-unit standby power ≤0.2mW; the valve body uses a low water hammer soft-close structure, quiet opening/closing without piping vibration; the sensing zone is three-layer potted to IP65 waterproof, working normally in high-humidity steam environments. In summary: the Sensor Shower makes public showers "flow only when a person comes, deliver stable temperature, stop when they leave, and need little attention after installation." The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Non-contact Start/Stop, Off on Release No Water Waste

Public showers fear two things: cross-infection from manual switches, and water still spraying after the person leaves. The Sensor Shower flows on entry, stops on leave, never touching switches; the 2-second false-trigger rejection window filters brief obstructions from changing-room running and hanging-rack swaying, no false spray. At an estimated 8 minutes per shower, about 9L/min outflow, "off on release" vs manual continuous flow saves dozens of liters per unit per day, thousands of tons a year for a 50-stall public bathroom—beyond water bills, fewer maintenance trips are real savings.

### Selling Point 2: ±1.5℃ Precise Thermostat, No Scalding Across Time Slots

The most complained issue with traditional public showers is hot/cold fluctuation—temperature jumps when a neighbor flushes, easily scalding the elderly and children. GIBO's PID thermostat compensates at the millisecond level of water pressure spikes, temperature control accuracy ±1.5℃, with over-temperature limiting and periodic self-calibration. For operators, this means fewer scald complaints, more stable experience ratings, and better fit with hotels and hospitals that have hard water-safety requirements.

### Selling Point 3: Wireless Remote Centralized Parameter Setting, One Trip for Maintenance

One remote adjusts temperature, sensing distance, and water-saving mode through walls, no per-unit panel removal. A facility managing dozens of shower rooms previously climbed ladders per unit to tune; now walks around with the remote and finishes; when water temperature needs seasonal fine-tuning, no shutdown or disassembly. Combined with low-power battery life, one battery change a year compresses the two most labor-intensive public-shower tasks—parameter setting and battery swap—to the minimum.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, removing sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid valve.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa ~ 0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa requires a booster pump.
3. Do not point the sensing window directly at sunlight and strong lamps; avoid obstructions larger than 1cm within 120cm in front.
4. Concealed model pre-embedded box installed before tiling; exposed model confirm wall load-bearing and inlet/outlet positions.
5. Confirm hot/cold water line markings, left hot right cold (or per site marking) connected correctly, avoiding reversed thermostat.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Always shut off water circuit and power before installation and maintenance.
- ⚠️ Each AC-powered unit must have a separate power switch and reliable grounding; forbid sharing circuit with other high-power equipment.
- ⚠️ Reversing hot/cold causes thermostat failure and overheated outflow; verify before wiring.
- Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new.
- Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Cut groove per pre-embedded box/exposed base size, place box, connect hot/cold inlet/outlet pipes.
2. Level and fix valve body and control box.
3. Open water for pressure test, confirm no leakage, correct hot/cold direction.
4. After tile or panel dries, remove protective cover.
5. Connect power (battery box or adapter), install sensing panel and showerhead/nozzle.
6. After power-on, LED flashes once, solenoid valve opens/closes once (self-check) then enters standby.
7. Use remote to set sensing distance, target water temperature, water-out duration.
8. Wave/approach in front of sensing zone to test water-out and close.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → solenoid valve opens/closes instantly → enters 1 minute learning mode (LED steady on) → transitions to normal standby. During learning mode, do not continuously block the sensing window, allowing the device to complete environmental baseline calibration. For first power-on, suggest one thermostat point calibration via remote.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- When voltage drops below 4.8V, the LED reminds battery replacement per sensing; below 4.5V auto valve close. Replacement: shut water → open panel → remove battery box, replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-self-check.
- If outflow decreases, first check if filter and valve core are scaling; self-cleaning structure auto-scoures, severe scaling just disassemble and clean valve core.
- For long-term disuse (e.g., pool off-season), shut water and power; after restart do one self-check before use.
