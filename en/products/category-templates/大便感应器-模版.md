## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Toilet Flusher handles the dirtiest and most error-intolerant task in public restrooms: when a person stands up, the squat pan or toilet auto-flushes, never touching any switch throughout. It covers a wider range of scenarios than urinal flushers—beyond conventional public restroom stalls, it includes trench-type public toilets with a row of pans flushed centrally, ordinary toilet aging-friendly renovation, and renovation projects in old public toilets wanting automation without breaking walls to re-pipe. The product forms are also varied: concealed flush valves bury the valve body and water circuit into a pre-embedded wall box, leaving only a stainless steel or glass panel outside; pneumatic water fittings install directly into ordinary toilet tanks, turning old toilets into sensor-flush; and there are dual-mode models with wireless remote, triggerable by both waving and remote, specifically for the mobility-impaired elderly.

Public places require just three things from a toilet flusher: no misjudgment in stalls, clean flush even at low pressure, and no frequent breakdowns after installation. GIBO's Sensor Toilet Flusher uses Triangulation Ranging Sensing Technology to solve stall recognition—traditional infrared loses signal when a person turns around in a stall and easily false-flushes from partition reflection, while triangulation directly calculates distance and position via the fixed geometric angle between emitter and receiver, unaffected by clothing color or partition reflection; low-pressure environments use a low water hammer high-flush valve body that still delivers stable flow at 0.2MPa; the self-cleaning anti-clogging valve core uses flow to self-wash away scaling and sediment at each open/close, reducing failure rates by 70% in poor water quality areas. The circuitry uses ultra-low-power Multi-stable solution, with 4 AA batteries lasting 8~12 months, combined with Smart Anti-overflow Power Cutoff protection achieving 99.5% valve-closing success rate on actual leakage. In summary: GIBO's Sensor Toilet Flusher makes public restrooms "flush clean when people leave, no wild flushing when they shouldn't, flushable even at low pressure, and no frequent repairs for a year after installation." The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Triangulation Ranging, Accurate Recognition Even in Stalls

The Sensor Toilet Flusher is installed in squat stall cubicles, an environment far more complex than urinals: as a person squats, stands, and turns, the relative position to the sensing window keeps changing, and partitions reflect light. GIBO uses triangulation: a fixed geometric angle between emitter and receiver directly calculates the person's position in the cubicle via light offset, not relying on reflected light intensity, so dark clothing, partition reflection, and half-standing persons won't cause misjudgment. GBL-8300AD and GBL-8307AD both use this, validated through three-plus years of mass production at high-speed stations, airports, and hospitals.

### 2.2 Low Water Hammer High Flush, Clean Flush Even at Low Pressure

Public buildings, especially old buildings and high-rises, often have insufficient water pressure; ordinary solenoid valves collapse flow at low pressure and fail to clean. ### 2.5 Smart Anti-overflow Power Cutoff, Auto Valve Close on Leakage

Public places most fear a flush valve that won't close and runs continuously, flooding the floor and unable to stop. The most direct benefit for facility staff: fewer repair tickets and complaints, no need to adjust decor layout specifically for stall sensing.

Many public toilets in old-building high-rises have low water pressure where ordinary flush valves collapse flow and fail to clean, becoming dirtier. In poor water quality areas, solenoid valve clogging is the bulk of sensor sanitary ware after-sales; GIBO's self-cleaning valve core washes away scaling and sediment at each open/close, reducing failure rate by over 70%; combined with Smart Anti-overflow Power Cutoff, actual leakage valve-closing success rate 99.5%, no floor flooding. ## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, removing sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid valve.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa ~ 0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa requires a booster pump.
3. Do not point the sensing window directly at sunlight and strong lamps; avoid obstructions larger than 1cm within 120cm in front.
4. Concealed model pre-embedded box (e.g., BC-YMH-8308) installed and connected before tiling.
5. For ordinary toilet renovation with pneumatic water fitting (e.g., BC-31519), first confirm tank size matches valve body height.

### 5.2 Precautions

⚠️ Always shut off water circuit and power before installation and maintenance
⚠️ Each AC-powered unit must have a separate power switch and reliable grounding
⚠️ Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new
⚠️ Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power
⚠️ Pneumatic water fitting air pump DC4.5V/650mA, keep separate from main control power supply, do not connect wrong voltage

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Concealed model: cut groove per pre-embedded box size, place box, connect inlet/outlet pipes (G1")
2. Level and fix, open water for pressure test confirming no leakage
3. After tile dries, remove protective cover
4. Connect power (battery box or adapter), install sensing panel
5. After power-on, LED flashes once, solenoid valve opens/closes once (self-check) then enters standby
6. Use remote to set sensing distance and flush time
7. Wave in front of sensing window / use remote to test flush
8. Pneumatic dual-mode model additionally connects air pump and tests wave + remote dual-mode trigger

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → solenoid valve opens/closes instantly → enters 1 minute learning mode (LED steady on) → transitions to normal standby. During learning mode, do not continuously block the sensing window. Pneumatic dual-mode model also verifies air pump path and remote pairing status during self-check.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement (for battery-powered models)

When voltage is below 4.8V, the LED flashes 5 times per sensing to remind battery replacement; below 4.5V it flashes 10 times and auto-closes the valve. To replace: shut water → open panel → remove battery box, replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-self-check. AC-powered models need no battery replacement; periodically check adapter and grounding.

Typical needs: experience-sensitive users, private stalls, no mis-flush; integrated decor. Advantages: triangulation precisely determines stall occupancy, flush only after person leaves; concealed panel unified with office tidy style; remote parameter setting personalizes flush delay by floor usage frequency.

Typical needs: 24-hour operation, extremely short maintenance windows, high-frequency use cannot be paralyzed. Advantages: triangulation validated through three years mass production at high-speed stations and airports; works at 5℃~50℃, full-condition adaptability; military-grade EMC stable beside escalators and security equipment; low water hammer valve body 1 million cycle life withstands high frequency.

Typical needs: active users easily cause brief obstructions, old school-building renovation hard to wire. Advantages: 2-second false-trigger rejection window filters brief obstructions from running students; battery solution wiring-free suits old school-building renovation; burst pressure 2.5MPa, rugged; self-cleaning anti-clogging reduces failure rate, convenient centralized maintenance during holidays.

### 6.7 Trench-type Public Toilet Renovation

Typical needs: a row of pans flushed centrally, old toilets renovatable but unwilling to heavily re-pipe, some scenarios without power. Advantages: trench-type sensor water saver (e.g., GBL-7000 series) uses triangulation to precisely determine pan occupancy; optional hydro-power energy-storage version wiring-free and battery-free; low water hammer valve body fits old pipeline low-pressure environments.
