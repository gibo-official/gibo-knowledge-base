## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Urinal Flusher is installed on urinals in men's restrooms of public toilets—auto-flushing when a person approaches and a delayed supplementary flush after leaving, never touching any switch throughout. It solves the three most annoying problems of sensor sanitary ware in public restrooms: failure under window-side direct strong light, poor sensitivity in dim light, and equipment that constantly needs maintenance. Shopping malls, office buildings, airports and stations, star-rated hotels, hospitals, and schools—these places see heavy daily foot traffic with high hygiene and reliability demands, and most fear equipment that "fails to flush when it should, wild-flushes when it shouldn't," which becomes an open complaint once it breaks down.

This generation of GIBO Sensor Urinal Flusher divides the sensing solution into three technical routes, flexibly selected by installation scenario: high-end and strong-light environments use 3rd-generation dTOF Laser ranging (940nm VCSEL laser pulse + single-photon receiver + time-to-digital converter, directly measuring photon round-trip flight time, millimeter-level accuracy, material-independent, unfazed by 100K Lux sunlight); those requiring penetration and concealed appearance use Millimeter Wave sensing (can hide behind ceramic or non-panel, no drilling needed, unaffected by strong light, backlight, or mist obstruction); general engineering models use Triangulation Ranging (fixed geometric angle for distance, 30μA ultra-low power, no false sensing in stalls). The three routes share the same Low-power Multi-stable power management, dual-stage flush algorithm, dual-mode strong-light immunity algorithm, and military-grade EMC design. The circuitry uses ultra-low-power management; taking battery-powered models as example, 4 AA batteries last over 12 months, installed in the pre-embedded box requiring essentially no attention.

In summary: GIBO Sensor Urinal Flusher makes public places "flush accurately when they should, no waste when they shouldn't, and no worry for a year after installation." The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Non-contact Auto-flush, Never Touch Switch

When a person approaches, it auto pre-flushes to wet the inner wall; after leaving, it delays a supplementary flush to drain—all via infrared/laser/millimeter wave sensing, no hand pressing. For public restrooms this means zero contact, zero cross-contamination, meeting infection control and daily hygiene requirements.

- Sensing triggers immediately, delayed supplementary flush after leaving, avoiding "person present but no flush"
- Fully no mechanical buttons, no contact surface, no hygiene dead corner
- Dual power compatibility: battery wiring-free / AC adapter permanent power, selected by site condition

### 2.2 Laser or Millimeter Wave Ranging, Millimeter-level Person Recognition

The laser model uses 940nm VCSEL laser pulse with single-photon receiver and time-to-digital converter, directly measuring photon round-trip time; the millimeter wave model emits millimeter wave signal to detect human micro-motion. Neither relies on "seeing reflected light intensity," so dark ceramic urinals, stainless steel panels, and low-reflection materials don't affect accuracy; both work normally under 100K Lux sunlight, bathroom heater strong light, and strobe lights.

### 2.3 Dual-stage Flush, On-demand Water Saving

Flushing is in two stages: when a person enters, first flush about 2 seconds to wet the inner wall; after leaving, delayed second flush about 6 seconds to drain. If the person leaves before the first stage ends, the second stage is not triggered, wasting no water. In high-frequency continuous-use places like malls and stations, a water-saving mode can be engaged: if a user is sensed again within one minute, skip the pre-wash stage and only flush the main stage, saving 40%~60% vs conventional mode. Default from factory is conventional mode, switchable with one remote click.

### 2.4 Remote Parameter Setting, No Panel Removal

Sensing distance, second-stage flush duration, and water-saving mode switching can all be set in front of the urinal with the standard wireless remote, no panel removal or pre-embedded box opening. At limit values the LED flashes 3 times to prompt, understandable at a glance. Concealed glass panel models also feature an LED light-ring touch for manual supplementary flush.

### 2.5 Multiple Protections, No Continuous Flow

Auto valve close on power loss; when voltage drops below 4.8V the LED flashes 5 times to remind battery replacement, below 4.5V directly closes valve to stop working; continuous obstruction over 3 minutes also auto-cuts off. Low-battery reminder is clear, letting facility staff replace in time without sudden breakdown. Combined with solenoid valve self-cleaning anti-clogging and smart anti-overflow power cutoff, leakage risk is layered with fallbacks.

| Protection Item | Action |
|--------|------|
| Power Loss | Auto valve close |
| Low Battery (<4.8V) | LED flashes 5 times to remind |
| Very Low Battery (<4.5V) | Flashes 10 times and auto valve close |
| Continuous Obstruction >3 min | Auto cutoff |
| Overflow / Abnormality | Triple power-cutoff water cutoff, valve-closing success rate 99.5% |

### 2.6 No False Trigger Under Seven Light Types, Stable in Electromagnetic Environments

The dual-mode strong-light immunity algorithm, iterated over 20 years, covers 23 light-source interference patterns: incandescent, fluorescent, halogen, electronic-ballast daylight, bathroom heater, hair dryer on the same outlet—alone or combined—cause no false action. Military-grade EMC design passes ESD level 4 (±15kV), EMI 3V/m, EFT burst level 4, working normally beside mall escalators, variable-frequency air conditioners, and large LED screens.

### 2.7 Three-layer Potting Waterproof + Low Water Hammer Quiet

The sensing window and circuit module are encapsulated with three-layer potting process, connectors reaching above IP65, usable even in high-humidity steam-filled bathroom environments. The solenoid valve uses a low water hammer soft-close structure, still delivering stable flow at 0.2MPa low pressure, with low flush noise and no piping vibration; the self-cleaning anti-clogging valve core uses flow to automatically clean the valve core surface at each open/close, significantly reducing failure rates in complex water quality areas.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Accurate Person Recognition in Strong or Dim Light — Farewell to "No Flush When Should, No Response to Wave"

Traditional infrared basically "strikes" under afternoon window-side sunlight; users wave with no response and finally manually flush or simply don't, bringing restroom odor and complaints. GIBO's laser model judges by timing not reflected light intensity, and the millimeter wave model penetrates non-metallic obstructions unaffected by light, so west-facing windows, dim underground garages, winter thick coats, and dark clothing all perform identically. The most direct benefit for facility staff: no more "urinal won't flush" complaints, no need to adjust decor to avoid strong light.

### Selling Point 2: Four Batteries Last a Year, Fewer Ladder Climbs — Standby Power as Low as 0.2mW

Battery-powered models have standby power just over 0.2mW, with the sensing module itself at 18μA level. Do the math: a mall with 50 urinal positions, if devices needed monthly battery replacement, would require 20-plus labor hours a year just climbing ladders; GIBO's solution replaces once a year, essentially zeroing that labor. For facilities managing dozens of restrooms, the savings add up substantially, and low battery flashes a reminder in advance, no sudden breakdown mid-use.

### Selling Point 3: Dual-stage Flush Saves Water by Itself — Saving Both Water and Electricity Bills

Public places fear two kinds of waste: wild flushing when no one is there, and incomplete flushing when someone is. GIBO's pre-wash + main-flush two-stage design ensures cleanliness, while the water-saving mode automatically skips the pre-wash stage during high-frequency periods. At 6L per flush estimate, a single device in water-saving mode saves over 200 tons of water annually; a commercial complex with 50 units saves over 13,000 tons a year. Beyond water bills, low water hammer solenoid valves extend piping life and reduce maintenance trips—all real facility cost savings.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, removing sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid valve.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa ~ 0.7MPa; below 0.05MPa requires a booster pump.
3. Do not point the sensing window directly at sunlight and strong lamps; avoid obstructions larger than 1cm within 120cm in front.
4. Concealed model pre-embedded box installed before tiling; exposed model confirm urinal reserved hole matches.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Always shut off water circuit and power before installation and maintenance.
- ⚠️ Each AC-powered unit must have a separate power switch and reliable grounding.
- ⚠️ Use high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new, do not reverse polarity.
- ⚠️ Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power, to avoid circuit damage.
- ⚠️ If water pressure exceeds 0.7MPa, install a pressure reducing valve to protect the valve body.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Concealed model: cut groove per pre-embedded box size, place box, connect inlet/outlet pipes; exposed model fix body and connect piping.
2. Level and fix.
3. Open water for pressure test confirming no leakage.
4. After tile dries, remove protective cover (concealed model).
5. Connect power (battery box or adapter), install sensing panel/glass panel.
6. After power-on, LED flashes once, solenoid valve opens/closes once (self-check) then enters standby.
7. Use remote to set sensing distance and flush time.
8. Wave in front of sensing window to test.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on, LED flashes once → solenoid valve opens/closes instantly → enters about 1 minute learning mode (LED steady on) → transitions to normal standby. During learning mode, do not continuously block the sensing window.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement (for battery-powered models)

When voltage is below 4.8V, the LED flashes 5 times per sensing to remind battery replacement; below 4.5V it flashes 10 times and auto-closes the valve. To replace: shut water → open panel → remove battery box, replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-self-check. AC adapter powered models need no such step.
