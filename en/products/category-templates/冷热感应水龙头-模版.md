## I. Product Introduction

The Hot & Cold Sensor Faucet is installed on public restroom basins, high-end office wash areas, star-rated hotel rooms, hospital wards, and kitchen sinks—warm water flows when you reach in and stops when you pull away, with no need to touch the switch throughout, and you can adjust the desired water temperature right on the faucet. It solves several hard problems of ordinary single-cold sensor faucets: a blast of ice water in winter, having to turn off the water and twist a mechanical handle to adjust temperature, and high-end venues finding mechanical knobs both dirty and tacky. The core of this product category is combining "non-contact water-out" with "adjustable hot/cold water" into a single faucet—some use a mechanical handle to mix hot/cold water with sensing only controlling valve open/close, others go further with Capacitive Touch Technology for temperature adjustment plus LED digital display, and some integrate an instant-heating module or thermostatic valve inside the faucet so the right temperature is there the moment you reach in.

Shopping malls, office buildings, airports and stations, star-rated hotels, hospitals, and schools—these places require basin faucets to be not just "able to deliver water" but also good experience, clean appearance, and worry-free maintenance. GIBO's hot & cold sensor faucet line covers the complete gradient from mechanical temperature adjustment to capacitive digital display, to instant-heating/thermostatic, with representative models including GBL-6170D Aishang basin sensor faucet, GBL-6193D Xiaoxibao sensor spout, GBL-6176 single-sensor digital-display basin faucet, GBL-6178 water-soap integrated faucet, GBL-9122 instant-heating sensor faucet, etc.

Technically, this subcategory mainly draws on four real capabilities: first, Capacitive Touch Technology (#4), doing wear-free touch temperature adjustment on the faucet surface, fully sealed and waterproof with long life; second, Smart Shower Precise Thermostatic Control Technology (#14), bringing shower-grade ±1.5°C precise control down to the faucet, with no hot/cold fluctuation even with pressure changes; third, Single-window Dual-mode Gesture Recognition Technology (#8), distinguishing instant water-out from long-duration water-out within one sensing window; fourth, the Low-power Multi-stable Agile Sensing (#6) + Dual-mode Strong-light Immunity (#11) + Military-grade EMC (#12) anti-interference combination, ensuring a row of faucets installed side by side with messy lighting won't cross-talk. High-end models also use dTOF Laser (#2) for millimeter-level person recognition, material-independent and unfazed by strong light. For safety, Smart Anti-overflow Power Cutoff (#13) provides the fallback—auto shutoff on overtime, auto valve close on power loss, undervoltage cutoff, no continuous flow flooding the floor.

In summary: the Hot & Cold Sensor Faucet makes the basin "deliver warm water when you reach in, with visible and adjustable temperature, no chaos when installed in rows, and no worry for a year." The three product values below are what we consider most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Hot/Cold Adjustable by Hand, Non-contact Water-out

The hot & cold model integrates dual hot/cold water paths within the faucet body; the mechanical handle or capacitive touch zone handles temperature adjustment, while sensing only controls valve open/close. The Capacitive Touch (#4) solution uses a tempered glass/engineering plastic touch surface on the faucet, detecting capacitance changes from finger contact to raise/lower water temperature—no mechanical wear, fully sealed and waterproof, with a nominal life far exceeding mechanical knobs. The mechanical handle model retains the traditional hot/cold mixing feel, fitting places accustomed to physical operation.

### 2.3 Single-window Dual-mode Gesture, One Window Two Uses

Single-window Dual-mode Gesture Recognition Technology (#8) integrates dual recognition logic within one sensing window: entering the sensing range from far to near is instant on/off water (water on when hand reaches, off when leaving, suitable for quick hand washing); entering from close side (about 2~4cm) is long-duration on/off water (one sensing to open, another to close, suitable for filling containers, rinsing, cleaning the counter). One window covers two interactions, keeping the faucet panel clean without stacking sensing windows.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Pre-installation Check

1. First open water to flush the pipeline, removing sand, stone, and rust to avoid clogging the solenoid valve.
2. Confirm water pressure is 0.05MPa~0.8MPa; below 0.05MPa requires a booster pump.
3. Confirm dual hot/cold inlet pipes are reserved and clearly marked (mandatory for hot/cold models).
4. Instant-heat models confirm on-site AC220V reliable grounded power point (Class II electric shock protection requires grounding).
5. Do not point the sensing window directly at strong light or mirror reflection; avoid obstructions larger than [specified] in front.
6. Use same-brand high-performance alkaline batteries; do not mix old and new.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Always shut off water circuit and power before installation and maintenance; instant-heat models must first cut AC220V and confirm no leakage.
- ⚠️ Each AC-powered unit must have a separate power switch and reliable grounding; never plug/unplug heating tube or NTC probes under power.
- ⚠️ Reversing hot/cold lines on hot/cold models causes inverted temperature adjustment direction; verify cold (blue) / hot (red) markings before connecting.
- Do not plug/unplug sensor module terminals under power.
- Avoid direct strong light and mirror reflection in the installation environment to prevent sunlight false triggering.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Drill the basin per drawing, fix the faucet body, connect dual hot/cold inlet pipes (G1/2 or M22/M24).
2. Open water for pressure test, confirm no leakage at 0.05/1.0MPa.
3. Insert 4×AA alkaline batteries (or connect adapter/AC220V), install sensing and display panel.
4. After power-on, indicator flashes once then enters standby (instant-heat model digital tube shows "88℃" for about 3s then goes dark).
5. Temperature test: rotate handle on mechanical model, capacitive touch +/- on display model, observe LED water temperature change.
6. Reach/wave in front of sensing window, test both instant on/off and long-duration on/off modes.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After inserting batteries or powering on, the faucet enters normal operation; the indicator flashes once on first sensing indicating trigger; the instant-heat model on re-power closes the valve and shows "88℃" on the digital tube for about 3s then goes dark entering standby, and the display lights on first valve opening showing current water temperature. At low voltage the LED flashes quickly. After installation, verify temperature adjustment, display, and both sensing modes one by one.

### 5.5 Battery Replacement (for battery models)

When voltage drops to the protection threshold, the LED flashes quickly per sensing to remind battery replacement and cuts off water-out. To replace: shut water → open panel → remove battery box, replace 4 same-brand new alkaline batteries → reinstall and re-self-check. Instant-heat/AC-powered models have no battery; for long-term disuse, cut power and water.
