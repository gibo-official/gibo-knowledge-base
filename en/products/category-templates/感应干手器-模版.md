## I. Product Introduction

The Sensor Hand Dryer is installed beside wash basins or at entrances/exits of public restrooms—when hands are placed under the air outlet it auto-blows, and stops when hands are withdrawn, never touching buttons or pulling paper towels throughout, solving the three troubles behind the small matter of "drying hands" in public spaces: overflowing trash bins, cross-infection, and machines that fail to blow when they should or blow wildly when they shouldn't. It does one thing—"blowing wet hands dry"—but in places like malls, office buildings, airports and stations, star-rated hotels, hospitals, schools, and food factories, the requirements for hygiene grade, reliability, noise, and usage frequency differ greatly, and most fear equipment failing under high traffic. GIBO's Sensor Hand Dryer is built on a self-developed non-contact sensing control platform—"blow on reach" relies on infrared/laser dual sensing, recognizing hands the moment they enter the sensing window and instantly cutting air when withdrawn; high-speed models use a Jet air blade to concentrate airflow into a high-pressure stream that scrapes water droplets off the hand surface in an instant, while conventional models use large-volume circumferential blow, both achieving "air on reach, quiet on release." The circuitry continues GIBO's twenty years of low-power Multi-stable and Agile algorithms for sensor sanitary ware, with negligible whole-unit standby power and sensing module standby suppressed to milliwatt level; the body features IPX4 splash-proof, auto power cutoff on overheat/overload, and 60-second auto-stop on continuous operation among multiple protections, stable even in steam-filled wash areas. In summary: the Sensor Hand Dryer turns "drying hands" in public spaces into a few seconds on reach—more hygienic, less paper, no attention needed after installation. The three product values below are most worth highlighting.

## II. Features

### 2.1 Dual-mode Sensing, Air on Hand Arrival

The sensing window adopts an infrared reflection + laser Time-of-Flight dual-beam solution; when a hand enters the sensing zone, the laser directly measures photon round-trip time while infrared does environmental baseline calibration, and the two signals cross-confirm before blowing. Dark cuffs, mirror reflection, and oblique sunlight don't affect judgment; the moment the hand is withdrawn, air cuts instantly, no "air keeps blowing after hand leaves."

| Parameter | Representative Value |
|--------|------|
| Sensing Principle | Infrared reflection + dTOF Laser dual-mode |
| Sensing Distance (adjustable) | 12~30cm |
| Air Response Time | ≤0.3s |
| Air Cut Delay | ≤1s (after release) |

### 2.2 High-speed Air Blade, Dry in Seconds

High-speed models use a Jet air blade to concentrate motor airflow into a flat high-pressure stream that "scrapes" water droplets off the back of the hand and between fingers rather than "blowing them apart," compressing dry time to around 10 seconds; conventional models use large-volume circumferential blow covering the whole palm, suitable for budget-sensitive bulk projects. Air volume and heating are independently switchable—heating on in winter for comfort, off in summer for power saving.

| Parameter | Representative Value |
|--------|------|
| Air Speed (Jet model) | 80~100 m/s |
| Dry Time (Jet model) | 8~15s |
| Heating Gear | Independent switch (cold/warm air) |
| Air Volume Gear | Strong/weak two adjustable |

### 2.3 Low-noise Air Duct, Quiet in Public Places

The volute air duct is streamlined and noise-treated, the motor uses a low-vibration brushless solution, with noise controlled within 70 dB(A) at 1 meter. In mall atriums, office corridors, and hospital wards—places sensitive to noise—users blowing hands won't disturb those nearby, nor clash with background music or announcements.

### 2.4 Air Purification, Clean Air Out

A removable filter is built into the air inlet, intercepting hair, dust, and droplet particles; a water-collecting trough + drip tray catch water droplets carried by airflow, avoiding wet countertops. Both filter and drip tray can be removed by hand for cleaning—no tools, no water cutoff—cleaning once or twice a week by janitorial staff suffices.

### 2.5 Multiple Protections, No Continuous Run, No Overheating

The body is IPX4 splash-proof, water splashing on the shell won't enter; the motor auto-stops after 60 seconds continuous operation (resumes on next reach), preventing children from inserting hands to keep blowing; overheat and overload protection cut power directly on abnormal temperature or current, avoiding burnout. These protections let the device be installed in unattended public restrooms with confidence.

| Protection Item | Trigger Condition | Action |
|--------|---------|------|
| Continuous Operation Protection | Single run ≥60s | Auto shutdown, reset after release |
| Overheat Protection | Internal temperature exceeds threshold | Power cutoff shutdown, resumes after cooling |
| Overload Protection | Abnormal current | Power cutoff protects motor |
| Splash-proof | Shell splashed with water | IPX4 no water entry |

### 2.6 Strong-light Immunity, No False Trigger or Wild Blowing

The sensing control module continues GIBO's dual-mode strong-light immunity algorithm, recognizing and filtering different light sources such as incandescent, fluorescent, halogen, LED, and bathroom heater, window-side sunlight—no self-blowing from light changes when no one is present, and no failure to recognize hands under backlight windows. Works stably on wash basins beside bay windows and under mirror-front lights.

### 2.7 Install-and-use, No Commissioning

Self-learning of sensing is completed at factory; hang on wall and power on to use, no on-site parameter adjustment. The body hangs via a metal hook with a pre-embedded wall box, directly usable for both old-building renovation and new construction. IoT-optional models can network to count usage frequency and view faults remotely, suitable for centralized operation by facilities managing dozens of restrooms.

## III. Core Selling Points

### Selling Point 1: Blow on Reach, Stop on Withdraw, Hygienic Without Touching

Traditional paper towel drying requires pulling paper and touching the bin, which fills quickly at peak and easily scatters bacteria-laden paper bits. The Sensor Hand Dryer is fully non-contact—air comes when hands are under the outlet and stops the moment they're withdrawn, the user never touching anything throughout. This is most direct for infection control and food factories: one less contact point, one less cross-contamination path, and janitorial staff need not frequently empty bins or replenish paper.

### Selling Point 2: High-speed Air Blade Dry in Seconds, No Queue at Peak

At mall weekends, airport peaks, and school breaks, the worst is a long queue at the basin waiting to dry hands. The Jet high-speed model compresses dry time to around 10 seconds, twice as fast as ordinary hand dryers, serving noticeably more people per outlet per unit time; air volume and heating gears can switch by season and electricity price, pure cold air in summer for more savings. Do the math: a commercial complex with 50 wash basins, replacing paper with hand dryers saves considerable annual paper procurement and disposal costs, plus eliminating bin odor and pipe clogging troubles.

### Selling Point 3: Near-zero Standby Power, No Attention for a Year

GIBO applied its twenty years of low-power Multi-stable and Agile algorithms from sensor sanitary ware to the hand dryer, suppressing whole-unit standby power within 1W, with the sensing module at only milliwatt level—the device "sleeps" most of the time and wakes only when a hand arrives. Combined with 60-second auto-stop and overheat/overload cutoff protections, installation requires essentially no facility attention; the IoT-optional version shows in the cloud which unit needs filter cleaning or has faulted, turning passive emergency repair into active operation. For facilities managing dozens or hundreds of restrooms, this is true peace of mind.

## V. Installation Instructions

### 5.1 Before Installation

1. Confirm power is AC 220V 50Hz, with a separate 10A leakage protector circuit reserved.
2. Select hanging position: flat, load-bearing wall, avoiding direct shower spray and large water accumulation areas.
3. Metal hook center about 1400mm from floor, hook center to main unit mounting hole about 483mm (referencing 33554L).
4. No sustained obstruction within 30cm in front of sensing window, no direct strong lamp or sunlight.

### 5.2 Precautions

- ⚠️ Electrical work must be performed by professional electricians per national regulations, otherwise misinstallation may cause fire.
- ⚠️ Must install leakage protector to prevent electric shock on fault or leakage; use separately a leakage protector rated 10A, voltage AC220V.
- ⚠️ Forbid blocking the air outlet with hands or inserting slender objects into the outlet; forbid unauthorized disassembly/repair/modification.
- ⚠️ During maintenance, forbid splashing water into the product interior; set power switch to OFF, otherwise risk of electric shock or false action.
- Filter never wash with water; if dirty, dust with dry cloth, replace if too dirty.

### 5.3 Installation Steps

1. Fix metal hook to wall with provided screws (center about 1400mm from floor).
2. Confirm hook center to main unit mounting hole about 483mm, level it.
3. Hang the whole unit on the metal hook.
4. Insert fixing screws into main unit mounting holes, tighten.
5. Connect AC220V power (via dedicated leakage protector).
6. Power on, set power switch to ON.
7. Place hand under outlet to confirm air blows; switch air volume/heating to confirm change.
8. When switching, wait for motor to fully stop before switching, to avoid impact.

### 5.4 Power-on Self-check

After power-on with switch ON, place hand under outlet to confirm air blows; then sequentially switch the air volume selector and heater switch, confirming air speed and temperature change accordingly—normal. The sensing window should stay silent with no air when no one is present.

### 5.5 Maintenance

- Wipe the body with a soft dry cloth when dirty; for heavy dirt use a damp cloth with diluted neutral detergent then wipe dry; forbid acid/alkali toilet cleaners, thinners, cleansers, nylon brushes.
- Periodically wipe sensing window with soft cloth, no detergent, otherwise poor or false sensing.
- Clean filter when air volume decreases: remove filter box → take out filter → brush off dust (replace if too dirty) → reinstall; filter never wash with water.
- Clean water-collecting trough and drip tray 1~2 times weekly: pull out trough, pour out accumulated water, wipe drip tray with soft cloth then reinstall; support drip tray by hand when removing screws to prevent dropping.

## VI. Application Scenarios

### 6.1 Shopping Mall / Shopping Center

Weekends and holidays bring dense crowds; Jet high-speed model compresses dry time to around 10 seconds, no long queue at basins; non-contact no paper pulling, bins no longer fill halfway; under atrium bay windows with strong sunlight, dual-mode strong-light immunity algorithm still recognizes hands and doesn't wild-blow.

### 6.2 Office Building / Corporate Headquarters

Experience-sensitive users, blow on reach, stop on withdraw, touching nothing; air volume/heating two gears switchable by season, pure cold air more power-saving in summer; concealed stainless steel panel matches high-end decor, quiet not disturbing neighbors.

### 6.3 Airport / High-speed Railway Station

24-hour operation, short maintenance windows, equipment must be rugged. IPX4 splash-proof + 60-second auto-stop + overheat/overload cutoff multiple protections, stable even in unattended wash areas; IoT-optional version views faults in cloud, turning emergency repair into active operation.

### 6.4 Star-rated Hotel / Club

Low-noise air duct keeps guest floors and corridors quiet; heating gear delivers warm air in winter for better experience; non-contact more clean and elegant, meeting high-star hotel hygiene standards.

### 6.5 Hospital / Elderly Care

Fully non-contact throughout, one less contact point one less cross-contamination path, fitting infection control; unaffected by cuff color or material, works with patient gowns and thick coats; auto power cutoff on overheat/overload, safe without worry.

### 6.6 School / Venue

Students running occasionally block the outlet; 2-second false-trigger rejection window filters transient obstruction without false triggering; hanging wiring-free renovation is simple, suitable for old school wash area upgrades; water-collecting trough and drip tray wiped 1~2 times weekly, light janitorial burden.

### 6.7 Food Factory / Restaurant Back Kitchen

Non-contact drying avoids secondary hand contamination, combined with HEPA/filter purified intake, blowing clean air; forbidding paper towels reduces paper bit scattering, more compliant with food-area hygiene norms.
