## I. Introduction:
The GBL-6712A sensor forced shower adopts single-chip fuzzy control technology and works on the principle of pyroelectric infrared emission and reception. Through single-chip program settings, it controls the solenoid valve to switch water on/off to achieve flushing. The sensor forced shower consists of three parts: the main controller, the curtain-type infrared sensor and the solenoid valve. When a person enters the detection range of the infrared sensor, the sensor transmits the detected signal to the main controller; the single-chip inside the main controller processes it according to the preset program and then issues a command to output a control signal to control the solenoid valve for flushing. If no one enters the detection range, the entire system will "stand still" and never flush. This realizes precise management of "unattended, automatic management — flush when someone is present, stop when nobody is present", thoroughly solving the waste of 24-hour uninterrupted flushing in public places. It is convenient, hygienic and water-saving, with a water saving rate of 60%-90%.

Widely used in public bathrooms and swimming pools of schools, factories, institutions, hospitals, military units, stations, etc.

## 2. Main Features:
• Automatic sensing, intelligent management:
Fully automatic microcomputer intelligent control, precise management — flush more when more people, flush less when fewer people, no flush when nobody.

The adjustable-angle omni-directional sensor has a maximum distance of 6 meters, 30 degrees vertically and 110 degrees horizontally, and can rotate forward/backward/left/right to cover your optimal sensing area.

• Humanized design, hygienic and practical:
Each time after a power outage and re-power-on, the self-check function is immediately activated and forces one flush to ensure environmental hygiene.

## 3. Curtain-type Infrared Detector:
3
![Shower User Manual](../../../assets/images/ins/6712强制淋浴器说明书-ins-05.png)

This controller adopts curtain-type infrared detection with a wide horizontal detection angle and a narrow vertical detection angle. Installed on the ceiling or both sides corresponding to the passage, with the detection window aimed at the passage, it can accurately detect passing personnel and avoid false triggers. The detector is connected to the controller via a three-core sheathed cable; the wires inside the sheath are red, black and blue, connected respectively to the red, black and blue terminals marked on the controller terminal block.

## 4. Infrared Detector Installation Diagram:

Precautions:
1 The distance between the shower and the detection area shall not exceed 3 meters;
2 To ensure the best effect, it is recommended to install the probe on both sides of the passage at a height of not less than 2.4 meters;
3 Set a U-pipe at the shower head water inlet to ensure the shower head water inlet pipe is horizontally higher than the solenoid valve outlet pipe, so as to ensure the fastest water output.

## III. Solenoid Valve:
The solenoid valve equipped with the controller is a clog-resistant, maintenance-free universal DC-12V pipeline solenoid valve. To ensure the service life of the solenoid valve, please dredge the pipeline before connecting the solenoid valve to ensure there are no impurities in the pipeline. After the solenoid valve is installed, perform pipeline pressure holding to ensure there is no leakage affecting normal use.

## VI. Precautions:
1 The detector should be installed away from outdoors, places with pets, direct sunlight, and under rotating objects, to avoid obstruction. The horizontal detection angle of the detector should be adjusted appropriately to prevent detection blind spots; adjust the probe direction if necessary.
2 Do not touch the detector's detection window surface. If you need to clean the detector mirror, disconnect the power and wipe gently with a soft cloth dipped in a little alcohol.
3 The installation surface should be solid and free of vibration.

## VII. Friendly Tips:
The controller will enter a self-check state when powered on for the first time; the self-check time is about 1 minute.

Carefully check whether the wiring is correct (incorrect wiring may cause equipment damage); turn on the power switch and the power indicator lights up. At this time, regardless of whether the infrared detector detects someone, the indicator will light up and perform one cycle of water output for 60S (the purpose of this step is: system self-check). After the cycle ends and the indicator goes out, the system enters normal operation; the water injection light turns on only when a person enters the infrared detection range. Press the manual button; regardless of the system state, the solenoid valve will open immediately and start water injection; when the water injection time ends, the solenoid valve closes and stops water injection, indicating the system is working normally.
