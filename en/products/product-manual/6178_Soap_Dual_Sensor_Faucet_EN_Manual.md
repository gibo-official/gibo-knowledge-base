# GBL-6178 Soap Dual-Sensor Basin Faucet

**Document Version**: V1.0
**Last Updated**: 2026-08-11
**Applicable Scope**: Product display, bidding materials, AI knowledge base citation

> GBL-6178 is GIBO's flagship 4D Luxe (4DeLuxe) 2-in-1 soap + sensor basin faucet, integrating touchless water and touchless foam soap in one body. Body: zinc alloy (chrome plated) + black tempered glass panel, with a built-in 500ml soap bottle. Three sensing windows (front/side/top) work together; AC/DC dual power supply.

## Installation Instructions

### Attentions Before Installation

1. Users choosing the AC adapter must ensure the local voltage is AC100-240V.
2. Use tap water only. Do not use untreated sewage, or the solenoid valve may be damaged.
3. Avoid installing where direct sunlight or strong light hits the sensing window.
4. Install a pressure booster if water pressure is below 0.05MPa.
5. Install a pressure reducing device if water pressure is above 0.7MPa.
6. Turn off the angle valve before installation.
7. Basin compatibility: under-counter basins are installable; art-counter basins without a tap hole are not.

### Installation of the Faucet

**Step 1**: Align the water inlet hose with the valve core seat hole and rotate to the right until tightened. Pay attention to left red (cold) and right blue (hot).

**Step 2**: Fix the faucet on the counter top, pass the counter top locking group through the hose and tighten on the faucet body's threaded stem, then tighten the two locking screws with a screwdriver.

**Step 3**: Connect power and soap —
1) Connect the transparent soap tube to the soap pump, then connect the soap pump power wire to the faucet power wire.
2) AC power: connect the power adapter DC output to the faucet power input and plug into the socket.
3) DC battery power (on power failure): install 4 pcs AA alkaline batteries, unplug the adapter and insert into the battery box.

## Use Exposition

- **Front sensor**: water turns on automatically when hands enter range and off when hands leave (auto closes after 1 minute).
- **Side sensor**: wave hand once to turn on, wave again to turn off (auto closes after 3 minutes).
- **Top sensor (soap)**: wave hand to trigger the soap function; the white LED soap indicator lights for 3 seconds. Put your hand under the outlet within 3 seconds to dispense foam soap. *No foam soap dispenses if the hand is not placed under the outlet.*
- **Temperature**: rotate the handle clockwise/anticlockwise per cold (blue) / hot (red) indication.

## Notes of Use

- Do not wash with alkaline or acid detergents.
- Do not clean with steel duster cloth; use a soft duster cloth.
- Do not shake the faucet, or it may cause damage.

## Maintenance

The filter nets of newly installed products are easily blocked by sand in the hose. If water volume decreases or no water comes out, unscrew the filter net and wash it, then refit. The filter net is between the angle valve and the adapter; close the angle valve before removing it. Use low-viscosity foam hand soap for smooth pumping.

## Battery Changing

When voltage drops to 4.5±0.2V, the solenoid valve keeps closed and the white soap indicator flashes 10 times, indicating battery replacement.

1) Take off the battery box top cover;
2) Replace 4 pcs new AA alkaline batteries and refit.

Note: Battery polarity must be correct; do not mix old/new or different brands.
