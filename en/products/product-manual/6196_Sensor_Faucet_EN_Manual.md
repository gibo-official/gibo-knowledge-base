## Installation Guide

Function Indication

Unscrew the screw and pull out the cover

## 1 Battery installation

Take out the battery case
Install 3pcs AAA batteries
(Self-provided)
Put the battery case back

## 2 Faucet installation

Side sensor: Wave sensor, 1st Waving - Watering, 2nd Waving - Turn off. Bottom sensor: Ready sensor, Watering when hand's in the sensor range, Turn off automatic when hands leave.

![Sensor_Faucet 2 Faucet installation](../../../assets/images/ins/6196_EN_SensorFaucet-ins-02.png)

Unscrew mounting nut and metal gasket, Insert the faucet with the hose into the installation hole. Install the metal gasket and the mounting nut under the deck and tighten the screw successively. Tighten the hose and filter valve (optional) successively to the Angle valve.

Turn on the angle valve to check whether the pipeline leaks. Turn on the handle, and the sensors would be ready to turn on/off the water.

![Sensor_Faucet 2 Faucet installation](../../../assets/images/ins/6196_EN_SensorFaucet-ins-03.png)

![Sensor_Faucet 2 Faucet installation](../../../assets/images/ins/6196_EN_SensorFaucet-ins-04.png)

Product Use

1 Side sensor: Wave sensor mode, 1st Waving - Watering, 2nd Waving - Turn off. Three minutes overtime water protection.

2 Bottom sensor: Ready sensor mode, Watering when hand's in the sensor range, Turn off automatic when hands leave. One minutes overtime water protection.

![Sensor_Faucet 2 Faucet installation](../../../assets/images/ins/6196_EN_SensorFaucet-ins-05.png)

When the power is low, waving hand to detect the sensor, the indicator light flashes slowly for 5 times, indicating need to replace the battery; When the power is very low, waving hand to detect the sensor, the indicator light flashes quickly for 10 times, indicating need to replace the battery, and force the water off.

## Applicable Basin

1 This faucet is suitable for installation on concave or flat basin, The distance from basin surface to the aerator should be more than 25cm.

![Sensor_Faucet Applicable Basin](../../../assets/images/ins/6196_EN_SensorFaucet-ins-09.png)

1 Do not use rough articles to scrub sensor window, such as: coarse linen, brush, steel wire ball. Do not use water to douse it or dripping wet cloth to wipe the product, so as not to cause sensor failure or product damage.
2 Do not use strong acid and alkali detergents for cleaning.

3 When the faucet is not in use foralong time, please turn off the handle.
4 Please ensure that the water pressure of the water supply is in the range of 0.05-0.8MPa, otherwise dripping or leakage may occur.

FAQ

1 Leaking after installation

1.1 Please confirm whether the batteries are installed correctly.

1.2 Please confirm whether the water pressure is in the range of 0.05-0.8MPa.
No water while hand's in the sensor range

2.1 Please confirm whether the faucet's handle and inlet angle valve have been turned on, and whether the water is turn off.

2.2 Please confirm whether the surface of the side and bottom sensors windows are covered with water droplets or fog, and wipe it clean.
2.3 Please confirm that there should be no obstacles within 30cm of the side and bottom sensors windows.

2.4 Please confirm that the indicator light of the side sensor window will flash one time when detecting the sensor. If it flashes continuously or no flash, Please replace the batteries before use.

2.5 Please check whether the inlet filter valve and outlet nozzle are blocked, and clean according to the cleaning for filter valve.
3. Intermittent or nonstop water out

3.1 Please confirm whether the surface of the side and bottom sensors windows are covered with big water droplets or fog, and wipe it clean.
3.2 Please confirm that there should be no obstacles within 30cm of the side and bottom sensors windows.

4 The water outlet decreased foralong time
4.1 Please check whether the inlet filter valve and outlet nozzle are blocked, and clean according to the cleaning for filter valve.
