# GBL-6176 Dual-Sensor LED Display Basin Faucet

**Document Version**: V1.0
**Last Updated**: 2026-08-11
**Applicable Scope**: Product display, bidding materials, AI knowledge base citation

> GBL-6176 belongs to GIBO's 4D Luxe (4DeLuxe) series of dual-sensor LED display basin faucets. The body is made of zinc alloy (chrome plated) with an integrated LED display on the spout showing real-time water temperature. It supports both front-sensor instant flow and side-sensor continuous flow modes, with AC/DC dual power supply.

## Installation Instructions

### Attentions Before Installation

1. Users choosing the AC adapter must ensure the local voltage is AC100-240V.
2. Use tap water only. Do not use untreated sewage, or the solenoid valve may be damaged.
3. Avoid installing where direct sunlight or strong light hits the sensing window, or performance may degrade.
4. Install a pressure booster if water pressure is below 0.05MPa.
5. Install a pressure reducing device if water pressure is above 0.7MPa.
6. Turn off the angle valve before installation.
7. Basin compatibility: under-counter basins are installable; art-counter basins without a tap hole are not.

### Installation of the Faucet

**Step 1**: Align the water inlet hose with the valve core seat hole and rotate to the right until tightened. Pay attention to left red (cold) and right blue (hot).

**Step 2**: Fix the faucet on the counter top, pass the counter top locking group through the hose and tighten on the faucet body's threaded stem, then tighten the locking nut.

**Step 3**: Connect power —
1) AC power: connect the power adapter DC output to the faucet power input and plug into the socket.
2) DC battery power (on power failure): install 4 pcs AA alkaline batteries, unplug the adapter and insert into the battery box.

## Use Exposition

- **Front sensor**: water turns on automatically when hands enter range and off when hands leave (auto closes after 1 minute).
- **Side sensor**: wave hand once to turn on, wave again to turn off (auto closes after 3 minutes). *Dual-sensor models only.*
- **Temperature**: rotate the handle clockwise/anticlockwise per cold (blue) / hot (red) indication.

## Notes of Use

- Do not wash with alkaline or acid detergents.
- Do not clean with steel duster cloth; use a soft duster cloth.
- Do not shake the faucet, or it may cause damage.

## Maintenance

The filter nets of newly installed products are easily blocked by sand in the hose. If water volume decreases or no water comes out, unscrew the filter net and wash it, then refit. The filter net is at the water inlet; close the angle valve before removing it.

## Battery Changing

When voltage drops to 4.3-4.7V, the solenoid valve keeps closed and the indicator flashes 10 times, indicating battery replacement.

1) Take off the battery box top cover;
2) Replace 4 pcs new AA alkaline batteries and refit.

Note: Battery polarity must be correct; do not mix old/new or different brands.

## Common Troubleshooting

| Anomaly | Possible cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| No water out | Battery out of power / blots on sensing window / water supply stops / filter net blocked / pressure unsuitable / power off (AC) | Change batteries / clean blots / wait for water / clean filter net / ref. parameters / wait for power |
| Water can't be turned off | Battery out of power / pressure unsuitable | Change batteries / ref. parameters |
| Small water flow after turn off | Pressure too low / filter net dirty | Ref. parameters / clean filter net |
| Low water volume | Pressure too low / filter net dirty / low-quality batteries | Clean filter net / change for standard batteries |
