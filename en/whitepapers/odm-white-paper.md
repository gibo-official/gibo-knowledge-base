## Chapter 1: Industry Background and Trends

### 1.1 Global Trends in Touch-free Sanitary Ware

Over the past decade, sensor sanitary ware has evolved from a "nice-to-have" feature into a standard requirement for public spaces. This transformation is driven by three key factors:

**Heightened Public Health Awareness**: In the post-pandemic era, public perception and demand for "touch-free" solutions have undergone a fundamental shift. Sensor faucets, sensor soap dispensers, sensor flush valves, and other touch-free products have expanded from exclusive hotel amenities to essential fixtures in hospitals, airports, shopping malls, schools, and all types of public spaces.

**Policy Impetus**: China's ongoing "Toilet Revolution" policy continues to advance. The Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development's *Urban Public Toilet Design Standard* explicitly encourages the adoption of touch-free sanitary ware. GB/T 41863-2022 *Non-contact Water Supply Device — General Technical Conditions for Water Saving Performance* established a unified water-saving performance evaluation standard for the industry.

**Technology Maturity**: Continuous advancements in sensing technology—from early infrared sensing to dTOF laser sensing—have significantly improved product reliability, substantially reduced false trigger rates, and brought costs to reasonable levels.

### 1.2 Sensor Sanitary Ware Market Landscape

China's commercial sensor sanitary ware market maintains an annual growth rate of 15%–20%. The competitive landscape has evolved through three phases:
- **Import Brand Dominance Era (2000s–2010s)**: International brands such as TOTO and Kohler dominated the premium market
- **Domestic Substitution Era (2010s–2020s)**: Domestic brands represented by GIBO achieved technological breakthroughs, replacing imported brands in landmark projects including the Liaoning Aircraft Carrier, Capital Airport, and Shanghai World Expo
- **Technology Export Era (2020s–present)**: Chinese ODM enterprises export products to over 40 countries and regions across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East
