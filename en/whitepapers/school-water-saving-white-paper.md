## 1. Background & Urgency

Water is a fundamental basis for sustainable social development. China's per-capita water resources are only one quarter of the world average, unevenly distributed, and the urban supply–demand gap is widening. Schools, as major water consumers, commonly show "running water" in public restrooms, bathing rooms and kitchens—many users lack conservation awareness and do not close taps, causing substantial invalid waste.

Retrofitting campus restrooms for water saving delivers three benefits:

- **Economic**: directly cuts water and wastewater-treatment bills; most retrofits pay back in 1–3 years;
- **Management**: hardware-enforced saving subtly builds conservation habits;
- **Demonstration**: schools are a microcosm of society; campus saving has strong educational and benchmark value.

This white paper focuses on two pillars—**trench toilet sensor retrofit** and **teaching-building sensor ware replacement**—providing a deployable, measurable and replicable campus water-saving solution.

## 3. GIBO Trench Sensor Water-Saving Retrofit

### 3.1 Working Principle

The GIBO trench sensor water saver (GBL-6710 series) is a high-tech water-saving product developed for trench toilets, based on infrared (pyroelectric) technology—"on when occupied, off when empty":

1. Add a **solenoid valve** to the inlet pipe of the original automatic tank;
2. Install a **curtain-type infrared (pyroelectric) detector** on the ceiling/side wall at the trough entrance;
3. When someone enters → detector sends signal to the **computer controller** → micro-computer drives the solenoid to fill and flush per preset program;
4. **When empty, the whole system "stands by"** and never flushes—unattended, automatic, "flush when occupied, stop when empty".

Micro-computer control **needs no battery**; the program is never lost; after power recovery it auto-flushes once first for hygiene.

### 3.2 Seven Core Features

1. **Auto sensing**: fully automatic micro-computer control—more users more flush, fewer users less flush, no user no flush;
2. **Digital adjustment**: program parameters adjustable on-site in seconds, fit to different tank capacities and frequencies;
3. **High reliability**: micro-computer control, program never lost, design life 8+ years (spec electrical life >500,000 cycles);
4. **Harsh environments**: heat and severe cold resistant, works normally down to minus tens of °C;
5. **Safe use**: controller mounted above 2.4 m, avoiding contact and vandalism;
6. **Easy install**: no embedded wall parts, ~2 hours per unit;
7. **High ROI**: comprehensive saving rate 60%~90%, full investment typically recouped in 1~3 months.

## 6. Rollout Path: Pilot First

To control risk and let data speak, adopt a "pilot first, batch rollout" path:

1. **Site selection**: retrofit 10~20 points in the highest-traffic teaching-building restroom first;
2. **Measure**: meter-read for 2~3 months to verify savings and ROI;
3. **Rollout**: after confirming benefit, deploy campus-wide in batches using weekends and vacations to avoid disrupting classes;
4. **Trench专项**: GBL-6710 needs no embedded wall parts, ~2 hours per unit, whole-campus retrofit doable in vacation; fill/delay set on-site via dial (delay typically 4~5 min); northern campuses get -1℃ freeze protection.
