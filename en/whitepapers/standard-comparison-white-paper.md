## Chapter 1: Standard Background and Significance

### 1.1 Why Compare These Two Standards?

CJ/T 194-2014 *Non-contact Water Supply Device* and GB/T 41863-2022 *Non-contact Water Supply Device — General Technical Conditions for Water Saving Performance* represent two milestones in the standardization of China's sensor sanitary ware industry.

| Dimension | CJ/T 194-2014 | GB/T 41863-2022 |
|-----------|:------------:|:---------------:|
| Standard Level | Urban Construction Industry Standard | National Standard (Recommended) |
| Release Year | 2014 | 2022 |
| Primary Focus | General performance and safety | Water-saving performance + general technical requirements |
| Drafting Unit | Multiple industry institutions | Including GIBO as a drafting unit |
| Status | Still valid | Newer, higher-level complement |

### 1.2 The Significance of Standard Upgrade

The transition from CJ/T 194-2014 to GB/T 41863-2022 marks three shifts in the non-contact water supply device industry:

1. **From industry self-regulation to national-level standardization** — Elevating the standard from industry to national level
2. **From general specification to water-saving focus** — The national standard explicitly centers on water-saving performance
3. **Product manufacturers directly participating in standard-setting** — GIBO participated as a drafting unit, bringing front-line R&D and manufacturing experience into the standard

## Chapter 3: Testing Methodology Comparison

### 3.1 Test Condition Standardization

GB/T 41863-2022 provides more detailed test condition specifications, reducing inter-laboratory result variability:

- **Ambient Conditions**: Temperature 23±5°C, relative humidity 45%–75% (more precise than CJ/T)
- **Water Supply Conditions**: Dynamic pressure 0.3±0.02 MPa (narrower tolerance)
- **Power Supply**: Specified voltage ±2% (more stringent)

### 3.2 New Test Items

GB/T 41863-2022 introduces several test items not present in CJ/T 194-2014:

| New Test Item | Purpose | Significance |
|--------------|---------|-------------|
| Comprehensive Water-Saving Performance | Holistic water usage evaluation | Core standard objective |
| Anti-Interference Performance | EMC immunity validation | Real-world reliability |
| Long-Term Stability | Extended durability testing | Lifecycle quality |

### 3.3 Sampling Plan Refinements

## Chapter 6: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does GB/T 41863-2022 replace CJ/T 194-2014?**
A: No, it does not directly replace it. GB/T 41863-2022 is a national standard focused on water-saving performance, while CJ/T 194-2014 remains the comprehensive industry standard for general technical requirements. Products should comply with both.

**Q: Which standard should ODM buyers reference?**
A: Reference both. CJ/T 194-2014 for comprehensive technical specifications; GB/T 41863-2022 for water-saving performance requirements. For export products, also reference corresponding international standards (EN 15091, ASME A112.18.1, etc.).

**Q: How does GIBO ensure standard compliance for ODM products?**
A: As a GB/T 41863-2022 drafting unit, GIBO's in-house laboratory can independently test 90% of the standard items, with third-party validation through SGS, CQC, and FQII.
