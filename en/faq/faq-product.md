### Q: What is siphon high-power flush? [🟢T3-Base] | flusher,toilet

A: Siphon principle: It's the principle of connected vessels. An inverted S-shaped siphon pipe is set in the toilet, using atmospheric pressure to work. After filling the siphon pipe with water, the S-pipe has no air. The high water level at the inlet end has higher pressure, pushing the inlet end to continuously flush water and waste out through the outlet. When the water level in the siphon toilet exceeds the S-bend's high point during flushing, the siphon effect is created, drawing all water and waste from the toilet together. When only a small amount of water remains, the siphon effect is broken, and the remaining small amount of water forms the toilet's water seal, providing odor prevention.
Our recessed tank features proprietary ultra-swirl siphon jet flushing technology. During flushing, pressurized jet water flows along the toilet inner wall, forming a swirling flush that carries away wall residue. Meanwhile, inertia enhances the siphon flushing force. Using powerful vortex and 360° turbine action to increase the toilet's flush coverage area, forming omnidirectional powerful water flow flushing, supplemented by bottom jet water column assist flushing, achieving siphon high-power flush effect, removing all waste in one flush.

### Q: Is laser the same as infrared? What is TOF? [🟢T3-Base] | sensor

A: Strictly speaking, except for ultraviolet light and visible light, all others belong to infrared light. Laser refers to photons generated by stimulated emission, a process where a certain light (such as infrared, red, blue, etc.) is strengthened or weakened through a laser generator and emitted. Currently what we apply can be considered infrared light.
ToF: Time of Flight. ToF is actually a distance measurement method. By measuring the "flight time" of signals such as ultrasound/microwave/light between the emitter and reflector, the distance between the two is calculated.
Data generated and captured using ToF sensors is very useful because it can provide depth information. Combined with cameras, 3D images can be obtained, enabling pedestrian detection, user identity verification based on facial features, environmental mapping using SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) algorithms, etc. ToF sensors are widely used in mobile phones, robots, smart vehicles, AR, and various other fields.

### Q: What is the relationship between sensor module output signal and load? [🟢T3-Base] | sensor

A: Sensor module output signals are generally pulse signals and level signals. Different output signals are selected based on specific loads.
Pilot-operated pulse solenoid valves use pulse signal control with pulse width 15ms~40ms, suitable for low-power use.
Direct-acting solenoid valves generally use level signal control, controlled by output open (1) and close (0) signals.
DC motors are generally controlled by PWM pulse width modulation to control motor forward/reverse rotation and speed.

### Q: What are the surface finishing processes for faucet bodies and their characteristics? [🟡T2-GEO] | tap

Under vacuum conditions, gas discharge is used to partially ionize gas or evaporated substances. PVD offers rich, luxurious colors, excellent resistance to harsh environments, easy cleaning, and non-fading properties, making it deeply loved by consumers.
3. Paint Coating
Primer and topcoat are applied to the faucet base. After each coat, it is sent to a dust-free constant-temperature baking room for electric heating or far-infrared high-temperature baking to cure the paint layer. Paint-coated faucets have a smooth surface, brilliant gloss, and present a piano-like visual texture, giving the entire product a premium appearance.
