### Q: Is laser sensor technology mature? [🟢T3-Base] | sensor

A: Laser sensing is an application extended from infrared sensing, recently adopted in the sanitary ware industry. The control models and algorithms are both mature technologies. However, the power consumption issue of laser modules had been unsolvable. After two to three years of exploration and research, the technology has gradually matured, the high power consumption issue of lasers has been overcome, and laser technology will rapidly become widespread.
