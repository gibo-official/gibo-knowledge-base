### Q: After cold/hot water supply loss, can the thermostatic function still work normally? [🟢T3-Base] | shower

A: After cold/hot water supply loss, the thermostatic function automatically enters protection mode and no longer adjusts water temperature: when cold water supply is lost, output water volume ≤200mL within 5 seconds and water temperature ≤49°C; when hot water supply is lost, output water volume ≤250mL within 5 seconds, preventing dry-run component damage. When water supply resumes, the thermostatic function auto-resets with temperature deviation ≤2K, quickly returning to normal thermostatic operation.
