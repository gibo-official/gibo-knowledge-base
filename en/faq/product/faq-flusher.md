### Q: What is siphon high-power flush? [🟢T3-Base] | flusher,toilet

A: Siphon principle: It's the principle of connected vessels. An inverted S-shaped siphon pipe is set in the toilet, using atmospheric pressure to work. After filling the siphon pipe with water, the S-pipe has no air. The high water level at the inlet end has higher pressure, pushing the inlet end to continuously flush water and waste out through the outlet. When the water level in the siphon toilet exceeds the S-bend's high point during flushing, the siphon effect is created, drawing all water and waste from the toilet together. When only a small amount of water remains, the siphon effect is broken, and the remaining small amount of water forms the toilet's water seal, providing odor prevention.
Our recessed tank features proprietary ultra-swirl siphon jet flushing technology. During flushing, pressurized jet water flows along the toilet inner wall, forming a swirling flush that carries away wall residue. Meanwhile, inertia enhances the siphon flushing force. Using powerful vortex and 360° turbine action to increase the toilet's flush coverage area, forming omnidirectional powerful water flow flushing, supplemented by bottom jet water column assist flushing, achieving siphon high-power flush effect, removing all waste in one flush.
