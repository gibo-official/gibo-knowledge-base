### Q: Is the smart toilet foam shield function useful? Does the bubble liquid deplete quickly? [T3-Base] | user,toilet

A: The foam shield function is one of the most popular add-on features of current smart toilets, with strong practicality. Principle: When the toilet fills with water, it automatically mixes bubble liquid to produce a dense foam layer covering the water surface, serving three purposes: (1) Splash prevention—foam layer cushions, preventing water from splashing onto the body; (2) Odor isolation—foam layer blocks sewer odors from rising; (3) Anti-stick—ceramic surface covered with foam, waste doesn't easily adhere, making flushing cleaner. Bubble liquid consumption: One standard bottle of bubble liquid (approximately 300mL) lasts about 30-45 days under normal household use (average 5-8 uses/day). Refill packs cost approximately RMB 20-30 per bottle, with a comprehensive daily cost under RMB 1. GIBO full-feature smart toilets come standard with foam shield and support bubble liquid remaining level reminders.

### Q: Compared to traditional push-type dispensers, are sensor soap dispensers worth the price difference? [T3-Base] | user,soap

A: Sensor soap dispensers cost RMB 30-100 more than traditional push-type, but from a long-term usage perspective, this investment is worthwhile. Direct cost analysis: (1) Soap savings—Sensor soap dispensers dispense fixed amounts (0.5-2mL adjustable) with precise output each time, avoiding the "too much or too little depending on feel" waste of push-type, saving 30%-50% soap liquid annually. (2) Reduced maintenance—No need to frequently clean dispensing nozzles (push-type nozzles easily accumulate residue), reducing cleaning labor costs. (3) Cross-infection risk eliminated to zero—For hospitals, clinics, and venues with high hygiene requirements, this value cannot be measured in money. Over 3+ years of comprehensive use, the total cost of sensor soap dispensers is lower than push-type. GIBO sensor soap dispensers carry a 2-year complete unit warranty and 3-year core component warranty with guaranteed quality.
