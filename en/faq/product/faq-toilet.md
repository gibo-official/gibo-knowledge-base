### Q: How to disable/switch the auto lid-opening function? [🔴T1-SEO] | toilet

A: The system defaults to auto lid opening — when the main unit detects a person approaching, the lid automatically opens.
Via remote control: press the stop button once to power off, then press the lid switch function.
To disable auto lid opening, operate as follows:
1) Long-press the stop button on the remote — the main unit enters standby, display turns off.
2) Release the stop button and immediately press the lid/seat open button — the main unit beeps once, disabling auto lid opening.
3) Long-press the stop button on the remote again to turn the main unit display back on.
To enable auto lid opening, repeat the above steps. The main unit beeping twice indicates auto lid-opening function is enabled.
