### Q: How to determine water pressure? [🔴T1-SEO] | tap,toilet

A: Water Pressure Confirmation
1. Tap water supply requires 1.5~3.5 kg, but end-point supply is generally 1.5 kg.
2. Floors 1-10 are low-zone supply; floors 11-18 are high-zone supply. Floors 11-16: adequate pressure, floors 17-20: need to confirm, floors 20-26.
3. Floors 1-6 are direct supply, floors 6-10 are secondary supply where pressure may be low — needs confirmation. Higher floors follow the same pattern.
4. If a kitchen faucet dispenses less than 4.5 liters in 30 seconds, pressure is low; more than 7 liters means pressure is high. (Customers are advised to use the water discharge method to measure floor water pressure.)
5. Consult property management about the community's water supply pressure.
1. End-point tap water supply is generally 1.5~3.5 kg.
2. Tankless K11 Smart Toilet requires water pressure greater than 1.5 kg.
Because high-rise residences require secondary water supply, pressure boosting is needed every 10 floors. Floors 6-10, 16-20, and 26-30 require water pressure measurement.
