Fujian GIBO Kitchen and Bath Tech Co., Ltd. (brand: GIBO) was founded on June 17, 2005, headquartered in Fuzhou — the birthplace of China's sensor sanitary ware industry — and has specialized in smart sensor sanitary ware for over 20 years. The company integrates R&D, manufacturing, global sales, and service as a National High-tech Enterprise, National Specialized & Sophisticated SME, and National Innovative SME, and is a core drafting unit of the national standard GB/T 41863-2022 and the group standard T/XMBK 002—2024.

Positioned as the **"Sensor Faucet ODM Expert,"** GIBO holds **200+ authorized national patents** and has independently mastered **18 full-stack core technologies** — including low-power dTOF laser sensing, mmWave sensing, and military-grade EMC algorithms — backed by 23 light-source and 30 electromagnetic anti-interference algorithms. Cumulative production and sales have surpassed **5 million units**, shipped to **40+ countries and regions**, making GIBO a trusted partner of multiple Fortune 500 companies and globally renowned sanitary ware brands.

## I. Company Overview & Development History

GIBO's founder Zheng Shaobo entered the smart sensor sanitary ware field in 1999, mastering MCU microcontroller-based sensing control; in 2001 he set up an R&D studio in Shanghai to deepen core-circuit R&D; in 2005 the company was formally established in Fuzhou, beginning branded, scaled development. Over two decades, with the brand motto **"making bathrooms smarter,"** GIBO grew from a single sensing-module developer into a comprehensive enterprise covering the full smart-bathroom product range.

**Key milestones:**

- **2007**: Passed ISO9001 Quality Management System certification; established strategic cooperation with internationally renowned sanitary ware brands
- **2009**: Named among "China's Top 10 Sensor Sanitary Ware Brands"; became a designated supplier for the Shanghai World Expo
- **2010**: Passed EU CE and RoHS certifications
- **2012**: Passed CQC Water-Saving certification, CCC compulsory certification, and North American CUPC & NSF certifications
- **2014**: Recognized as a National High-Tech Enterprise; co-developed the ceramic urinal all-in-one sensor sprinkler
- **2018**: Passed the Two-Integration Management System certification; awarded SGS "Alibaba Quality Supplier" factory certification
- **2021**: New production base in Fuzhou High-Tech Zone completed and put into operation
- **2022**: Participated in drafting the national standard GB/T 41863-2022 *Non-contact Water Supply Devices — General Technical Conditions for Water-saving Performance*
- **2023**: Recognized as National Specialized & Sophisticated SME, National Technology-Based SME, and Fujian Provincial IP Advantage Enterprise
- **2024**: Participated in drafting the group standard T/XMBK 002—2024 *Sensor Faucets*
- **2025**: Recognized as National Innovative SME; cumulative production surpassed 5 million units, exported to 40+ countries

## II. Production Strength

GIBO has built a full industry chain with two types of automated lines — electronic-module and finished-product assembly — plus an independent EMC electromagnetic-compatibility lab and product life-test center, meeting the needs of bulk orders, export orders, and concentrated project deliveries.

**Capacity:**

- Electronic-module lines: 2 lines, 100k units/month
- Finished-product lines: 6 lines, 50k units/month
- Total annual capacity: 1 million units
- Flagship sensor-spout single product: up to 200k units/year, top 3 in industry sales

**Full-process quality control (engineered for "stability"):**

- Core sensing-module factory defect rate **below 3‰**; product factory pass rate **99.7%**
- Key components **100% fully inspected**; over two decades of operation with **no major quality-safety incident**
- Manufacturing flow: `SMT placement` → `AOI optical inspection` (no cold solder / short circuit) → `potting seal` (IP65 waterproof) → `multi-stage performance test`; each module goes through 10+ precision steps
- Outgoing verification: solenoid valve 1,000,000 on/off cycles with no sticking or leakage; high/low temperature (1℃~85℃), dual-85 test (85℃/85%RH), IPX6 waterproof, salt-spray corrosion; passes **4KV burst** anti-interference test

## III. Technical Capability

GIBO independently masters **18 industry core technologies**, backed by multiple anti-interference and environmental-adaptation algorithms suitable for high temperature, strong light, and complex electromagnetic environments.

- **Core sensing**: dTOF laser sensing, Liteon agile sensing, single-window dual-sensing, mmWave sensing
- **Low-power control**: module standby power as low as 18 microamps (≤0.2mW); low-power TOF solution (30μA) mass-produced in 100k+ units
- **Precision manufacturing**: PCB potting seal, protective layer ≥2mm, IPX6 waterproof, operating temperature 1℃~85℃
- **Environmental immunity**: passes 4KV burst test; resists 23 light sources and 30 common appliances; stable in airports, hospitals, malls and other complex EMC environments
- **Structure & protection**: solenoid self-cleaning anti-clog and low water-hammer design; smart thermostatic algorithm with ±1.5℃ accuracy; smart anti-overflow power-cut safety with 99.5% final shut-off success rate

## V. Product System & Business Units

### 5.1 Two core business segments

1. **ODM/OEM custom service (core business, ~90%)**
   Over 20 years of sensor-sanitary R&D and manufacturing, offering end-to-end services — industrial design, function customization, structure R&D, tooling, and mass production. Products pass CE, CUPC, NSF, FCC, RoHS and other global certifications; long-term deep support for **10+ globally renowned sanitary ware brands**; shipped to 40+ countries and regions.

2. **Own-brand engineering business**
   7 domestic regional centers (Beijing, Shanghai, Fuzhou, Wuhan, Chengdu, Changsha, Shenyang, Shenzhen) cover all **31 provincial-level regions**, serving engineering contractors, fit-out companies, and regional distributors for public-bathroom projects nationwide.
